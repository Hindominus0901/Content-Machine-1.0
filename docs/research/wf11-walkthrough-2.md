# Persona walkthrough: Anh Tuấn (32, insurance + real-estate agent, part-time sales trainer, TP.HCM, ChatGPT Plus on laptop and phone, posts on FB profile + TikTok)

## Who Tuấn is (this changes how the designs read)
- **He isn't the "45-year-old non-tech coach" these designs were built for.** He is phone-native, already makes TikToks, and clicks through ChatGPT without trouble. What limits him is patience: "trong 10 phút phải thấy cái gì đó" (I need to see something within 10 minutes). The other limit is focus: he has 3 hats and all of them pay.
- **Install differences matter less to him than two other things:**
  1. what he gets in the first 10 minutes;
  2. whether the "one message" lets him keep selling houses this month.
- **Plus plan removes the Free-tier problems.** No upload caps, tasks work, and Projects are available on the web.
- **He has been burned before.** He has tried template packs and dropped them. He also knows insurance trust in VN is low after the 2023 mis-selling scandal, so he wants to know the machine won't make him say something he could get in trouble for.

## DESIGN 1: zero-setup

**Install (Door A, laptop, Chrome).** 3 actions, about 2 min. Smooth.
- Mild doubt at "Create (keep Default memory)": "Default với Project-only khác gì? Chọn sai có sao không?" (What's the difference between Default and Project-only? Does choosing wrong matter?) He keeps Default.
- If he had opened the ChatGPT Mac app from habit, "New project" wouldn't be there. The setup page's "computer browser" line saves him.
- Typing "Bắt đầu" is fine.

**First session.**
- **Min 0–11 (brain dump), the biggest risk.** He talks well because he trains salespeople, and he talks about all three hats. He gets "Got it. Keep going or say 'done'" three times with nothing tangible.
  - Around min 8 he thinks: "Nói nãy giờ mà chưa thấy gì, giống mấy cái form" (I've been talking all this time and seen nothing; it's like those forms). He would type "done" early.
  - **Most likely quit window: min 8–10.**
- **Min 11, the inventory line** ("You know a lot… about 6 months of content… nothing thrown away"). This is the moment he feels understood, and it keeps him going.
- **Min 17–19, the Message Map.** This is the real test of the insight. If the Map picks the sales-trainer angle or "insurance for young parents" and puts real estate under NOT NOW (stored only as names in the Brand Card), he pushes back: "Tháng này anh cần chốt căn hộ, sao bỏ BĐS?" (This month I need to close an apartment, why drop real estate?).
  - "Locks the Map for 90 days (at most 1 rewrite)" scares him if it is worded as "khóa 90 ngày" (locked for 90 days). He would use his one rewrite on day 1.
- **Min 20–24, FILM TODAY (20–40 s).** He likes it, because it is native to TikTok. "Not filming today? Post the caption as a text post" is a good fallback for his FB profile.
- **Min 24–26, the defaults line.** He is unsure about xưng hô: "mình – bạn" or "anh – em" for young-parent clients? He picks one at random.
- **Min 26–29, saving the Brand Card.** "⋯ under the Brand Card → Save to project" is unverified. If he has moved to the phone app to dictate and the option isn't there, he skips it.
- **Min 29–30, "Adds the method file (1 upload)", the second quit point.** He already has his short, the dopamine is spent, and now he has to go back to the email, then the setup page, download, find it in Downloads, and upload. "Thôi mai làm" (I'll do it tomorrow).
  - "To write your Week 1, add my method file" then blocks Week 1.
  - If he does upload it, Week 1 arrives in 2 replies by about min 38.
- **Hidden quality problem.** The Map and the first short are written before the method file (and its LOCALE section) loads. The very first VN script is therefore the one most likely to sound written and Sino-Vietnamese ("do đó, bởi lẽ, vô cùng") instead of Saigon speech ("nha, nè, á"). The first piece is exactly where trust is won or lost.

**First week.**
- "tiếp" (next) is one word, which is ideal for a phone user.
- Each day he gets today's single piece in a copy box. The keyword asset and DM lines go into "Zalo Cloud của tôi", which he already uses.
- He would skip "Pancake/ManyChat reply set once" ("cái này là gì?" (what is this?)) and reply manually.
- **Friday is a strong fit.** The numbers he reports are "keyword comments, DMs, calls/sales, best post", which is exactly how a sales agent already counts.
- The reminders offer ("say 'yes'") is great if the model really can create tasks from a project chat. If not, the copy-box fallback is OK.
- **Weekly from Week 2:** Monday 10-min chat interview, Tuesday film the shorts by reading from the screen, daily "tiếp", Friday numbers. About 60–65 min, realistic.
- There are no daily "new idea" drops, so he isn't flooded with more options. That is anti-dilution done right.

**Summary.**
- **Total Day 0:** about 2 min install + 40 min session + 10 min to film and post = about 52 min. Realistically he stops at 29 and finishes Week 1 the next day.
- **Knows exactly what to say this week:** yes, once the file is in. The pocket Map plus daily "tiếp" covers it.
- **Clients:**
  - The keyword comment → DM → Zalo path fits FB profile and TikTok.
  - Expect 0–3 DMs in Week 1 and a first consult in Weeks 3–6, if the Map handles his multiple hats well.
  - There is no "cash this week" piece, so real-estate income feels neglected.

**Scores:** install 9 · first-session value 7 · weekly doability 8 · "I finally know what to say" 8 · trust in output 6 (the first script lacks the method/locale; the save step is fragile).

## DESIGN 2: guided-project

**Install (Door A).** 4 steps, about 3 min. For a Plus user at a laptop, front-loading the 2 files is actually easier: one flow, no context switch later.
- The VN copy "Thêm tệp (Add files) → chọn cả 2 file vừa tải (nút B)" (Add files → select both files you just downloaded) works.
- On a MacBook with Safari, he hits "A `.md` file opens as text": "Sao nó mở ra một trang chữ?" (Why does it open as a page of text?). The fix "Press Cmd+S" risks saving `.md.txt`. Chrome or Windows is fine.
- The sample Map and script at the top of the setup page ("what you'll have in 40 minutes") genuinely help him. He sees the outcome before he invests any time.

**First session.**
- **Min 3–13, the brain dump.** Same quit risk at min 8–10, a bit lower thanks to the sample outcome he saw.
- **Min 13–14, "line 7: How you'll work…"** Folding the defaults into the check is good; there is one fewer turn.
- **Min 21–23, MY BRAND v1 at ≤15 KB VN. This is the worst moment in any of the three designs.**
  - It is a huge wall of text on his phone, and it contains "Phần dành cho máy (không cần đọc)" (the part for the machine, no need to read) with "Big Domino, B1–B7 and the focus scores". He reads it anyway: "B1–B7 là gì? Sao phức tạp vậy?" (What is B1–B7? Why so complicated?)
  - Then the gate: "Bấm ⋯ → Lưu vào dự án … Xong gõ 'đã lưu', mình đưa kịch bản đầu tiên liền" (Tap ⋯ → Save to project … then type 'saved' and I'll give you the first script right away). If the phone app has no "Save to project", **he types "đã lưu" without saving, just to get the script.** Gating the reward behind a step the machine can't verify teaches people to fake it.
  - **Most likely quit minute: about 22**, or a silent failure that shows up next week as "I can't find your MY BRAND".
- **Min 23–26, FILM TODAY.** Good. He likes the plain check line ("✓ Đã kiểm: … không có câu hứa rủi ro" (Checked: … no risky claims)) as an insurance agent.
- **Min 26–36.** Season, CTA kit and Week 1 film list. Fine.

**First week.**
- "Week 0" with a Saturday setup is mildly confusing ("Tuần 0 là sao?" (what is Week 0?)).
- **Weekly Talk (Tue).** "Phone on a stack of books, camera on… tap the mic on your computer and answer my questions":
  - The 25-minute recording is mostly him looking at a laptop and waiting for replies.
  - "cut it" gives first and last words per clip, so he has to scrub a 25-minute video in CapCut. He skips the filmed version entirely and uses only the re-say lines. The filming instruction is wasted effort.
- **Friday screen.** It needs "~4,200 views · 38 new followers". His FB personal profile has no easy insights, so he guesses.
- **L4 automations.** "new chat **outside** the project → paste" directly contradicts the rule he was drilled on: "Lúc thì bảo luôn mở dự án, giờ bảo ra ngoài?" (First you tell me to always open the project, now you tell me to go outside?)
  - The daily "1 idea, 3 hooks, 1 question" adds more options, which is exactly the dilution he fears. He ignores it and the task auto-pauses.
- **Weekly load:** 75–90 min Lean. Too heavy for an agent with an irregular schedule. He drops the Talk by Week 3.

**Summary.**
- **Total Day 0:** about 36 min plus filming, about 50 min.
- **Knows what to say:** yes for Week 1. By Week 3 the Talk friction makes his material run out.
- **Clients:** same funnel. Less sales-oriented tracking (views and followers rather than DMs), so he sees less proof that it is working.

**Scores:** install 8 · first-session value 6 (save gate + 15 KB wall) · weekly doability 4 · "I finally know what to say" 8 · trust in output 8 (full method from minute 1, plain claims check line).

## DESIGN 3: concierge

**Install.** 4 steps, about 3 min. The helper handoff is irrelevant to him, but harmless.
- The step 4 pass test ("reply begins 'Content Machine ·' + setup-check line") is reassuring: "OK, chạy rồi" (OK, it's working).
- Step 5, "Pick up the phone… the same chat → mic", is the most natural move for him, and the QR code helps.

**First session.**
- **Min 0–11, the brain dump.** Same min 8–10 risk. The 3-line promise "1) Empty your head 2) I pick the ONE thing 3) You film today" sets expectations better than the other two designs, so he is more patient.
- **Min 17–19, Map + "Why this one: {4} people paid you for it…" + NOT NOW (≤7) + reassurance, all on the same screen.** This is the best answer to "sợ loãng" (afraid of being diluted) for a multi-hat seller: he sees why insurance and young families were chosen, based on his own sales. Real estate is still parked, though, with no bridge.
- **Min 20–21, the save.** "on the computer, ••• under the Map → Save to project". But step 5 just moved him to the phone. "No button visible? I'll remember it for now" means he defers, and the Brand Card stays unsaved for days.
- **Min 21–25, FILM TODAY plus a 3-tick "before you film" card.** He likes it.
- **Min 26–27, stop point.** "Hôm nay vậy là đủ. Muốn làm tiếp tuần 1 (8 phút) thì gõ 'tiếp'." (That's enough for today. Type 'tiếp' to continue with Week 1, 8 minutes.) **This is perfect for his impatience.** He feels done. He probably types "tiếp" because it is only 8 minutes. The most likely "quit" here is a planned stop, not an abandonment.

**First week.**
- **Day 2, the 35–40 min record-day block.** Film 2 shorts plus a 15–20 min Weekly Talk in ChatGPT voice mode.
  - He would actually enjoy voice mode; it feels like a podcast.
  - But the AI voice interrupts his pauses and may answer instead of asking. Whether it follows the interview rules and leaves a usable transcript is unverified.
- **Friday "3 insight screenshots".** His FB profile likely isn't in professional mode, so he has no insights. He sends a TikTok screenshot only.
- The phrase "Tiếp theo làm gì?" (what's next?) is long to type. He types "tiếp" and hopes it works.
- **Weekly load:** about 60 min, but concentrated in a 40-min Tuesday block. One busy Tuesday showing apartments means a zero week.

**Summary.**
- **Total Day 0:** 3 + 27 min (or 37 with Week 1) + 10 min filming = about 40–50 min.
- **Knows what to say:**
  - If he stops at 27: yes for today's post, and Week 1 topics but not the words.
  - If he says "tiếp": yes for the whole week.
- **Clients:** same funnel. The pocket card with keyword and phrases on his phone helps him stay consistent.

**Scores:** install 8 · first-session value 8 · weekly doability 6 · "I finally know what to say" 9 · trust in output 7.

## Which one Tuấn would buy

**Zero-setup.** One-word "tiếp", a short Brand Card, the sales-number Friday, a Monday chat interview with no filming or editing, and no daily idea flood. Its weekly loop is the only one he would still be running in Week 4.

He would want it changed in three ways:
1. On a laptop, do the method-file upload during install, as in guided and concierge, not at minute 29.
2. Use concierge's Map screen ("why this one" + NOT NOW + reassurance in one view) and its minute-27 stop point.
3. Use guided's plain "✓ Đã kiểm … không có câu hứa rủi ro" check line.

Guided-project is the one he would abandon: the save gate at minute 22 on Day 0, and the Weekly Talk plus clip cutting in Week 2.

## The 5 most important fixes (merged across designs)

1. **Give a win before minute 10.**
   - After the first 2–3 minutes of dictation, reply with "3 câu đáng tiền anh vừa nói" (3 lines worth money that you just said), plus one hook he could post today, taken from his own words.
   - Show the inventory line early.
   - Keep guided's "what you'll have in 40 minutes" sample on the setup page and concierge's 3-line promise.
   - All three designs lose impatient buyers at min 8–10 of a dump where nothing comes back.

2. **Handle multiple hats before parking anything.** This is the buyer insight itself.
   - The engine should first look for a bridge buyer across the coach's income streams. For Tuấn: "gia đình trẻ TP.HCM mua căn hộ đầu tiên" (young HCM families buying their first apartment), with insurance protection plus the home, and his sales-training stories as proof.
   - It should include one "cash-now" piece per week for the parked stream.
   - Wording: "thử 4 tuần" (try for 4 weeks, one Season), never "khóa 90 ngày" (locked for 90 days).
   - Show NOT NOW with a reason on the Map screen, as concierge does.

3. **Don't let memory depend on an unverified button, and never gate a reward on it.**
   - Drop guided's "say 'đã lưu' to get your script". It trains people to fake the save.
   - Keep the Brand Card at ≤4–5K chars, not 12–15 KB, and with no visible B1–B7 or Big Domino.
   - Verify in the next chat and walk the fallback then.
   - Phase-0 must test "Save to project" on the **ChatGPT phone app**, because all three designs push dictation onto the phone. Use Plus project chat-memory and the Zalo Cloud copy as backups.

4. **Make the first script sound like him and keep it legally safe.**
   - Put the VN register rules (spoken particles, Southern/Northern dialect choice, xưng hô) and an insurance/real-estate claims guard into the instruction/START block itself. The guard must block "cam kết lợi nhuận" (guaranteed returns), "tốt nhất" (the best) and unfair comparisons.
   - In zero-setup the first piece is written before the method file loads. That is backwards.
   - On Door A, upload the file during install.

5. **Cap the weekly loop at ≤60 min, with no editing and no new option streams.**
   - Monday chat interview without a camera (zero-setup); re-say/re-film instead of clip trimming; no daily "new idea" drops.
   - Friday input is spoken sales numbers: comments, DMs, Zalo adds, calls, contracts. Views and followers, or FB insights he can't find, don't work.
   - Accept "tiếp" for every job.
   - Never tell him to go "outside the project" (guided L4). Either the model creates the tasks, or the instruction is rephrased as one copy box with a clear "this one is special" line.
