# Persona walkthrough: Linda, EN edition, Claude Pro on a MacBook

**Assumptions I made as Linda.** I have an iPhone. My main channels are LinkedIn, my newsletter (900 people) and a little Facebook. I have never filmed a short or used a Claude Project.

## Issues that apply to all three designs

- **The core engine works on me.** All three designs use the same steps: brain-dump, inventory, pre-filled check, Message Map, then "OK" and a NOT NOW list. One line from that engine would make me stay: "You know a lot… about 6 months of content… nothing thrown away." Being told what *not* to talk about is what I actually needed.
  - **Condition:** the "why this one" line has to use my own evidence, for example "your 3 paying clients all came for the career move." If the reason is generic, I'll argue with it.
  - **My likely conflict:** I do life coaching and career transition. The machine will park the life topics. I accept that only if it tells me life topics come back in as the "who am I after corporate" angle. Only zero-setup says it bridges topics by default.
- **The comment-keyword call to action is my biggest trust problem.** All three end posts with something like "comment EXIT". To my LinkedIn peers that looks like an Instagram marketer, which is exactly the "salesy" I'm afraid of. No design asks me about it on Day 0 or offers a quieter version. On Day 0 I'd delete the line, which breaks the keyword system immediately.
- **My two best assets are treated as side items.** I have 900 newsletter subscribers and 3 past clients who will give testimonials. All three designs give me one email a week, and the client-testimonial step ("Buyer Mirror") is optional or homework. No design asks "do you have an email list?".
- **Voice input on a Mac is unclear.** Zero-setup and guided say "tap the mic in the message box". Whether Claude's Mac app or claude.ai in Safari shows a dictation mic is unverified (it is in the designs' own Phase-0 test lists). I'd probably see only the voice-mode wave. Mac's built-in dictation (press fn or 🌐 twice) works in any text box, but I don't know that. Only concierge sends me to my phone.

## Design 1: zero-setup

**Install (Door A, Claude): about 3–4 minutes.**
- "Projects → + New project → 'Content Machine'": fine.
- "Set project instructions → paste START → Save": fine. Seeing a wall of text pasted is a bit scary, but the Copy button makes it easy.
- "Chat in the project → 'Start'": fine.
- This is the easiest install of the three; I'd feel competent. One small catch: if the EN setup page opens on the ChatGPT tab, I have to find the "Use Claude?" link.

**First session**
- **Minute 0: stuck on the mic.** "tap the mic in the message box (dictation, not the voice-mode wave)". I can't find a mic I recognise. I either tap the wave and it starts talking back to me, or I give up and type. Typing turns a 10-minute talk into about 20 minutes over 4 chunks. I wouldn't quit, but I'd be tired.
- **Minutes 1–17: this part works.** The prompts about what I haven't mentioned yet ("You haven't mentioned…") make me remember what my clients actually said. The inventory is the emotional win. I'd write "fix 3" as a whole paragraph.
- **Minutes 17–20: the Map, then OK.** If the word "Locks the Map for 90 days" reaches me, I hesitate ("what if it's wrong?"). I'd say OK after rereading the why line.
- **FILM TODAY: skipped.** I use "post the caption as a text post" on LinkedIn and delete the "Comment EXIT" line.
- **Minutes 26–29: stuck on the Brand Card save.** The step is "Copy → knowledge + → Add text content → paste". Where is "knowledge"? I'm in a chat. I have to work out that I click the project name, find Project knowledge and its +, find "Add text content", and paste. Then I'm afraid I've lost the chat. That's 3–5 minutes of anxiety.
- **Minutes 29–30: the method file.** "Add the method file" makes me ask why it wasn't part of setup. Getting it means:
  1. Back to the purchase email, then the setup page.
  2. Download the file.
  3. Find it in Downloads.
  4. Back to the project page, then + → Upload.

  That takes 3–4 minutes.
- **Most likely quit: about minute 30.** I'm tired and suddenly doing computer chores. I already have my Map, so I might come back tomorrow, or I might not.
- **Minutes 30–38: Season 1 and Week 1 over 2 replies.** Readable on a laptop. The email to my list is the piece I'd use first.
- **Total: about 50–55 minutes including install** (I type slowly and ramble).

**Week 1 and the weekly loop**
- **Day 1:** "films the 3 remaining shorts back to back". I'd skip this, post text, and maybe film one by day 4.
- **Days 2–6:** typing "next" each morning is easy and I like it.
- **Words I'd skip:** "M1 → inbox (VA or a Pancake/ManyChat reply set once)": what's Pancake? "Buyer Mirror": what's that? If it said "ask your 3 past clients one question", I'd do it on day 1.
- **Friday:** typing "my numbers" is easy.
- **Gap: no simple reminders for Claude Pro.** The Friday upgrade offers ChatGPT tasks and Claude Free calendar links. Claude Pro only gets "Autopilot" in Weeks 2–3: skill zip in Chrome, Notion, a connector and a scheduled task, 10–12 minutes. I don't use Notion, so I'd skip it and end up with no reminders. By week 3 I'd forget to open the project.
- **Monday's 10-minute chat interview is the best weekly design of the three for me.** I love talking about my clients. About 65 minutes a week is doable.

**Do I know exactly what to say and post this week?** Yes.

**Will it bring clients?** In week 1, maybe 2–5 replies and possibly 1 call, but only from the email to my list, and only after I change "comment EXIT" to "reply". Shorts won't bring clients in week 1.

| Install ease | First-session value | Weekly doability | "I finally know what to say" | Trust in the output |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | 8 | 7 | 9 | 7 |

## Design 2: guided-project

**Install: about 5–7 minutes for me.**
- The setup page asks Claude or ChatGPT, then computer or phone. At the top it shows a sample Map and script: "what you'll have in 40 minutes". I love this; it tells me this isn't another template pack, and I trust it more before I start.
- Creating the project and pasting button A: fine.
- **Stuck at "select both files (button B) at once".**
  - Two downloads may trigger an "allow multiple downloads?" prompt.
  - I don't know Cmd-click, so I can't select two files at once.
  - A .md file may open as a page of text in Safari. The Stuck box says "Press Cmd+S", but first I'd assume the file is broken.
  - A less patient person might quit around minute 4. I'd watch the 90-second video and get through.

**First session**
- **Same mic problem.** "tap the small mic, NOT the sound-wave button". By now I've forgotten the setup-page tip that I can talk on my phone.
- Folding my working preferences into line 7 of the check is good: one fewer turn.
- **Minutes 21–23: the riskiest minute of this design.** The save message reads: "Tap ⋯ under this message → Save to project. Then say 'saved' and I'll give you your first script."
  - On Claude, this relies on an unverified "document → ⋯ → Add to project" option. If it exists, it's the easiest save of the three designs.
  - If it doesn't, a technical chore stands between me and my first script, right at my emotional high point. **Most likely quit: minutes 21–25.**
  - The design doesn't say whether "later" still gets me the script.
- **I will read MY BRAND even though it says not to.** It's a 12 KB message with "Big Domino", "B1–B7", "focus scores" and "S-2" in it. My reaction: "so it IS a template system underneath."
- **The trust device I liked most in any design:** the line "✓ Checked: one idea · sounds like you · uses your 2019 story · no risky claims". The "? Needs you: how many weeks did it take Maria?" line is good too.
- **The Weekly Talk announcement worries me.** "answer my questions for 25 minutes while your phone films": filmed for 25 minutes?
- **Calendar links at minutes 34–36:** one tap into Google Calendar, and I'd do it. This is the best reminder timing of the three designs.
- **Total: about 45–50 minutes.**

**Week 1 and the weekly loop**
- **Sunday:** the text post is easy. **Monday:** new chat, "What's next?": easy.
- **Tuesday is where it breaks.** The instruction: "Phone on a stack of books, camera on, start recording. Then tap the mic on your computer and answer my questions."
  - I'd be reading questions on the laptop while the phone films my profile, tapping the mic for each answer.
  - "Cut it" then gives me "first and last words" for each clip. I don't edit video and I don't have CapCut, so I'd use the re-say versions instead, and the 25 minutes of filming would be wasted.
  - I'd drop the camera in week 2 and probably the Talk itself by week 3.
- **Friday:** 3 numbers by voice is easy, and the review screen is clear.
- **75–90 minutes a week** is too much, mostly because of the Talk. I'd skip L5/L6 (Notion, skill zip).

**Do I know exactly what to say this week?** Yes.

**Will it bring clients?** About the same as zero-setup. The Thursday email exists, with the same call-to-action problem.

| Install ease | First-session value | Weekly doability | "I finally know what to say" | Trust in the output |

| 7 | 8 | 5 | 9 | 8 |

## Design 3: concierge

**Install: about 5–8 minutes, including the phone app.**
- The setup page asks only which app I use. 5 pictures, and direct file downloads rather than a zip, so fewer surprises than guided.
- **The best moment in any of the three designs comes after I type "Start":** "Content Machine · Setup check: instructions OK · method files 2/2 · Brand Card: not yet… Ready." Now I know I did it right, and I relax.
- **Stuck at step 5, "Claude app → the project → the same chat → mic".** I've never installed the Claude iPhone app. Downloading it from the App Store and signing in with an email code takes 3–4 minutes that aren't counted in "3 min". The QR code helps. Once I'm in, the phone mic works and my voice problem is solved.
- I don't need the helper handoff, but it's reassuring; I'd forward it to my son if I got stuck.

**First session**
- Talking on the phone feels natural. I ramble happily for 10 minutes and say OK to the Map at about minute 20.
- **Minutes 20–21: stuck on the save.** "on the computer, ••• under the Map → Save to project … Claude: + → add text content, or 'remember this'".
  - I'm on my phone now.
  - "•••" with "Save to project" is ChatGPT wording.
  - "+": which plus?
  - I'd take "I'll remember it for now; save it next time at the computer", then probably never do it.
- **The backup tip I fully understand:** "email it to yourself". The pocket card with 3 phrases would get screenshotted and go on my fridge.
- **Naming worry:** if "Belief Bomb" appears on screen, it sounds aggressive. The 3-tick "before you film" card is helpful.
- **"Stop here, or 'go' for Week 1 scripts (8 min)?"** Thank you. Permission to stop matters to me after 30 minutes.
- **Missing piece:** Week 1 is "3 shorts + 1 text post". My newsletter is my best channel, and Week 1 doesn't use it.
- **Total: about 35–45 minutes.**

**Week 1 and the weekly loop**
- **Day 2:** 2 shorts plus a 15–20-minute Weekly Talk by dictation, audio only. My own sentences come back for me to re-film. Audio-only is right for me.
- **Stuck on day 5:** "3 insight screenshots". Where are my LinkedIn insights? How do I take a screenshot on a Mac? I'd rather type 3 numbers.
- **Mild confusion:** the phrase "Here's my recording", when I didn't record anything.
- **Level 1 reminders on Claude Pro:** "Scheduled → New task" — maybe. I'd rather have a calendar link. I'd skip Autopilot.
- **About 60 minutes a week:** doable.
- **Real risk in week 2:** the Brand Card was never saved, so the output turns generic and I conclude "it stopped working."

**Do I know exactly what to say this week?** Yes. The pocket card reinforces it.

**Will it bring clients?** Least likely in week 1, because there's no email. That improves from week 2.

**Which design I'd buy: concierge.** It told me I'd installed it correctly, it moved the talking to my phone, it let me stop at minute 27, and its weekly talk is audio-only. I'd want three things borrowed from the others:
- From zero-setup: the 10-minute Monday chat interview and reporting numbers by voice or typing.
- From guided-project: the "what you'll have in 40 minutes" preview, the "✓ Checked" line, and calendar links offered on Day 0.

## The 5 most important fixes (merged across designs)

1. **Make voice input work on a Mac in minute 1.** The first message should say: "On a Mac, press fn (🌐) twice and talk, or open this same chat in the Claude app on your phone and tap the mic." The setup page should have a QR code to install the phone app. Phase 0 should check whether Claude's desktop app and web version actually have a dictation mic.

2. **Finish all setup in one sitting, with a visible check and a reliable Brand Card save.**
   - Put all file work into the install: direct downloads served as attachments, ideally one method file (zero-setup's single file plus concierge's setup check).
   - Show the "instructions OK · files 2/2" check. If files fail, run without them for now rather than blocking.
   - Never send the coach back to the setup page mid-session (zero-setup's minute 29).
   - The Brand Card save needs one verified, picture-guided route per app. Do it at the computer, at the end of the session. It must not be mid-flow, on the phone, or a gate before the first script (guided's gate).
   - Add a backup the coach already understands: "email this to yourself".
   - Strip B1–B7, Big Domino and IDs out of the visible card. The next chat's check confirms "Brand Card: saved v1".

3. **A weekly loop a 52-year-old can keep up.**
   - An audio-only chat interview of 10–15 minutes, with no simultaneous filming and no clip trimming.
   - Shorts re-filmed from the coach's own sentences.
   - Numbers typed or spoken, with screenshots optional.
   - One-tap calendar reminders on Day 0 for every plan. Claude Pro currently falls through the gap in zero-setup.

4. **Use what the coach already has.**
   - The defaults line asks: "Do you have an email list? How big? Any past clients who'd vouch for you?"
   - With a list of a few hundred or more, Week 1's lead piece is an email to the list with a "reply to me" call to action.
   - "Ask 3 past clients one question" (the Buyer Mirror, renamed in plain words) becomes a core Week-1 step that unlocks real testimonials.
   - The weekly mix follows the coach's platform: a LinkedIn- and email-first coach gets text first, with filming optional.

5. **A call to action that doesn't feel salesy, and plain words everywhere.**
   - Keep comment keywords as the default (the founder's decision), but add one line on why they aren't pushy ("it gives people something real").
   - Offer a one-word switch to a quiet version: "reply or message me".
   - Replace the coach-facing jargon: Buyer Mirror, Belief Bomb, keyword asset, M1, Pancake/ManyChat, cut kit, Week 0, "lock", and "Here's my recording" when nothing was recorded.
