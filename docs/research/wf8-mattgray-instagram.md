### Design and format specs Matt states (EP 113 / EP 139)
- **Slides:** "Instagram carousels with 8 slides, starting with numbers or 'how to' hooks, and minimal text per slide".
  - The carousels I could check on Threads have 8 and 9 images.

- **Look:** blurred, paper-textured backgrounds; keywords in bold italic; minimal charts; "eighth-grade reading level"; a "light, dark, light, dark" alternating grid.

- **Tools:** Figma, Cosmos, Pinterest, Miro, Slack.
- **Design inspiration:** "old Porsche ads, Japanese design… Rick Rubin meets Patagonia". This comes from a search snippet of the EP 134 description; I couldn't open the page.
- **Team cost:** $10–12K a month (social media manager $3–7K, designer about $3K, editor $3–5K).

## 2) Patterns and templates

**P1. Number + rare or quiet modifier**
- Examples: #1 "8 rare traits I see in every high performer"; #12 "33 habits that (quietly) changed my life forever"; #29 "…these 8 deadly patterns"; Short "5 Laws of The Universe That Are Surprisingly Accurate".
- Template: "[N] [rare/quiet/deadly] [traits/habits/patterns] I see in every [aspirational identity]:"
- Coach version: "7 rare habits I see in every client who hits $20K/month".

**P2. Volume proof, then the list**
- Examples: #1 "I've hired 90+ high performers…"; #16 "…But only one hire changed my life."; #26 "…attract 2.5 million followers over the last 3 years"; #30 "I interviewed 10 founders making $75,000+ per month…".
- Template: "I've [done X, at volume] over [time]. Here are [N] [insights]:" Optional twist: "But only one [thing] changed everything."
- Coach version: "I've run 1,200 discovery calls. 6 sentences predict who buys:"

**P3. Age-anchored regret lessons (a yearly franchise)**
- Examples: #21, #22, #23, #24, and the video "I'm 35. If you're in your 20s or 30s watch this."
- Template: "I just turned [age]. When I was young, I [wasted years on X]. Learn from my mistakes. [N] lessons at [age] I wish I knew at [age − 10]:"
- Coach version: "12 years consulting. 11 lessons I wish I knew in year one:"

**P4. "How to [outcome] ([without pain] / [in a short time])"**
- Examples: #27, #28, #33, #10, #11, #6.
- Template: "How to [desired result] (without [feared cost]):" or "…(in just [N] days):"
- Coach version: "How to raise your prices (without losing your best clients):"

**P5. Borrowed authority + paradox**
- Examples: #9 Rick Rubin, #25 Tony Robbins, #8, and the Shorts about Naval and Tim Stokely.
- Template: "[Famous person], [impressive stat]. Yet [paradox quote]. [N] lessons from [their work] that changed my [life/business]:"
- Coach version: "Drucker never ran a startup. Yet every founder quotes him. 7 ideas I use with clients:"

**P6. Contrarian claim or "secret"**
- Examples: #3, #15, #14, and "95% of founders are doing marketing wrong" (https://x.com/matt_gray_/status/1828889646782394780).
- Template: "Controversial opinion: [inverted belief]." or "[Topic] is simple when you understand this secret: [inversion]. Here's my system:"
- Coach version: "Controversial opinion: more leads won't fix your coaching business."

**P7. Named-system card (the value-slide anatomy)**
- Examples: #17, #18, #19, #20.
- Template: [Named System] / [promise or stat] / 3–4 bullets / [2–3 beat aphorism].
- Coach version: "The Client Retention Loop / Keep 80% past month 3 / • Day-7 win • Day-30 review • Day-60 upsell / Results retain. Hype churns."

**P8. Identity question**
- Examples: #4 "Want to become a Brand Architect?"; Short "Want to Create a $100,000/Year Income Stream?"; "Want to learn how I scaled from 100 followers to 100k in 90 days?"
- Template: "Want to become a [identity label]?"
- Coach version: "Want to become the go-to [niche] coach in your city?"

**P9. Lifestyle proof statement (lifestyle carousels and reels)**
- Examples: "Freedom is knowing the business keeps moving while I'm somewhere like this."; "The Real flex is being able to vanish for 30 days…"; "woke up and decided i wanted to disappear into the mountains for the day".
- Template: "[Freedom outcome] is [specific scene] while [business result keeps happening]."
- Coach version: understate it. "School pickup at 3pm. The client onboarding still ran."

**P10. Trigger-word CTA**
- Examples: "IG", "Brand" (three times), "30".
- Template: "Comment '[ONE WORD tied to the topic]' and I'll send you the [secret link to my / free] [Named Asset: Template, Blueprint, Playbook, Flywheel System] (follow me first so I can DM you)."
- **Caption format:** the hook restated, then "—", then the CTA.
- **Funnel steps:**
  1. DM sends a link to a /discover opt-in page (name, email, phone).
  2. The resource is a Google Doc or Slides file, plus a bonus email series (Content GPS).
  3. Then the workshop or apply page.

## 3) What the Content Machine should copy

1. **A carousel skill with 4 style presets.**
   - White & Paper is the default and carries a CTA.
   - Long-form carries no CTA.
   - Lifestyle is low-text and personal.
   - Black is for launches only; cap it at roughly 1 in 10 posts. That cap is my recommendation, since Matt only says "sparingly".
   - Default length: 8 slides (hook, 6 value slides, CTA), up to 10.
   - One idea per slide, using the P7 anatomy.
   - A number on slide 1, an 8th-grade reading level, and one bold-italic keyword per slide.
2. **A hook generator built on P1–P9.** Include the age-lessons, book-takeaways and "rare traits" franchises as recurring series.
3. **A winners-to-carousel converter.** Pull the top text posts (by saves or bookmarks) every 90 days and turn them into carousels. That matches his "Repurpose high-performing hooks from LinkedIn" and "Waterfalling winners every 90 days".
4. **A trigger-word CTA module.** It outputs four things: the keyword, the asset name, the short caption, and the ManyChat DM copy plus opt-in page bullets.
   - Enforce one keyword per asset.
   - Name assets "[Named System] + Playbook / Template / Blueprint / Checklist / Scorecard / Deck".
   - Include the "follow me first" gate.
   - **For the VN edition:** use ASCII keywords without diacritics, such as "MAU" or "GUIDE", so keyword matching doesn't break. This is my recommendation, not something from Matt's material.
5. **Grid planning:** alternate light and dark posts in the grid.
6. **Stage-based templates:** "which template you've earned". For example, beginners use White & Paper, and Lifestyle and Black come once trust exists. This reading of the checklist headline is my inference.
7. **Metrics to track:** email sign-ups per post, Instagram conversion rate, and revenue per follower.
8. **Caveats.** The 9M views, 80% of revenue and 20% follow-conversion figures are all self-reported. Tone down the lifestyle flexing for early-stage coaches.
9. **Positioning:** Matt released "I built a content machine that runs itself with Claude Code" (EP 167, Jul 2026).
   - His stack: Fathom, Clip Engine, Poppy AI, Claude Code.
   - His lead magnet: "Tasteful AI Content Playbook".
   - His thesis: "AI is making most content worse"; taste is the moat.
   - Our product will be compared to this, so stress voice and taste.
   - https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/i-built-a-content-machine-that-runs-itself-with/id1678152217?i=1000775797398
