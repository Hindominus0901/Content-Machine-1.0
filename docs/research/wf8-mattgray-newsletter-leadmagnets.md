## 2) Patterns and templates

**P1. Subject lines** (6 formulas; casing moved from Title Case in 2022–23 to all lowercase from 2024)
- **(a) Exact-number "how I":** "How I booked 14,479 calls and made $10M (with $0 ad spend)"; "how I get 112,341 followers/month on LinkedIn"; "how I used lead magnets to get 138,427 subscribers".
  - Template: `how I [result] [odd specific number] ([constraint removed])`
- **(b) Regret or confession:** "I ignored this as a founder (it cost me millions)"; "11 Millionaire Skills I Wish I Knew At 21"; "The AI conversation that terrified me".
  - Template: `I ignored [X] (it cost me [loss])`
- **(c) Diagnosis of identity:** "7 signs you've become the bottleneck"; "Are you building a 7-figure prison for yourself?"; "What 'busy' says about a founder".
  - Template: `[N] signs you've become [feared identity]`
- **(d) Steps plus outcome plus time box:** "8 Simple Steps To Grow Your Newsletter To 50,000 Subscribers"; "How To Build a Profitable Personal Brand (In Just 7 Days)"; "How To Plan Your Week For Maximum Impact (In 30 Minutes Or Less)".
- **(e) Contrarian reframe:** "Everyone is exhausted. Here's why it's not burnout."; "Why hustling is the slowest path to fulfillment"; "Attention Is The New Oil".
- **(f) Promo:** "Introducing: [Product]", then "Some Kind Words About [Product]", then "The price of [X] is about to increase".
- **Subtitle / preview line:** "My System For…", "Discover My Proven…", "PLUS: … - 4 Days Only", "[Free Resource Below]".
- **Coach adaptation:** "how I booked 31 clients from 1 LinkedIn post (with $0 ads)"; "5 signs you've become your clients' crutch"; "I ignored my email list for 3 years (it cost me ~$200k)".

**P2. Issue structures** (the 4 most useful)
- **S1, 2022 "letter to my younger self":** reflection → one framework (Ikigai) → a quote → a personal commitment → sign-off. Example: #001.
- **S2, 2023 "story to system to resource"** (examples: the board-meeting issue, the 50k-subscribers issue, the 5-automations issue):
  1. Historical anecdote (Air NZ crash and the 1-in-60 rule; Tim Ferriss rejected 26 times)
  2. Bridge to the lesson
  3. "In today's newsletter, I'm going to share… read all the way to the end to get a copy"
  4. **"Before we dive in…"** promo block (workshop replay, deadline, discount code)
  5. "Now, let's get back to…"
  6. `## #1 - [Question]` sections, each with a quote, a stat and his own example
  7. Resource link (Google Slides copy plus Loom)
  8. Action ask: "hit me up on Twitter with what you came up with (… screenshot… tag me!)"
  9. "Let's win together 🥇 Matt"
  10. **"P.S. Whenever you're ready, there are 4 ways I can help you:"** (YouTube / program / sponsor / follow)
  11. "Gem of the Week", then the sponsor, then a referral bribe: "Share this newsletter with 1 friend and you will get access to this $149 resource for FREE."
- **S3, 2026 listicle diagnosis to offer** ("7 signs…", ~1,050 words):
  1. Bold paradox opener: "the thing that makes you successful eventually becomes the thing that holds you back"
  2. 7 H2 "Sign #n:" headings, each a one-line symptom
  3. Pivot: "None of these are growth problems. They're complexity problems."
  4. Name the proprietary frameworks
  5. Scarcity CTA: "Apply here (only 5 spots left)"
  6. "Let's win together, Matt"
- **S4, 2026 sales letter as an article:**
  1. Mirror the pain: "You know you should be creating content…"
  2. Reframe: "What you really need is architecture."
  3. Output math: 20/20/20/4/4
  4. "This is not for beginners…"
  5. Deadline bonus stack: "until July 1… over $30,000 in value… 11 spots left"
  6. "Much love, Matt"
- **Template (S2, the best default for coaches):** `[Story hook 80–120w] → [lesson bridge] → "Today I'll share my [Named System]… read to the end for the template" → [1 promo block] → [5–7 numbered steps: quote + stat + my example] → [free template link] → [reply/screenshot ask] → [sign-off] → [P.S. 3–4 ways I can help]`

**P3. Lead-magnet naming and promise** (the 2026 shift is toward self-scored diagnostics)
- **Examples:**
  - "Your story isn't broken. The structure is."
  - "Your content has a gap. This finds it." (Content GPS Alignment Audit)
  - "One function is capping your growth." (Personal Brand Team Evaluation)
  - "You have a level-mismatch problem." (Content Mastery Checklist)
  - "The carousel template you're actually eligible to use."
  - Outcome and time versions: "One voice memo becomes 30 days of content."; "Ten minutes to your full diagnosis."

- **Template:** H1 `Your [asset] isn't broken. The [hidden structure] is.` + sub `Find the exact [line/stage/function] where [asset] stops [converting] — and fix it.` + name `The [Topic] [Diagnostic noun]`
