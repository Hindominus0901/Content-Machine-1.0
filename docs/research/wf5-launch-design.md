# Content Machine 1.0: LAUNCH module ("Launch Mode" / "Chế độ Mở bán")

**Scope.** This module turns the always-on domino series into a time-boxed, mostly organic direct-response launch. It produces one Launch Brief, a 7-, 14- or 21-day calendar, every script in EN and VN, a guard layer (scarcity, bait and claims checks) and a scoreboard.

**Labels used throughout:**
- **[DATA]**: platform documentation, law or a dataset.
- **[DATA-2]**: data reported by a secondary or vendor source. Verify before shipping.
- **[PRACTITIONER]**: guru or vendor advice, a case study, or a pattern seen in practice.
- **[INFERENCE]**: design reasoning.

**Worked-example coach** (people and numbers are illustrative; in real use the machine fills them only from the user's proof bank):
- **Coach / offer:** Coach Minh. "Make It Launch", a 6-week cohort with 12 live group calls (2 a week), an AI skill pack and a support group.
- **Price:** **$997 / 9.900.000đ**. The next cohort costs $1,297 / 12.900.000đ, and that increase is real.
- **Cap:** **30 seats**, a real limit because Minh reviews every member's work each week.
- **Fast-action bonus:** a 30-minute 1:1 offer review for the first 10 buyers or until **Mon 19 Oct 23:59**, whichever comes first. Stated value 2.000.000đ, about 20% of the price, so it passes the VN 50% gift cap.
- **Lead magnet:** the "Outline Skill" (EN keyword OUTLINE, VN keyword DÀN Ý). It is step 1 of the 3 promises: Đổ não ra giấy / Trải chữ lên kênh / Nhét tiền vào túi.
- **Dates:** workshop **Fri 16 Oct, 20:00 (GMT+7)**. Cart open from Fri 16 Oct to **Wed 21 Oct 23:59**. Next cohort March 2027.

## 1. Launch types and the 3-question picker

### 1.1 Supported launch types

| Type | Best when | Length (cart window) | Live load | Engine | Real scarcity lever |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Founding-member / seed** ("Mở suất Founding") | New offer, **no client proof yet**, or a micro audience of 30–300 | 7 days (3-day cart) | 0–1 live | Waitlist via DM keyword, then a founding price locked for cohort 1 in exchange for co-creation and feedback [PRACTITIONER: Walker seed, McLaren] | Small cap tied to delivery; founding price ends at the stated close |
| **B. Mồi launch** (lead-magnet / DM-keyword launch; the founder's default) | ≥1 consented proof; warm pool of roughly 300–5,000; few lives possible | 14 days (6-day cart) | 0–1 live | Bait posts and lead magnet → DM → email/Zalo nurture → case series → offer post | Cohort cap with reason, bonus deadline, hard close |
| **C1. Workshop launch** | Proof exists and you can do one 60-minute live | 14 days (6-day cart) | 1 live | Same as B plus one workshop as the opening event (Perfect Webinar structure) | As B, plus a replay that expires (48h) |
| **C2. Challenge launch** (or the VN Zalo mini-class variant) | Proof exists; you can do 5 lives (or 3 recorded nights plus 1 live); you want list growth | 21 days (6-day cart) | 5 lives (or 1) | 5-day challenge in a FB Group or Zalo group, with the Stack on day 5 [PRACTITIONER: Brunson, Duffield-Thomas] | Event dates, diminishing bonus stack, Zalo group cap of 200 [DATA-2] |
| **D. Evergreen mini-launch / rolling cohort** | This exact offer has already converted in a live launch | 5–7 days, repeated monthly or quarterly | 0–1 live | Re-runs the best proven assets, with a cart for the next cohort start | Next-cohort start date; per-person deadlines that are actually enforced |

JV/affiliate launches are excluded from 1.0. They are advanced, and partners become "người chuyển tải" under Art. 15a of the Advertising Law, which carries disclosure duties.

### 1.2 The picker (no more than 3 questions)

1. **"Has at least one person paid for this offer (or a close version) and got a result you can show with written permission?"**
   - No → **A. Founding-member** (no case series; process proof only).
   - Yes → Q2.
2. **"Have you already run a live launch of this exact offer that hit your conversion plan?"**
   - Yes → **D. Evergreen mini** (or re-run B/C for a big push).
   - No → Q3.
3. **"During the launch window, how many times can you go live?"**
   - 0 → **B. Mồi launch** (no-live variant: a pre-recorded 10-minute offer walkthrough replaces the workshop).
   - 1 → **C1. Workshop launch**.
   - 3–5, with 21 days available → **C2. Challenge launch** (VN default: Zalo mini-class variant).

**Automatic math check** (asks no extra questions; uses the Launch Brief inputs):
- `seats_goal = min(revenue_goal ÷ price, real_capacity)`
- `warm_leads_needed = seats_goal ÷ 0.02`. This uses a 2% planning rate (range 1–3%) [PRACTITIONER]. Kajabi's lifetime free-to-paid figure is 4.0% [DATA].
- If there is a live: `attendees_needed = seats_goal ÷ 0.05` (5% is conservative, 10% is strong) and `registrants_needed = attendees ÷ 0.30` (coach webinar show-up runs 25–35%) [PRACTITIONER].
- `warm_pool` = email + Zalo contacts + people who DM'd or commented in the last 90 days.
- **Rule:** if `needed > 3 × warm_pool`, downgrade to A with a smaller seat goal, or add a 4–8-week runway (always-on plus lead magnet) first [INFERENCE].
- **Minh's example:** 20 seats × 9.9tr ≈ 198tr. That needs about 1,000 warm leads, or 400 attendees from about 1,300 registrants.

## 2. Phase model

Retargeting (P6) is a **layer** that runs from P1 to P7 (stories, DMs inside the 24h window, email/Zalo, optional ads), not a single day. Awareness levels follow Schwartz.

| # | Phase (EN / VN) | Job | Awareness (in → out) | Domino stage | Formats | CTA (bait level, see §6.2) | Success metric |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| P0 | Listen / **Dò sóng** | Mine objections and the audience's own words; build curiosity | Problem-aware | Likable | BG teaser, story poll, email/Zalo question | A real question (exempt-style, L1) | Replies; top 5 objections logged |
| P1 | Bait + lead magnet / **Mồi** | Turn readers into conversations and contacts; seed the retargeting pools | Unaware/problem-aware → problem-aware | Admirable → Likable | BG post, demo reel, short video | DM keyword (L1). Comment keyword only on a Page with automation (L3, max 1–2 a week) | Lead conversations; % captured to email/Zalo |
| P2 | Belief shift / **Shift niềm tin** | Break the false beliefs in order: vehicle → internal → external | Problem-aware → solution-aware | Credible (reuses the domino belief chain) | Epiphany Bridge long post, myths carousel, reel | Save, reply with your own story, follow the series | Saves, quality replies, nurture reply rate |
| P3 | Value / **Trao giá trị** | Reciprocity: a quick win using your mechanism | Solution-aware | Likable / Credible | Teach-one-step post, template, mini-class, challenge day 1 | Try it and report back | Saves, shares, exercise submissions |
| P4 | Educate + proof / **Educate – case study** | Show the mechanism works for people like them; invite to the event | Solution → product-aware | Credible | 5-part case series, testimonial, "a week inside", workshop/challenge | DM "WORKSHOP"/"CASE"; follow the series | Series read-through; registrations; show-up |
| P5 | Open cart / **Mở bán (DR)** | Make one clear, complete offer | Product-aware → most aware | Trustable | Live Stack, pinned offer post, FAQ, not-for, email/Zalo | DM "JOIN"/"ĐĂNG KÝ"; link sent by DM/Zalo | Enquiries, sales-page CTR, sales |
| P6 | Retargeting (layer) | Re-reach warm people who haven't acted | Product / most aware | Trustable | Stories, email/Zalo, 1:1 replies, click-to-message ads | "Send message" button; reply | Cost per conversation, CPA, frequency |
| P7 | Urgency + scarcity | Turn "later" into a decision using real constraints | Most aware | Trustable | Counter updates, bonus deadline, close-day BG, last email | Reserve a seat (DM/reply) | Share of sales in the last 48h; bonus-deadline spike |
| P8 | Close / **Đóng** | Close on time and keep trust | Most aware | Trustable | Doors-closed post, waitlist post | DM "WAIT"/"CHỜ" | Closed on time (Y/N); complaints; waitlist size |
| P9 | Post-launch / **Hậu launch** | Onboard, capture proof, recover non-buyers, learn | Buyers + non-buyers | Back to always-on | Onboarding, wins, survey, downsell, lessons post | Survey, downsell, waitlist | Activation, testimonials, refunds, downsell %, survey responses |

**Mapping to the brief stages:** P1 = Mồi/lead magnet, P2 = shift niềm tin, P3 = trao giá trị, P4 = educate, P5 = direct response, P6 = retargeting, P7 = urgency/scarcity, P8–P9 = đóng/hậu launch.

**Mapping to PLF:** P2 = PLC1 Opportunity, P3/P4 = PLC2 Transformation, the D8 "week inside" post = PLC3 Ownership [PRACTITIONER, Walker].

## 3. Day-by-day calendars

### 3.0 Runway (T-4 weeks to T-1), the same for every type

| When | Tasks (coach time on top of the always-on 1–2 h/week) |
|---|---|
| **T-4 wk** | Run the picker and math; fill the Launch Brief; send consent requests to 3–5 past clients (testimonial SOP, §8.5) — 45 min |
| **T-3 wk** | Tilt the always-on series: 1 belief post a week on the launch's Big Domino. Set up the Page/IG mirror and pixel, the automation tool, and the Zalo group or OA — 60 min, VA-able |
| **T-2 wk** | Machine designs the lead magnet; set up the sales page or form; sign off the **scarcity ledger** (§6.1) — 60 min |
| **T-1 wk (prep)** | **Batch session.** The machine outputs the whole asset pack; the coach edits and records reels and the stories script; the VA loads DM macros and schedules posts and emails. Takes 2.5 h (7-day) / 4 h (14-day) / 5 h (21-day), and replaces that week's always-on work |

Column key: "Follow-up" = email, Zalo or Messenger. Ads are optional and always exclude buyers. "Min" = coach minutes / VA minutes. With no VA, the coach does both, so suggest a Page plus Botcake/ManyChat automation to cut DM time.

### 4.3 Long Facebook post

**(a) Belief shift, Epiphany Bridge (P2).** Skeleton:
`False belief I held → what it cost me → the moment (specific person/date) → new belief → proof with consent and context → implication for the reader → cliffhanger to tomorrow`

**EN (internal belief):**
> I used to believe you had to be a top expert to sell a course.
> So for three years I just… prepared. Another certificate. Another course. "When I'm better."
> Then one night Hằng, a real-estate agent I'd coached, messaged me: "I opened my 'read a land title in 30 minutes' class. 12 people signed up."
> She isn't the top agent in her city. She's two steps ahead of her students, and she explains those two steps clearly.
> What I realised: people don't buy the most expert person. They buy the person who understands exactly where they're stuck and shows a clear path.
> (Shared with Hằng's permission. Her result, not a promise.)
> If you're waiting to be "expert enough" to launch, you may be waiting for the wrong thing.
> Tomorrow: why most first-product ideas are wrong from day one, and a 15-minute way to check yours.

**VN:**

| **No client proof** | Forces **Founding-member** framing: "cohort 1, founding price, you help shape it". Uses process proof (own demo, pilot numbers with context). Refuses to write testimonials, "students achieved…" lines or a case series |
| No real reason for the seat cap | Writes **no seat cap**; urgency comes from the deadline and bonus only |
| No hard close, or "might reopen" | Refuses countdown, "last chance" or close lines. Offers a rolling cohort with an honest "next cohort starts [date]" |
| Income or result figure without the context block or substantiation | Refuses to write the number; writes a process story instead. Numbers never go into ads |
| Testimonial without a stored consent date, or ads use not consented | Excludes it from that use |
| Bonus value > 50% of price (VN) | Flags it and lowers the stated value, or removes the value line |
| No follow-up channel (no email and no Zalo) | Refuses a cart-open "DM blast". Adds a capture step to P1 and a public "reply/DM for the link" re-engagement post |
| Personal profile only, but wants retargeting | Requires a Page/IG mirror plus pixel; otherwise drops the ads column |
| Superlatives (nhất / số 1 / duy nhất / tốt nhất / "#1", "best") without legal proof | Strips them |
| Affiliates, students or partners promoting | Adds the disclosure ("#QuảngCáo", "Được tài trợ" / "#ad") and a "I've taken this course" condition |
| **Hard stops (no workaround)** | Fake counters or timers; fabricated or AI-generated testimonials; clone-account seeding; guaranteed income or health outcomes; asking people to post phone numbers publicly; impersonating real people or brands |

## 6. Guardrails

### 6.1 Real vs fake scarcity: the Scarcity Ledger (required before any urgency line is written)

`Constraint type | Real reason | Number | Public update times | What happens after the deadline | Owner`

Example:
- Seats: 30, because of weekly review; updated 12:00 and 20:00 by the VA; after the cap is hit, a waitlist only.
- Bonus: 1:1 review, first 10 or Mon 19/10 23:59; after that the course is still open without the bonus.
- Close: Wed 21/10 23:59; the link is turned off and the next price is 12.900.000đ.

**Checker questions** (all must be "yes"):
1. Is the cap enforced?
2. Does the deadline actually turn off the link or change the price?
3. Will you avoid reopening?
4. Are the numbers updated truthfully?

**Exception:** if a payment gateway or other technical failure blocks buyers, announce the reason publicly and extend once by a stated number of hours.

| Use (real) | Block (fake) |
|---|---|
| Cap tied to delivery (lives, 1:1 time, Zalo group 200) | "Only 3 left" on unlimited supply; a counter that never changes |
| Hard close announced in advance | Routine "reopened by popular demand" |
| Bonus tied to real cost (your time) | Timers that reset or outlive the deadline |
| A price increase you actually make | "Today only" repeated every day |
| Founding price for cohort 1 only | Fabricated "X people viewing" or buyer counts |
| Per-person evergreen deadline that is enforced | "First 99" with no enforcement (as in the founder's example) |

Evidence: time scarcity works best for high-involvement products [DATA, meta-analysis]. The FTC, CMA and VN Law 19/2023 Art. 10 treat false scarcity as deceptive [DATA].

### 6.2 Engagement-bait risk and platform rules

**Bait-risk meter.** The linter scores every post.

| Level | CTA | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | "DM me [KEYWORD]"; a real question asking for advice or experience | Default |
| L2 | Link or form | Send links by DM/Zalo/email because of Page link limits |
| L3 | Comment a keyword | Only on a Page with official private replies; ≤1–2 posts a week; the post must have value on its own |
| L4 (block/rewrite) | Comment thresholds ("đủ 100 comment"), react/tag/share bait, coded spellings ("c.m", "q.t", "chấm" used as a CTA) | Rewrite with an explanation; never fix silently |

Facebook demotes engagement bait and applies stronger demotion to Pages that repeat it [DATA]. Instagram says earnest comment-to-DM lead generation is fine [DATA]. Coded spellings don't hide bait from classifiers [DATA-2], and slang or misspelling in ads risks a fine for unclear wording [DATA].

**Founder's posts, made safe:**

| Founder line | Issue | Rewrite (≤130 chars) |
|---|---|---|
| "Skill edit video tự động với 1 click… không bán, chỉ tặng cho 99 slot c.m đầu tiên trên bài này" | Comment bait + coded spelling + a cap on an unlimited file | `Skill AI edit video 1 click: tặng, không bán. 99 bạn đầu được mình góp ý 1 video đầu tay. Nhắn "EDIT" cho mình` (the cap is review time and is enforced) |
| "Sau 1 đêm… 1 cỗ máy… 50 slot trải nghiệm ai muốn?" | Acceptable if the 50 is real | `Sau 1 đêm, mình xây xong 1 cỗ máy viết content. Cần 50 người dùng thử và góp ý thật. Ai nhận lời, nhắn "THỬ"` (post "đã đủ 50" when full) |
| "Đã xong skill… Chấm q.t mình hướng dẫn" | "Chấm" comment bait + coded "q.t" | `Đã xong skill [X]. Ai cần, nhắn "SKILL" cho mình, mình gửi kèm hướng dẫn cài trong 5 phút` |
| "Mở bán sản phẩm đầu tay… trong vòng 21 ngày? … anh em hữu duyên" | Outcome promise with no conditions; "hữu duyên" is fine as tone once, not as scarcity | `Ra mắt sản phẩm đầu tay trong 21 ngày, nếu bạn dành 1 giờ/ngày. Mình mở 1 lớp nhỏ 30 người. Nhắn "21" để xem lịch` (plus substantiation: how many students actually did it in 21 days) |
| "Đủ 100 comment 'UP', làm series casestudy 571.000.000…" | Threshold bait (L4) + income figure without context | Date-based series + DM "CASE" + context block (§4.3c). If "UP" is kept, use it once, on a Page, as L3: "Comment UP để mình biết bạn muốn đọc phần nào; ít hay nhiều mình vẫn đăng" |

**Platform rules baked into routing:**
- **Private reply:** one message per comment, within 7 days [DATA].
- **Promotional messages:** only inside the 24h window after the user's last message [DATA]. Message tags, including HUMAN_AGENT (7 days), are for non-promotional human support replies, not offers [DATA, verify current wording]. This is why the module adds the "reply YES" step and uses email/Zalo for cart open.
- **Automation:** Pages and IG pro accounts only. Personal profiles and Groups need manual or VA replies [DATA-2].
- **Retargeting audiences:** come from Pages, IG pro accounts, lead forms and the pixel, never from personal profiles [DATA]. Profile-first coaches therefore mirror every post to a Page and run ads from the Page.
- **Links:** Meta One (15 Sep 2026) sells organic link posting for Pages and professional-mode profiles; reports say links in comments also count [DATA / DATA-2]. Deliver links by DM, Zalo or email.
- **BG posts:** ≤130 characters, text only [DATA-2].
- **Ads:** no comment-keyword CTAs, no income screenshots, no unrealistic outcomes [DATA-2].
- **Advantage+ audience:** expands custom audiences unless constrained. Exclude buyers using a custom audience [PRACTITIONER].
- **TikTok:** keyword DM auto-replies are reviewed by TikTok; bait loses For You eligibility. Route viewers through the bio link or a Zalo group [DATA-2].
- **Messenger Marketing Messages:** availability in VN is unverified, so the module doesn't rely on them.

### 6.3 Claims and trust (the anti-"lùa gà" kit)

- **Income:** never promised. Case numbers need:
  - the **context block**: audience size and years spent building it, ad spend, price, buyers, refunds, team;
  - consent;
  - the line "kết quả cá nhân, không phải cam kết" / "individual result, not a promise";
  - at least one **variance case**.
  
  Case numbers never appear in ads. No bank-transfer or luxury screenshots.
- **Results promises** ("trong 21 ngày", "in 6 weeks"): always conditional ("if 1 h/day") and substantiated.
- **Health and fitness coaches:** no cure, treatment or weight guarantees, and no before/after in ads. Anything medical or about supplements is out of scope and goes to licensed review.
- **Guarantee wording:** guarantee the process, not the outcome.
- **Trust signals the machine adds by default:**
  - value-complete posts;
  - "who it's not for";
  - refund terms;
  - visible, honest counters;
  - closing on time;
  - keeping critical comments (answer them; don't delete them);
  - real seeding only: students with disclosure, the coach's own explanatory comments.

| Misleading info | Law 19/2023/QH15, Art. 10 (in force from 1 Jul 2024) | Scarcity ledger; fake counts and timers blocked | DATA |

| Superlatives | Circular 12/2026/TT-BVHTTDL (from 5 Jul 2026): "nhất/duy nhất/tốt nhất/số một" need a licensed survey or an award certificate | Blocked unless proof is attached | DATA |
| Clear wording | Ad wording must be clear, accurate Vietnamese; slang or misspelling that distorts meaning was reportedly fined 5–10M (re-check against Decree 87/2026) | No "c.m/q.t" in any ad; flagged in organic posts | DATA / DATA-2 |

**Guardrail metrics:**
- Number of L3 posts a week (≤2) and L4 posts (0).
- Page reach versus the previous 4 weeks (a demotion signal).
- Hides and unfollows.
- STOP/DỪNG and unsubscribe rate.
- Ledger updates made on time.

**Daily cart tracker columns:** `Date | Reach | DM enquiries | Sales-page visits | Sales (#, revenue) | Seats left (ledger) | Top objection | Tomorrow's change`

### 8.2 Debrief (D+7 for the numbers, D+30 for refunds, activation and testimonials; 30 minutes; the machine fills it from the scoreboard)

1. **Plan vs actual:** seats, revenue, warm pool, and conversion at each step against the launch math.
2. **Biggest leak:** the step furthest below plan, and one fix for it.
3. **What sold:** the post, email or day that produced the most enquiries; the most common objection.
4. **Trust check:**
   - Did we close on time?
   - Were there any complaints?
   - Was any post flagged as bait, or did reach drop?
   - Was any claim made that we couldn't back up?
5. **Proof pipeline:** testimonials scheduled, with consent.
6. **Keep / Kill / Test:** at most 3 of each.
7. **Brand Brain updates:** objections → belief posts; hooks → hook bank; FAQ → offer page; proof → proof bank.
8. **Next:** next launch date and type (from the picker); waitlist size; always-on themes for the next 4 weeks.

**Non-buyer survey (D+7):**

| # | EN | VN |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "What made you consider joining?" | "Điều gì khiến bạn cân nhắc tham gia?" |
| 2 | "What stopped you? (time / price / not sure it fits / timing / other)" | "Điều gì khiến bạn chưa tham gia? (thời gian / học phí / chưa chắc hợp / chưa đúng lúc / khác)" |
| 3 | "What would make the next cohort a yes?" | "Điều gì sẽ khiến bạn tham gia đợt sau?" |

Then a 72-hour downsell (self-study version or payment plan) with its own real deadline.

**Testimonial SOP (weeks 2–4):**
