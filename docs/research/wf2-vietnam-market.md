## 1) The main points

1. **Facebook builds trust, TikTok brings reach, and Zalo closes the sale.**
   - The Facebook personal profile (with Professional Mode) plus Groups is where trust and selling happen.
   - TikTok is the main reach engine.
   - Zalo is where deals close and customers are retained.
   - YouTube gives long-form authority, mostly through podcasts.
   - Threads and Instagram are secondary. LinkedIn is only for B2B and corporate consultants. [V for platform sizes / P for roles]
2. **Email is not the backbone of nurturing in Vietnam; Zalo and Messenger are.** The English edition's email sequences have to become Zalo/Messenger sequences, not just be translated. [P]
3. **Trust is low right now.**
   - The 2025 Kera candy case led to criminal prosecutions of top influencers. [V/K]
   - Only 13% of consumers favour influencer reviews; 54% trust ordinary users more. [V]
   - The slang terms "lùa gà" (luring naive buyers) and "coach dạy làm giàu" (get-rich coach) make hype language actively harmful. [K/P]
   - So the Vietnamese edition should lean on proof and humility, not on income claims.
4. **The law tightened in 2025–2026.**
   - The amended Advertising Law (reported as No. 75/2025/QH15, in force from 1 Jan 2026) adds duties for influencers: verify the product, label ads, and share liability. [K]
   - Decree 147/2024 requires verified accounts. [V]
   - A new Personal Data Protection Law takes effect from 2026. [K]
   - Long-standing bans remain on unproven superlatives ("nhất / duy nhất / số 1") and on health claims. [K]
5. **Naturalness is the main quality bar.** AI-written Vietnamese gives itself away through:
   - written instead of spoken register,
   - inconsistent pronouns,
   - missing particles,
   - English calques ("Đây là lý do tại sao…"),
   - buzzwords ("nâng tầm, bứt phá, hành trình").

   The Vietnamese edition needs its own native example bank and a list of banned phrases. Translating the English edition will not work.
6. **Choosing pronouns (xưng hô) is a strategic decision.** It sets age, hierarchy and closeness, so it belongs in the onboarding "Voice Card".
7. **Part 1/2/3 series are already native to Vietnamese TikTok and livestream culture.** This fits the founder's domino thesis. Part-numbered TikTok series, a weekly livestream and a free workshop make a natural domino chain. [P]
8. **Vietnamese buyers also use AI to research before buying** (ChatGPT 80%, Gemini 68%; 62% use AI always or often before purchases). [V] Claude is officially available in Vietnam. [V] Consider keeping the Vietnamese edition portable to Gemini.

## 3) Vietnamese-native content formats (glossary for the pack)

- **"Chia sẻ" (sharing) posts.** First-person lessons: "Mình chia sẻ thật…", "Tâm sự nghề", "Nhật ký khởi nghiệp", "Hành trình từ 0 đến…". This is the default trust format on Facebook. [K/P]
- **Long Facebook storytelling.**
  - Popularised by Tony Buổi Sáng's anecdote-plus-humour-plus-lesson style.
  - 500–1,500 words with frequent line breaks is normal.
  - The hook must land before the "Xem thêm" (See more) cut, roughly the first 3 lines. [K/P]
- **TikTok "góc nhìn".**
  - An expert take on a trending story ("Góc nhìn của một luật sư / chủ spa / HR về…").
  - "Góc nhìn" is also the name of VnExpress's opinion section, so the word signals reasoned opinion. [K]
  - It blends educational and entertainment content, which makes it ideal for reach.
- **Series marked "Phần 1/2/3".** "Phần 2 ở video sau" and "Comment 'phần 2'" are expected conventions. This is the native carrier of the domino idea. [P]
- **Other formats:**
  - "Một ngày làm ___ của mình" (day in the life)
  - "Sự thật về nghề ___" (industry truths)
  - "Hỏi nhanh đáp gọn" (quick Q&A)
  - "Bóc phốt" (call-outs): high reach but high legal and defamation risk
  - Street interviews ("hỏi người lạ lương bao nhiêu")
  - "#HọcCùngTikTok / #LearnOnTikTok" edu-tainment [K]
- **Livestream.** Selling ("chốt đơn") is mainstream on TikTok Shop and Facebook. For coaches it means Q&A or a hot-seat session plus an offer ("livestream chốt lớp"). [K]
- **Funnel events.**
  - "Workshop/hội thảo 0đ" (free) or tripwire workshops at 99k–499k VND.
  - Then a 1-to-1 "tư vấn" (consultation) call, then closing by bank-transfer QR (VietQR).
  - Seminar-style selling from the stage is a strong cultural pattern in business training. [K/P]
- **Entertainment and relatable hooks with a Vietnamese flavour** [K/P]:
  - "con nhà người ta" (the neighbour's perfect kid)
  - Relatives asking about salary or marriage at Tết
  - The boss messaging at 11pm
  - Customers who "bom hàng" (refuse cash-on-delivery) or "seen" after asking the price
  - "Tuổi thơ 8x 9x" (childhood of people born in the 80s/90s)
  - First paycheck
  - Exam season
  - IYKYK becomes "Ai từng ___ sẽ hiểu" / "Chỉ dân ___ mới hiểu". "POV:" is used as is.

## 4) Vietnamese creators and patterns worth studying

This item is a market, not a single creator, so the 9-point format is condensed per profile. Confidence is noted for each.

**1. Tony Buổi Sáng** (anonymous Facebook author; books such as *Cà phê cùng Tony* and *Trên đường băng*) [K-high]
- **Pattern:** humorous Southern-slang anecdotes with recurring characters, ending in a life or career lesson.
- **Steal:** fuse entertainment and education; a consistent persona.
- **Does not fit:** anonymity; a writer's talent the user may not have.

**2. GS. Phan Văn Trường** (author of the "Một đời…" books on management and negotiation) [K-high]
- **Pattern:** reflective "tôi" posts that answer readers' questions with lived corporate stories.
- **Steal:** a Q&A-driven educational series where a reader's question becomes a story and then a principle.
- **Does not fit:** his authority comes from a 40-year career.

**3. TS. Lê Thẩm Dương** (lecturer and speaker) [K-high]
- **Pattern:** provocative one-liners on love, money and career, plus folk wisdom and humour on stage. Fan pages clip these heavily into TikTok.
- **Steal:** one long talk becomes 10+ short clips; take a strong point of view.
- **Does not fit:** a big stage audience.

**4. TS. Nguyễn Hoàng Khắc Hiếu** (psychology PhD; teaches psychology and soft skills at 25+ universities; YouTube about 153K subscribers [V])
- **Pattern:** lecture clips on emotional intelligence and relationships, with workshops behind them.
- **Steal:** credential plus everyday examples.
- **Does not fit:** academic credentials.

**5. Giang Ơi** (YouTube 1.67M subscribers; tagline "Mình là Giang và mình ghi lại hành trình bước vào cuộc sống người lớn" [V])
- **Pattern:** diary vlogs and podcast in a vulnerable, friendly "mình–bạn" voice; recurring formats.
- **Steal:** relatable daily life as the trust layer (the entertainment pillar).
- **Does not fit:** production polish.

**6. Spiderum** (writing community "Động Nhện" with about 200K members; YouTube 1.19M [V])
- **Pattern:** narrated essay videos with contrarian analysis.
- **Steal:** essay-to-video "góc nhìn" for long form.

**7. Vietcetera / Have A Sip** (HCMC media company with about 80 staff [V]; host Thùy Minh [K])
- **Pattern:** long interviews with founders, clipped into Shorts.
- **Steal:** an interview series for borrowed authority. A coach can interview clients or peers.

**8. Shark Tank Việt Nam personalities** (e.g. Shark Hưng – Phạm Thanh Hưng, Shark Linh – Thái Vân Linh, Shark Bình – Nguyễn Hòa Bình) [K-high on identity, medium on patterns]
- **Pattern:** TV clips plus personal commentary on business news; Shark Linh writes long leadership posts.
- **Lesson:** scandals (e.g. Shark Bình's 2023 personal controversy [K]) erode a personal brand quickly.

**9. Phạm Thành Long** (business and sales trainer, seminar-led) [K-medium; I could not verify his site or channel]
- **Pattern:** "thực chiến" (real-world, hands-on) stage clips, direct sales tone, large events that close sales on site.
- **Does not fit:** most DIY coaches cannot run large events.

**10. Rosie Nguyễn** (author of *Tuổi trẻ đáng giá bao nhiêu*) [K-high]
- **Pattern:** self-development essays for young people.
- **Steal:** a book-like essay series that builds authority.

**11. Ms Hoa (Anh ngữ Ms Hoa)** [K-medium]
- **Pattern:** a teacher's personal brand of free study materials and Facebook groups, grown into an education business.
- **Steal:** a lead-magnet-heavy educational funnel.

**Cautionary cases the Vietnamese edition should reference** (trust context):
- **Kera candy (2025).** Miss Grand 2021 Thùy Tiên was prosecuted on 19 May 2025 for consumer fraud. About 135,000 boxes were sold for about 18B VND, she received about 7B VND, and her film *Chốt đơn!* was pulled. [V] Quang Linh Vlogs and Hằng Du Mục were also arrested. [K]
- **"Mr. Pips" (Phó Đức Nam).** Forex-course fraud, arrested 2023. [K]
- **Other trust shocks:** insurance mis-selling in 2023, which hurt trust in insurance agents [K]; the backlash against "chữa lành" (healing) wellness coaching [K/P]; 2024 controversies over charity fundraising by livestreamers [K].

**Main lessons for the pack:**
- Long-form becomes clips (Lê Thẩm Dương, Vietcetera).
- Q&A-to-story series (Phan Văn Trường).
- Vulnerable daily-life layer (Giang Ơi).
- Analysis takes (Spiderum).
- Lead-magnet groups (Ms Hoa).

**What does not fit a DIY coach:** big seminars, TV fame, anonymous author personas, PR-article buying.

## 5) Copy and tone norms

**Pronouns (xưng hô): put this in onboarding** [K/P]

| Self / audience | Signals | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| **mình – bạn / các bạn** | Friendly peer; neutral to age | Default for TikTok, Threads, IG; Gen Z and millennials |
| **tôi – bạn** | Authority, some distance; can feel cold or arrogant in short videos | Senior experts, long-form, LinkedIn |
| **anh/chị – em** | Older creator to a younger audience; warm hierarchy | Mentors addressing juniors |
| **em – anh chị** | Younger creator showing respect to owners and clients | Service businesses selling to 30+ owners; DMs ("Dạ em chào anh/chị ạ") |
| **mình – cả nhà / mọi người / anh em / các mom / chị em** | Community warmth or specific tribes | Groups, livestreams |
| **tui, tớ – cậu** | Casual Southern / cute Gen Z | Entertainment only |
| **tao – mày** | Avoid | Never |

Rule: one pronoun pair per piece, with no drift. In sales DMs, open with "Dạ" and end with "ạ"; without them, replies read as rude.

**Register**
- AI defaults to written, Sino-Vietnamese wording: "do đó, tuy nhiên, nhằm, bởi lẽ, vô cùng".
- Scripts need spoken wording: "nên, nhưng mà, để, vì, cực kỳ".
- Use short sentences and particles ("nhé / nha / nhỉ / á / nè / đó / ạ / luôn"). [P]

**Regional dialect**
- Northern: nhé, nhỉ, ạ, thế, đấy; cốc, bát.
- Southern: nha, nè, á, hông, dạ, tui; ly, chén.
- Scripts must match the speaker's own accent. [K]

**English code-switching**
- "content, sale, feedback, deadline, KPI, insight, booking" are natural for urban professionals.
- Over-translating ("tiếp thị nội dung") sounds stiff to them, while rural and older audiences prefer pure Vietnamese. [P]

**Slang**
- Gen Z examples: "khum, u là trời, chằm Zn, xu cà na, ét o ét, gét gô, out trình, flex, red flag, đỉnh nóc kịch trần, báo thủ".
- Slang goes stale fast and AI's slang is often years out of date. The default should be light slang supplied by the user. Avoid slang with 35+ business owners. [K/P]

**Emojis and formatting**
- Sales posts use emojis as bullets (👉 ✅ 🔥 📌). Premium personal brands use few.
- Casual posts use native emoticons like ":))", "=)))", "hihi", "huhu"; AI almost never produces these.
- Unicode "bold" fonts (𝐁𝐎𝐋𝐃) are common but break Vietnamese diacritics and hurt readability, so use them sparingly or not at all.
- Hashtags are often written without diacritics (#kinhdoanh, #thuonghieucanhan). [K/P]

**Numbers, dates and names**
- Prices: 1.990.000đ / 1,99tr / 99k. Decimal comma: 2,5. Dates: dd/mm/yyyy. Times: "20h".
- Call people by **given name**: Nguyễn Thị Lan becomes "chị Lan", never "Ms. Nguyen". [K]

**Humility norms**
- "Nổ / chém gió" (bragging) is pejorative, and flaunting wealth now triggers suspicion.
- Specific results stated modestly win over hype. [K/P]

**Calendar the Vietnamese edition needs** [K]
- Tết and Vía Thần Tài (10th day of the first lunar month)
- 8/3, 30/4–1/5
- Mid-year exam season
- Tháng cô hồn (7th lunar month, roughly Aug–Sep): many people avoid launches and big purchases
- 2/9
- 13/10 Ngày Doanh nhân (Entrepreneurs' Day)
- 20/10 Women's Day
- 20/11 Teachers' Day: a major moment for coaches and educators to share students' thanks as proof
- Double-day sales (9.9 through 12.12), Black Friday, year-end "tất niên"

## 6) Trust and selling culture

**Default funnel** [P]:
1. Post with "Comment 'X' / '.' / 'xin' để nhận tài liệu" (comment to get the resource)
2. Auto or manual inbox ("check ib")
3. "Kết bạn Zalo" (add on Zalo)
4. Nurture on Zalo
5. Free workshop or livestream
6. 1-to-1 consultation call or chat
7. Pay by VietQR bank transfer

Common tools: Pancake/Botcake or Fchat for inbox automation, LadiPage for landing pages, payOS/Casso/SePay for automatic transfer reconciliation. [K]

**Social proof norms** [K/P]:
- Screenshots of customer feedback with names blurred, photos of students, crowd photos from offline classes, student-count claims, press/PR articles (often paid), certificates.
- Buyers are now sceptical: 54% trust ordinary users over KOLs; 69% want photo or video evidence; 74% want 50+ reviews; 67% say they can spot fakes from generic text and missing real photos. [V]
- Implication: show real, specific, verifiable proof (named clients with consent, the context, the process). Never fabricated testimonials or "seeding" (planted comments).

**"Giá ib" (price on request by inbox)** annoys buyers. Public prices build trust. [P]

**Relationships, referrals and private groups** carry a lot of weight. Response speed in DMs is expected to be fast. [P]

**Distrust triggers to design against** [K/P]:
- "lùa gà", "dạy làm giàu", MLM vibes
- "chữa lành" pseudo-science
- Flaunting luxury
- Guaranteed income or ROI
- Stock-tip "rooms"

## 7) Legal and ethical constraints (have a lawyer verify every [K] item)

**Advertising Law 16/2012/QH13, Article 8 bans** [K]:
- "nhất / duy nhất / tốt nhất / số một" (and equivalents) without lawful proof
- Direct comparison with competitors
- Misleading content
- Using someone's image or words without consent

**Amended Advertising Law, reported as No. 75/2025/QH15** (passed June 2025, effective 1 Jan 2026) [K]:
- Defines "người chuyển tải sản phẩm quảng cáo" (people who carry ad content), including influencers.
- Influencers must verify the advertiser's and the product's documents and must clearly signal that content is advertising.
- Influencers can share liability for false ads.
- Online video ads must be skippable within 5 seconds.

**Special categories** [K]:
- **Health supplements:** the mandatory line "Thực phẩm này không phải là thuốc và không có tác dụng thay thế thuốc chữa bệnh", plus prior approval of ad content.
- **Medical and health-worker imagery:** no use of health workers' titles or images to promote supplements.
- **Cosmetics:** no treatment claims.
- **Fitness and nutrition coaches:** avoid "giảm X kg trong Y ngày" (lose X kg in Y days) and disease-cure claims.
- **Securities:** advisory needs a licence, so no buy/sell tips and no guaranteed returns.
- **Insurance and real estate:** practice-licence rules apply.
- **Education:**
  - Avoid "cam kết đầu ra 100%" (guaranteed outcomes) unless it is contractually real.
  - Private courses should issue a "giấy chứng nhận hoàn thành" (completion certificate), not a "chứng chỉ" (state certificate), unless licensed. [K-medium]

**Decree 147/2024 (effective 25 Dec 2024):**
- Identity verification is required. [V]
- Only verified accounts may post, comment or livestream; large channels (around 10k+ followers) must give contact details to the authorities. [K, check thresholds]

**Personal Data Protection Law, reported as No. 91/2025/QH15, effective 1 Jan 2026** [K]:
- Lead capture of phone or Zalo numbers needs consent.
- Buying or selling "data khách hàng" (customer lists) is illegal.

**Anti-spam Decree 91/2020** [K]: advertising SMS and calls need consent and fall within time windows.

**E-commerce Law (late 2025)** [K-low, verify number and dates]: livestream selling and affiliate/KOL obligations.

**Defamation and sensitive topics** [K]:
- Fines for false or defamatory posts (Decree 15/2020 as amended); criminal liability in serious cases.
- Avoid politics, sovereignty and maps (Hoàng Sa/Trường Sa, nine-dash-line imagery), and mocking religion or the war.

**Platform ad policies:**
- **Meta** [V]:
  - No asserting personal attributes ("Bạn đang nợ nần?" is banned; "Tìm hiểu về giải pháp tài chính" is fine).
  - No negative self-perception or body-part attacks.
  - Weight-loss and cosmetic ads must target 18+, with no extreme outcome promises.
  - No "guaranteed investment returns", deceptive pricing or unauthorised endorsements.
- **TikTok** has parallel policies, including market-specific approval rules; I did not read the detailed pages. [V that they exist]

**Red-flag phrases and safer rewrites** [P]:

| Red flag | Safer rewrite |
|---|---|
| "Số 1 / tốt nhất / duy nhất" | "Cách mình đã áp dụng cho [N] học viên trong [thời gian]" (only with real evidence) |
| "Cam kết 100% ra đơn" | A verifiable guarantee: "Làm đủ bài tập mà chưa có khách, mình kèm 1-1 thêm 30 ngày" |
| "Thu nhập 100tr/tháng sau 30 ngày" | A named, consented case with context, plus "kết quả tùy mỗi người" (results vary) |
| "Chữa khỏi / điều trị" | Coaching language, with referral to professionals |
| Undisclosed sponsorship | Label it: "Hợp tác quảng cáo" or "#QC" |

## 8) Problems when AI writes Vietnamese, and fixes for the pack

1. **Draft in Vietnamese; never translate from English.** Ship a native example bank (hooks, posts, DMs) because few-shot examples beat instructions. [P]
2. **Banned calques and buzzwords** [P]:
   - "Đây là lý do tại sao…", "Ở cuối ngày…", "Điều đó nói rằng…", "Hãy cùng khám phá…", "Trong thế giới hiện đại ngày nay…", "Bạn có biết rằng…?", "Tóm lại,", "Hy vọng bài viết hữu ích"
   - Overuse of "không chỉ… mà còn…", "một cách + adjective", and passive "được… bởi"
   - "nâng tầm, bứt phá, khai phá tiềm năng, hành trình, chìa khóa, thay đổi cuộc chơi, lan tỏa giá trị"
3. **Fix pronoun drift, missing particles and over-formality** ("Quý khách", "Kính gửi" on social media). Add a final "đọc to" (read-aloud) self-check.
4. **Spelling and diacritics** [K/P]:
   - Pick one tone-mark style (hòa/thủy vs hoà/thuỷ) and use it consistently.
   - Watch hỏi/ngã errors and the usual confusions (dành/giành, chẩn đoán, xán lạn, tham quan).
   - Normalise text to Unicode NFC (precomposed). Decomposed text from some copy paths breaks fonts in Canva and CapCut.
   - Recommend fonts with full Vietnamese glyphs (Be Vietnam Pro, Inter, Roboto, Montserrat, Lexend).
5. **Localise, don't convert.**
   - Examples: Tết, school fees, "lương 3 cọc 3 đồng" (meagre salary), Grab drivers, spa owners in Đà Nẵng.
   - Prices that make sense locally: tripwire 99k–499k, course 1–5tr, high-ticket tens of millions. [P]
   - Never US references.
6. **Do not fabricate statistics** ("90% chủ spa…"). It is a legal and trust risk.

## 9) Frameworks and fill-in templates for the Vietnamese edition (my own drafts)

**A. Voice Card ("Hồ sơ giọng")**

```
Tôi tự xưng: ___ | Gọi người xem: ___ | Giọng vùng: Bắc/Nam/Trung/trung tính
Độ trang trọng (1–5): ___ | Từ đệm: ___ | Tiếng Anh được chen: ___
Emoji: không/ít/vừa | 3 câu cửa miệng: ___ | Từ cấm: ___
Khách lý tưởng gọi mình là: thầy/cô/anh/chị/coach/tên riêng
```

**B. Facebook "Chia sẻ thật" long post**

```
Dòng 1–3 (trước "Xem thêm"): [khoảnh khắc cụ thể] + [điều bất ngờ]
→ Bối cảnh (ai, khi nào) → Sai lầm phổ biến → Góc nhìn của mình (1 ý)
→ 3 bước làm ngay → Câu chốt
→ "Ai cần [checklist ___] comment '[TỪ KHÓA]', mình gửi inbox nha."
```

Example opening: "11 giờ đêm, một chị chủ spa nhắn mình: 'Em ơi, chị đăng bài mỗi ngày mà 2 tháng chưa có khách nào inbox.' Mình hỏi lại đúng 1 câu…"

**C. TikTok "Góc nhìn" (45–75s)**

```
0–3s:  "[Chuyện đang hot]. Dưới góc nhìn của một [nghề], mình thấy [nhận định ngược]."
3–10s: Vì sao nhiều người hiểu sai
```

## 10) How the Vietnamese edition must differ beyond translation

1. **Platform map:** Facebook personal profile, TikTok and Zalo as the core; LinkedIn optional; email replaced by Zalo/Messenger nurture.
2. **Onboarding adds the Voice Card:** pronouns, dialect, register, code-switching level.
3. **A native hook, post and DM bank** written in Vietnamese, plus a banned-phrase list and a read-aloud QA step.
4. **Native formats as first-class templates:** chia sẻ long post, góc nhìn, Phần 1/2/3, livestream, workshop 0đ, Zalo Nhật ký.
5. **A trust module tuned for a low-trust market:** proof over hype; a "chống lùa gà" (anti-scam-vibe) style guide; humility norms; public pricing.
6. **A compliance layer built into every converting script,** using Vietnamese law and Meta/TikTok rules (section 7), with special-category switches for health, finance, education and real estate.
7. **A Vietnamese calendar:** lunar dates, tháng cô hồn, 13/10, 20/10, 20/11, double-day sales.
8. **Vietnamese pricing and payments:** VietQR, đ formats, local price ladders.
9. **A Vietnamese tool stack:** Pancake/Botcake, LadiPage, Zalo OA, payOS/Casso.
10. **Typography notes:** Vietnamese-safe fonts, NFC normalisation.
11. **Separate audience presets:** "chủ doanh nghiệp 35+" (anh/chị, Facebook/Zalo, low slang) vs "Gen Z/young pros" (mình/bạn, TikTok/Threads).
12. **Cross-assistant support:** test on ChatGPT and Gemini as well as Claude.
13. **Don't rely on account memory.** Many Vietnamese users share ChatGPT accounts [K-medium], so the brand brief should live in a file the user pastes in.
