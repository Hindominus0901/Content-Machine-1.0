# Entertainment Format Catalog v2 for Content Machine 1.0

**Scope.** This is the entertainment and relatable short-form part of the pack, for coaches, consultants and service businesses working DIY at 1-2 h/week, in EN and VN editions. It merges the v1 22-format catalog with what the global and Vietnam scouts found. Where two formats used the same mechanic, they are now one format, and the other name survives as a variant.

**How to read each card**
- **Stage**
  - **Likable** is about who the buyer wants to be *around*: warmth, flaws, shared moments.
  - **Admirable** is about who the buyer wants to *be*: competence, principles, the path ahead.
  - "L → A" means a bridge format that does both.
- **Character field** is the Character Card field the format expresses: trait, principle, value, enemy, quirk, flaw or vision.
- **Tier** is the production effort:
  - **no face**: text over B-roll or screenshots.
  - **face no talk**: you react or lip-sync.
  - **talking**: you speak to camera.
  - **skit**: you play roles.
- **Buyer filter (applies to every format).** Every moment has to sit where the ICP (who buys) overlaps the dream follower (who follows and shares), as Soo Wei Goh frames it. The quick test: *your ICP would tag someone, and a random 19-year-old would scroll past.*

### E01. POV: [buyer moment] / POV: [khoảnh khắc của khách]
- **Mechanic:** The camera is the viewer, so the buyer is "you." You play the other person in the scene (client, staff member, spouse) and deliver 4-6 lines that escalate. It ends on a flip line or end-card that carries your principle.
- **Stage:** Likable. **Field:** principle (in the flip). **Tier:** talking. The no-face variant puts text over B-roll of the object, such as a phone lighting up.
- **Buyer filter:** The moment has a time, an exact phrase or an object taken from the VoC bank, and it happened to the buyer this month. "You" is always the buyer, never your coaching peers.
- **On-screen text:** `POV: [time/day] and your [client/staff/boss] just [exact action]`
- **Beats:** 0-2s text plus first line → 2-20s four escalating lines → 20-27s last line twists → 27-30s end-card principle (≤8 words).
- **Caption + CTA:** "Tag the [buyer] who's lived this. [One-line principle]." / Comment **SCRIPT** and I'll send the reply I'd use.
- **EN (business coach for service providers):** "POV: it's 10:47pm and a client texts 'quick question 🙂'." Coach as the client: "Quick question… actually three… can we also redo the logo… I know it's late but you're so fast!" End-card: "Boundaries aren't rude. Your price just doesn't include 10:47pm."
- **VN (coach cho chủ quán/SME):** "POV: mùng 4 Tết, bạn vừa mở lại quán và nhân viên nhắn…" Coach đóng vai nhân viên: "Chị ơi em xin nghỉ thêm 3 hôm… à chị cho em ứng lương trước được không… với lại… em định nghỉ luôn ạ." End-card: "Thưởng Tết không giữ được người. Lộ trình mới giữ được."
- **Modeled on:** Corporate Natalie ("Today DRAINED ME": camera as you, doorway monologue). VN post-Tết staff-quitting moment.

### E02. Types of [people in the buyer's world] / Các kiểu [khách] mà [nghề] nào cũng gặp
- **Mechanic:** One archetype per video, run as a series. A prop or costume marks the type. The punchline is the gap between what the person says and what they do. The tone is affectionate, never mean.
- **Stage:** Likable. **Field:** enemy (the behaviour, never the person). **Tier:** skit (solo).
- **Buyer filter:** The archetype is someone your buyer deals with: their client, staff or relative. If the archetype *is* your buyer, frame it as a version of you ("me in 2021"). The buyer is never the butt of the joke.
- **On-screen text:** `[Type of person in buyer's world] who [one exaggerated behaviour]:`
- **Beats:** 0-2s text → 2-22s 4-6 lines in character → 22-30s a last line that contradicts the first.
- **Caption + CTA:** "We all have one. Tag yours (gently 😅)." / Comment **FIX** for how I handle this type.
- **EN (business coach):** "Client who says they're 'not ready to raise prices' while fully booked:" "I don't want to scare anyone… maybe next year… *(checks calendar)* why am I working Saturdays again?"
- **VN (coach cho freelancer/agency):** "Kiểu khách 'em tin team lắm' nhưng sửa 17 lần:" "Em tin team lắm… chỉ sửa xíu thôi… logo to hơn mà nhỏ lại xíu… à mà bản số 1 đẹp nhất anh ạ."
- **Modeled on:** Corporate Natalie ("Coworker who thinks they're the only person with a hard job"; "My work bestie while I'm trying to work"). v1 "types of clients."

### E03. IYKYK / Ai từng ___ sẽ hiểu
- **Mechanic:** One tiny, very specific detail from the buyer's world, shown silently: a screenshot, an object, a calendar. Insiders recognise it instantly and outsiders don't. The specificity is the joke.
- **Stage:** Likable. **Field:** quirk (a shared one). **Tier:** no face. The end frame shows your face or handle, because anonymous accounts build no trust.
- **Buyer filter:** If your mom gets it, it's too broad. The detail should be unreadable to non-buyers.
- **On-screen text:** `If you know, you know: [one specific detail]` / `Ai từng [trải nghiệm của khách] sẽ hiểu`
- **Beats:** 0-3s text over the detail → 3-10s slow zoom or a second detail → 10-15s "…and yes, I still do this."
- **Caption + CTA:** "IYKYK. Drop a 🫠 if this is you." / Comment **LIST** for the checklist that fixes it.
- **EN (coach for agency owners):** Over a Slack screenshot: "The client's 'Hey, got a sec?' at 4:58pm on a Friday. IYKYK."
- **VN (coach cho chủ shop online):** "Ai từng livestream 2 tiếng, có 3 người xem, 1 người là mẹ mình, sẽ hiểu."
- **Modeled on:** Consulting Comedy (faceless relatable text), the founder's "Ai từng ___ sẽ hiểu" hook, and v1 IYKYK.

### E04. Expectation vs reality / "Người ta nói…" → "Thực tế:"
- **Mechanic:** The advice the buyer keeps hearing appears as a quote, followed by one silent 5-10s shot of their real life. It takes a side against the advice.
- **Stage:** Likable. **Field:** enemy (the bad advice). **Tier:** face no talk.
- **Buyer filter:** The quote comes from VoC, meaning advice the buyer was actually given. The reality shown is their constraint (time, family, cash), not yours.
- **On-screen text:** `"[Advice my buyer keeps hearing]"` → `The reality:` / `Thực tế:`
- **Beats:** 0-3s the quote → 3-12s silent reality → 12-15s optional stance text (≤6 words).
- **Caption + CTA:** "The advice isn't wrong. It just skips [real constraint]." / Comment **REAL** for the version that works with [constraint].
- **EN (fitness coach for busy professionals):** "'Just eat less and move more'" → "The reality:" standing at an open fridge at 11pm, laptop still open on the counter.
- **VN (tư vấn tài chính cho hộ kinh doanh):** "'Tách tiền nhà và tiền quán ra là xong'" → "Thực tế:" one envelope labelled "TIỀN QUÁN" is opened for tiền học con, tiền điện, tiền ứng lương and tiền chợ.
- **Modeled on:** Corporate Natalie ("Is the 'office culture' in the room with us?", 2.8M). v1 expectation vs reality.

### E05. Me explaining [buyer's choice] to [family] / Giải thích cho [bố mẹ/họ hàng] (bản Tết)
- **Mechanic:** A two-role skit. You, playing the buyer's position, try to explain a modern work choice to a relative. The relative's questions are the punchlines, and they voice the buyer's own objections. In VN this absorbs the Tết questions and "con nhà người ta."
- **Stage:** Likable. **Field:** value (in the final answer). **Tier:** skit (solo; glasses or a cardigan mark the relative).
- **Buyer filter:** The skit explains the buyer's decision (going freelance, opening a shop, raising prices), not your coaching jargon. The relative's lines come from the VoC objection bank.
- **On-screen text:** `Me explaining [buyer's job/decision] to my [relative]:` / `Giải thích cho [bác/mẹ] vì sao [quyết định], mùng 2 Tết:`
- **Beats:** 0-2s text → 2-22s three Q&As that escalate → 22-30s the relative's last question hits the real fear, and your one-line answer is the value.
- **Caption + CTA:** "Every holiday. Every year." / Comment **TET** for the 3 answers I give.
- **EN (career coach, corporate to freelance):** "Me explaining 'fractional CMO' to my dad at Thanksgiving:" "So you're unemployed in four places?" "Four clients, Dad." "Who pays your pension?" "Me." "And when it's slow?" "Then I sell. I'd rather learn that now than at 55."
- **VN (coach cho người mở tiệm nhỏ):** "Giải thích cho bác vì sao bỏ ngân hàng ra mở tiệm bánh, mùng 2 Tết:" "Thế lương tháng bao nhiêu?" "Dạ tháng nào bán được thì có." "Con nhà ông Tư làm ngân hàng mua ô tô rồi đấy." "Lỡ ế thì sao?" "Dạ thì con học cách bán. Còn hơn cả đời không biết mình làm được không."
- **Modeled on:** v1 "me explaining X to Y"; Corporate Natalie (work and home colliding); VN founder hooks (Tết questions, con nhà người ta).

### E06. Using [buyer's lingo] in real life / Dùng ngôn ngữ [nghề] ngoài đời
- **Mechanic:** 4-5 home micro-scenes, each misusing one work phrase in daily life. The last is the bluntest mash-up. Only insiders get every line, which builds belonging.
- **Stage:** Likable. **Field:** quirk. **Tier:** skit (solo).
- **Buyer filter:** The lingo is the buyer's daily vocabulary (their tools, KPIs, client phrases). Avoid coaching words like "mindset" unless your buyers are coaches.
- **On-screen text:** `Using [industry] lingo in real life`
- **Beats:** five scenes of about 5s each, then a final 5s mash-up.
- **Caption + CTA:** "Which one have you said out loud? 😅" / Comment the line you've actually said; optional keyword **LINGO** for part 2.
- **EN (coach for agency owners):** "Let's put a pin in dinner." "Revisions on these dishes by EOD?" "That's out of scope, babe." "Per my last text…" Final: "Circling back on the dishes. They're still in the sink."
- **VN (coach sales/bảo hiểm):** "Mẹ ơi, mẹ đang quan tâm gói rửa bát nào ạ?" "Anh 'để suy nghĩ thêm' về đi chợ hả, 8h em follow-up nha." Final: "Ưu đãi chỉ áp dụng cho người rửa bát trong hôm nay."
- **Modeled on:** Corporate Natalie ("Using corporate lingo in real life", 1.8M).

### E07. What your [buyer choice] says about you / [Lựa chọn] của bạn nói gì về bạn
- **Mechanic:** Green screen over a phone Notes list of five options the buyer picks between, with a one-line verdict on each. The last item is the funniest or your recommended answer. Viewers comment theirs, which gives you reply-video material.
- **Stage:** L → A. **Field:** principle (the verdicts are your standards). **Tier:** talking (green screen).
- **Buyer filter:** The items are choices only your buyer makes: pricing page, bio, sign-off, discovery-call opener.
- **On-screen text:** `What your [buyer choice] says about you:`
- **Beats:** 0-2s title → 2-25s five items at 4-5s each → 25-30s "Drop yours, I'll rate it."
- **Caption + CTA:** "I'll rate the first 20 below." / Comment **RATE** plus yours. Pin the best and reply to it with a video.
- **EN (career coach):** "What your LinkedIn headline says about you:" Passionate professional ("you're hiding"), Ex-[Big Co] ("your best year was 2019"), Open to work ("asking, not offering"), Emojis only ("a vibe, not a job"), "[Result] for [who]" ("hireable").
- **VN (coach thương hiệu cá nhân cho chủ DN nhỏ):** "Bio Facebook của bạn nói gì về bạn:" "CEO & Founder" (của một người), "Sống là phải cháy", "Inbox để biết giá" (bạn đang sợ báo giá), "Không bán hàng online" (nhưng đăng bán mỗi ngày), "[Kết quả] cho [ai]" (khách hiểu ngay).
- **Modeled on:** Corporate Natalie ("What your Email Sign-Off Says About You"). v1 green screen, which now counts as a technique rather than a format.

### E08. "We're [buyer identity]. We…" self-roast / "Bọn mình là [danh tính]. Bọn mình…"
- **Mechanic:** A walking selfie with five stereotype confessions about a shared identity. It ends with something from that world interrupting you.
- **Stage:** Likable. **Field:** flaw (shared). **Tier:** talking (walk-and-talk).
- **Buyer filter:** Only say "we" about an identity you share or shared with the buyer. If you don't share it, use E02. Roast yourself and the identity, never a subgroup of your buyers.
- **On-screen text:** `We're [identity]. We…` (or spoken with no title)
- **Beats:** 0-3s first confession → 3-22s four more, escalating → 22-30s the phone rings or a kid shouts, then you look to camera.
- **Caption + CTA:** "Send this to the [identity] who needs to feel seen." / Comment **WE** plus the line I missed.
- **EN (coach for coaches):** "We're coaches. We tell clients to rest and answer DMs at midnight. We have four unfinished courses. We charge less than our own coach…" *(phone rings)* "Hi! Yes, totally free!"
- **VN (coach F&B, từng làm chủ quán):** "Bọn mình là chủ quán. Bọn mình nói 'mai nghỉ' từ 2019. Bọn mình biết giá hành tăng trước cả báo. Khuyên nhân viên nghỉ ngơi rồi tự rửa bát lúc 1h sáng…" *(điện thoại)* "Alo, 30 ly ạ? Dạ có liền."
- **Modeled on:** Corporate Bro ("We're Salespeople… We like money.", 3.7M).

### E09. "[Buyer event] be like:" paper-sign skit / "[Sự kiện] kiểu:" (bảng giấy nhiều vai)
- **Mechanic:** One person plays every role, each marked by a paper sign (CLIENT, SPOUSE, INNER CRITIC, YOU), with hard cuts between them. It acts out an objection or a decision without lecturing.
- **Stage:** Likable. **Field:** principle (how YOU handle the moment). **Tier:** skit (solo).
- **Buyer filter:** The event is on the buyer's calendar: discovery call, price-raise day, first hire, Tết bonus. The roles are the voices in the buyer's head and life.
- **On-screen text:** `[Event in buyer's world] be like:` / `[Sự kiện] kiểu:`
- **Beats:** 0-2s title → 2-25s 4-6 cuts, one line per sign → 25-30s the YOU sign says the principle, or holds an awkward silence.
- **Caption + CTA:** "Which sign is loudest for you?" / Comment **CALL** for the script.
- **EN (business coach):** "Discovery calls be like:" CLIENT: "Love it!" SPOUSE: "We need to think about it." INNER CRITIC: "Who do you think you are?" YOU: "So… next steps?" *(silence)*
- **VN (coach cho chủ spa/salon):** "Ngày tăng giá dịch vụ kiểu:" KHÁCH QUEN: "Ủa sao tăng vậy em?" NHÂN VIÊN: "Chị ơi khách hỏi em nói sao?" TIẾNG NÓI TRONG ĐẦU: "Hay giữ giá thêm năm nữa…" MẸ: "Tăng giá mất khách đấy con." MÌNH: "Dạ, giá mới là giá của tay nghề ạ."
- **Modeled on:** Corporate Bro ("National Sales Meetings and Sales Kickoffs be like:"). v1 skits, which now counts as a tier rather than a format.

### E10. Coach × sidekick duo / "Không hiểu sao coach cứ…" + "Mặt tối của coach"
- **Mechanic:** Two recurring characters, the coach and an assistant, student or partner. The sidekick complains about, or "exposes," one of the coach's habits. The coach reveals the standard behind it, and the sidekick gets the last, self-deprecating word. The coach is always the butt of the joke. Give the series a hashtag universe.
- **Stage:** L → A. **Field:** quirk (exposed) → principle (revealed). In the "Mặt tối" variant: flaw → value. **Tier:** skit (two people, a real workplace).
- **Buyer filter:** The exposed habit is something done *for* the buyer (checking their numbers, calling back their leads), so the joke is also proof that you care.
- **On-screen text:** `I don't get why my coach always [habit] 😭` / `Không hiểu sao coach cứ [thói quen] 😭`; variant `Mặt tối của coach mà học viên không biết`
- **Beats:** 0-3s the complaint → 3-15s an exaggerated example → 15-24s the coach's one-line reason, with a number if possible → 24-30s the sidekick's last word.
- **Caption + CTA:** "[Punchline]. We do [service] btw." / "Bên mình làm [dịch vụ] nha." / Comment **CALLBACK** for the follow-up script.
- **EN (sales coach):** "I don't get why my coach makes me call back every 'let me think about it' 😭" Coach: "Three of your five deals last month came on the second call." Student: "Fine. I'm calling. I'm *calling*."
- **VN (coach kinh doanh):** "Mặt tối của coach: 11h đêm vẫn soi số liệu học viên." Trợ lý quay lén: coach đang ghi voice note 7 phút "Chị Hoa tuần này 12 đơn mà quảng cáo 8 triệu…" rồi bấm *hẹn giờ gửi 8h sáng*. Trợ lý: "Coach ơi đi ngủ đi ạ." Coach: "Học viên của mình không phải nhận tin lúc 11h đêm." This flips the 11pm-boss hook into a principle.
- **Modeled on:** Phương Thử Việc × Sếp Việt / MD Việt Nam ("Mặt tối của Sếp", 2.9M; "Không hiểu sao sếp…"). Dalali Academy (cô Lan boss–staff skits).

### E11. Real [DM/email] sent in by a [community name] / Tin nhắn có thật do một [tên cộng đồng] gửi
- **Mechanic:** Play both sides of a real message a follower sent in, anonymised and used with permission, then give your one-line fix. It is a named series, and submissions keep it supplied.
- **Stage:** L → A. **Field:** principle (the fix). **Tier:** skit (solo, both sides). The no-face variant is a screenshot with text.
- **Buyer filter:** Use only messages the *buyer* received from their clients, boss or family, never one your own client sent you. Strip names and identifying details.
- **On-screen text:** `A real [DM/email] sent in by a [community name]:` / `Tin nhắn có thật do một [tên cộng đồng] gửi:`
- **Beats:** 0-3s label and first line in character → 3-20s back and forth → 20-30s "Here's what I'd reply:" with the reply on screen.
- **Caption + CTA:** "Send me yours (anonymised). The best one gets a reply video." / Comment **SEND** for the submission form plus the reply-scripts doc.
- **EN (coach for consultants):** "A real email sent in by a Profit Pirate: 'Can you do a quick 15-min audit, unpaid? Could lead to more work!'" Fix: "Happy to. My audit is $X and credits toward the project if we go ahead."
- **VN (coach cho freelancer thiết kế):** "Tin nhắn có thật do một 'Chiến Binh Báo Giá' gửi: 'Em làm thử 1 mẫu logo free nha, đẹp chị lấy luôn.'" Fix: "Dạ em không làm mẫu miễn phí, em gửi chị 3 dự án tương tự để chị xem phong cách trước ạ."
- **Modeled on:** Corporate Bro (Cold Call Reenactment, the "Sales Savages" series).

### E12. Gatekeeper vs insider / "Họ không biết là mình biết"
- **Mechanic:** A gatekeeper in the buyer's world says no. You whisper an aside to camera ("watch this"), then use one specific, accurate rule or tactic. The gatekeeper caves and asks "Who taught you that?", and the credit goes to you or your free guide. A costume swap marks the second role.
- **Stage:** Admirable. **Field:** enemy (the default "no") + principle (the tactic). **Tier:** skit (solo, costume swap).
- **Buyer filter:** The gatekeeper is someone the buyer negotiates with: a recruiter, landlord, supplier or procurement team. The tactic is one your method actually teaches.
- **On-screen text:** The gatekeeper's line, e.g. `"That's the top of the band."`
- **Beats:** 0-3s the no → 3-7s the aside → 7-20s the move → 20-25s the cave → 25-30s "Who taught you that?" "[Name]. Free script in my bio."
- **Caption + CTA:** Credit plus the free guide. / Comment **SCRIPT**.
- **EN (career coach):** Recruiter: "That's the top of the band." Aside: "She doesn't know I've seen the posted range." The candidate cites the range and asks for a 6-month review in writing. "Who taught you that?" "The free salary script in my bio."
- **VN (coach cho chủ tiệm thuê mặt bằng):** Chủ nhà: "Năm sau tăng 20% nha." Aside: "Chú không biết mình đã hỏi giá 3 mặt bằng cùng phố." "Dạ con trả đúng hạn 3 năm chưa trễ ngày nào, 2 mặt bằng bên cạnh trống 4 tháng rồi. Con xin giữ giá, ký thêm 2 năm." "…Ai dạy con nói vậy?" "Dạ kịch bản ở bio ạ."
- **Guardrails:** Run at most one per month. Don't use "they don't want you to know" framing. No real logos. Every claim must be accurate. No promised money outcomes.
- **Modeled on:** Erika Kullberg ("What Nike doesn't want you to know…", 76M; "Who taught you this?").

### E13. Two paths, one counter / Hai con đường (bản "con nhà người ta")
- **Mechanic:** A counter sits at the top of the screen. Path A is the common way and Path B is your method. Time jumps forward, the counter diverges, and Path A asks "Who told you about this?" In the VN edition, "con nhà người ta" (the perfect neighbour's kid) is Path A, and the counter shows the hidden cost of their path.
- **Stage:** Admirable. **Field:** principle (the method) / vision. **Tier:** skit (solo; a wig or prop marks Path A).
- **Buyer filter:** The counter is a number the buyer already tracks. Path A is what the buyer does today, from VoC, shown with empathy. Label it "illustrative, not a promise."
- **On-screen text:** `[Metric]: [start value]` plus `Path A: [common way]` / `Path B: [your way]`
- **Beats:** 0-3s setup → 3-22s three time jumps with the counter updating → 22-30s "Who told you about this?"
- **Caption + CTA:** "Illustrative. The difference is [principle]." / Comment **PATH**.
- **EN (fitness coach):** Counters for "Gym hours this month" and "Progress." Path A does random workouts six days a week (24h, progress flat). Path B does three structured sessions plus sleep (12h, progress up).
- **VN (coach kinh doanh cho chủ shop):** Counter "Tiền còn lại cuối tháng." Con nhà bác Tư chạy quảng cáo, giảm giá, đơn nhiều, tiền còn lại 0. Mình tăng giá 10%, chăm khách cũ, đơn ít hơn, tiền còn lại tăng. Mẹ: "Sao con nhà bác Tư bán nhiều thế?" "Dạ bán nhiều, mẹ hỏi bác còn lại bao nhiêu xem." Bác Tư: "Ai chỉ cháu vậy?"
- **Modeled on:** Erika Kullberg ("How to DOUBLE your salary" running counter). VN "con nhà người ta" hook.

### E14. SAY THIS WHEN [authority] [moment]! / NÓI CÂU NÀY KHI…!
- **Mechanic:** A selfie with the other person's line in a grey bubble, your scripted reply in a green bubble, then 2-3 numbered rules. Under 20 seconds: a script for a scary moment.
- **Stage:** Admirable. **Field:** value (boundaries, fairness). **Tier:** talking. The no-face variant uses bubbles and voiceover only.
- **Buyer filter:** The authority is in the buyer's world (boss, client, supplier, mother-in-law asking about money), and the moment comes from VoC.
- **On-screen text:** `SAY THIS WHEN [authority] [moment]! — FROM A [profession]` / `NÓI CÂU NÀY KHI [ai] [khoảnh khắc]! — TỪ MỘT [nghề]`
- **Beats:** 0-2s headline → 2-6s their bubble → 6-12s your bubble → 12-20s the rules.
- **Caption + CTA:** "Save this for next time." / Comment **SCRIPT** for 5 more replies.
- **EN (career/leadership coach):** "SAY THIS WHEN YOUR BOSS ADDS 'ONE MORE THING'!" Boss: "Can you take this on too?" You: "Happy to. Which current priority should I move to make room?"
- **VN (coach cho dân văn phòng):** "NÓI CÂU NÀY KHI SẾP NHẮN 11 GIỜ ĐÊM!" Sếp: "Em gửi lại file báo cáo giúp anh." Mình: "Dạ em nhận rồi ạ. 8h sáng mai em gửi bản đầy đủ, anh cần gấp phần nào nhất để em gửi trước không ạ?" Rules: 1) xác nhận đã đọc, 2) hẹn giờ cụ thể, 3) hỏi phần gấp nhất.
- **Modeled on:** Law By Mike ("SAY THIS TO THE COPS! FROM A LAWYER", 22.5M, 16s). VN 11pm-boss hook.

### E15. Things nobody tells you about [buyer milestone] / Những điều không ai nói với bạn khi [cột mốc]
- **Mechanic:** A text list of 3-5 unglamorous truths the buyer will meet at a milestone they want. The last truth turns warm. The honesty is what builds trust.
- **Stage:** L → A. **Field:** flaw (lived experience). **Tier:** no face (text over B-roll of your real day); a talking variant works too.
- **Buyer filter:** The milestone is on the buyer's path, taken from the desire and vision bank. The truths come from your experience or anonymised client experience.
- **On-screen text:** `Things nobody tells you about [milestone]:`
- **Beats:** 0-2s title → 2-25s four truths at 5s each → 25-30s "…and it's still worth it."
- **Caption + CTA:** "What would you add?" / Comment **FIRST** for the [milestone] checklist.
- **EN (business coach):** "Things nobody tells you about your first $10K month:" you'll check Stripe 40 times; you'll still feel like a fraud; a third of it is tax; you'll still be working Saturdays.
- **VN (coach cho người mới mở spa):** "Những điều không ai nói với bạn khi mở spa đầu tiên:" 6 tháng đầu bạn là lễ tân, kế toán, lao công; khách quen nhất là người xin giảm giá nhiều nhất; và lần đầu khách quay lại, bạn sẽ muốn khóc.
- **Modeled on:** v1 "things nobody tells you"; Therapy Jeff (list structure on a fixed set).

### E16. Dumb lessons from my first year / Những bài học ngốc nghếch năm đầu
- **Mechanic:** Three numbered mistakes you made at the buyer's current stage, told against yourself, then one sentence on what you do now.
- **Stage:** L → A. **Field:** flaw. **Tier:** talking.
- **Buyer filter:** The mistakes are the ones the buyer is making now, from the VoC pains. They get to diagnose themselves without being called out.
- **On-screen text:** `Dumb things I did in my first year as a [buyer's role]:` / `Những bài học ngốc nghếch năm đầu làm [vai trò]:`
- **Beats:** 0-3s title and a wince → 3-24s three mistakes, each with one concrete detail → 24-30s "Now I [principle]."
- **Caption + CTA:** "Which number are you on? No judgement, I did all 3." / Comment **1**, **2** or **3**.
- **EN (coach for agency owners):** "1. Took a client who said 'we don't really have a budget.' 2. Unlimited revisions to be 'nice.' 3. No contract because we had 'a good vibe.' Now: budget question on call one, two revisions, contract before kickoff."
- **VN (coach cho coach mới):** "Năm đầu làm coach: nhận khách không hợp vì sợ mất tiền; giảm giá cho người đầu tiên mặc cả; không có hợp đồng vì 'chỗ quen biết'. Giờ: có buổi lọc khách, giá niêm yết, hợp đồng trước buổi 1."
- **Modeled on:** Eric Thỏ ("Những bài học ngốc nghếch khi mới đi làm").

### E17. +1 sign you've leveled up / +1 tín hiệu bạn đã lên trình
- **Mechanic:** One tiny, slightly absurd moment that signals the buyer has reached a milestone they want. It's a meme format, and the sign is a behaviour, never a purchase. **List variant:** "5 signs you've outgrown [state]," a talking head on a fixed set with your product in frame.
- **Stage:** Likable (with vision). **Field:** vision. **Tier:** face no talk (a reaction or screen recording).
- **Buyer filter:** The milestone comes from the buyer's desire bank. No cars, watches or revenue screenshots.
- **On-screen text:** `+1 sign you've [milestone]: [tiny moment] 🤣` / `+1 tín hiệu bạn đã [cột mốc]: [khoảnh khắc] 🤣`
- **Beats:** 0-3s text → 3-10s the moment → 10-15s a smug or awkward face.
- **Caption + CTA:** "Collect them all. What's yours?" / Comment **+1** with your sign.
- **EN (business coach):** "+1 sign you've leveled up: a client asks 'when can I start?' before you've said the price."
- **VN (coach cho chủ spa):** "+1 tín hiệu spa bạn đã ổn: khách tự đặt lịch tháng sau trước khi về 🤣"
- **Modeled on:** Eric Thỏ ("+1 tín hiệu…", 1,199 shares on 132K views). Therapy Jeff ("N signs…") for the list variant.

### E18. I tested [common advice] & the ending / Thử [lời khuyên] [N] ngày & cái kết
- **Mechanic:** A mini-experiment on advice the buyer has heard. Show the setup, three checkpoints with real numbers (including the fails), a twist, and your verdict. Present results as your experience, not a promise.
- **Stage:** Admirable. **Field:** principle (the belief you tested); secondary trait (curiosity). **Tier:** talking.
- **Buyer filter:** Test advice the buyer is debating, taken from the VoC questions and objections, and measure something they care about. Use your own real numbers only.
- **On-screen text:** `I tried [advice] for [N] days. The ending:` / `Thử [lời khuyên] [N] ngày & cái kết…`
- **Beats:** 0-3s setup → 3-20s three checkpoints → 20-27s the twist → 27-30s a one-line verdict.
- **Caption + CTA:** "One person, one test, not science. Want the full numbers?" / Comment **TEST**.
- **EN (sales/business coach):** "I replied to every lead within 5 minutes for 30 days. The ending:" day 3: two calls booked; day 12: answered a lead during my kid's recital; day 30: speed mattered less than my first question. "Fast is good. Specific is better."
- **VN (coach marketing cho chủ quán cà phê):** "Thử đăng bài mỗi ngày 30 ngày cho quán & cái kết…" ngày 7: 3 khách mới; ngày 20: hết ý, đăng ảnh ly nước lần thứ 5; cái kết: bài nhiều khách nhất là chuyện bác bảo vệ. "Đều đặn quan trọng, chuyện thật quan trọng hơn."
- **Modeled on:** Jerry Lee / Wonsulting (resume "experiments"); Eric Thỏ ("& cái kết"); Bino ("Thử nhờ giáo viên người Mỹ review sách", where the product becomes a challenge).

### E19. Calendar or payday moment / Khoảnh khắc theo lịch (mùng 10, Tết, cuối quý)
- **Mechanic:** A joke pinned to a date the buyer feels, in a grateful-sarcastic frame: "Thank you [X] for teaching me [lesson] before [date]." Post it on or just before that date.
- **Stage:** Likable. **Field:** value (the lesson). **Tier:** face no talk.
- **Buyer filter:** The date matters to the buyer's business (their payday, peak season, a client's year-end freeze), not a generic holiday.
- **On-screen text:** `Thank you [clients] for teaching me [lesson] before [date] 🫶` / `Cảm ơn [khách/công ty] đã giúp em biết [bài học] trước [ngày] 🫶`
- **Beats:** 0-3s text over a deadpan face → 3-12s 2-3 shots of the situation → 12-15s the lesson moment.
- **Caption + CTA:** "Ai từng ___ trước [ngày] sẽ hiểu." / Comment **COC** or **DEPOSIT** for the deposit-policy template.
- **EN (coach for wedding/event vendors):** "Thank you, clients, for teaching me to take deposits before peak season 🫶" A calendar full of "tentative," then an empty Saturday.
- **VN (tư vấn cho chủ spa):** "Cảm ơn khách bùng lịch đã giúp em biết thu cọc trước Tết 🫶" Lịch kín, rồi ghế trống, rồi tờ "Chính sách đặt cọc" dán lên quầy.
- **Modeled on:** 3am D'spa ("Cảm ơn công ty… trước ngày mùng 10"); Corporate Bro (calendar-timed: kickoffs, conferences, Fridays).

### E20. "Based on a true story ⚠️" trend-audio skit / "Tất cả dựa trên câu chuyện có thật ⚠️"
- **Mechanic:** A 10-15s lip-sync or reaction to a trending sound. Line 1 is the buyer-world situation and line 2 is your reaction. The sound carries the emotion and the text carries the buyer's world.
- **Stage:** Likable. **Field:** quirk. **Tier:** face no talk.
- **Buyer filter:** The remove-the-audio test. If the text alone still names a buyer moment, keep it. If it reads "Mondays be like," kill it. Laugh at yourself or the situation, never the client.
- **On-screen text:** `When [buyer-world situation]` / `Me:` plus the tag `Based on a true story ⚠️`
- **Beats:** 0-2s line 1 → 2-12s lip-sync → 12-15s line-2 payoff.
- **Caption + CTA:** "Based on a true story ⚠️" plus "Ai từng… sẽ hiểu." / Comment-only CTA; trend posts are for reach.
- **EN (insurance/financial advisor):** "When I explain the policy terms for the third time and finally understand them myself ⚠️"
- **VN (coach cho chủ shop):** "Khi mình dặn học viên 'đừng rep khách lúc nửa đêm' rồi 0h15 mình vẫn đang trả lời inbox ⚠️"
- **Modeled on:** 3am D'spa ("Tất cả dựa trên câu chuyện có thật ⚠️", 7,923 shares on 263.6K views, about 30 per 1,000).

### E21. Then vs now / Hồi đó – Bây giờ (nostalgia)
- **Mechanic:** An old photo, video or screenshot from your early days set against now. It is driven by emotion and memory. The "now" is a behaviour or value, never a purchase, and it includes what is still hard.
- **Stage:** L → A. **Field:** vision (with a flaw in the "then"). **Tier:** no face (photos); face-no-talk variant.
- **Buyer filter:** The "then" should look like the buyer's present, so they see themselves in it.
- **On-screen text:** `[Year] me [doing what my buyer does now]` → `[Year] me:` / `Hồi [năm] mình [như khách bây giờ]…` → `Bây giờ:`
- **Beats:** 0-5s old photo and text → 5-15s 2-3 more details from back then → 15-25s now, including what's still hard → 25-30s "If you're in the 'then', you're not behind."
- **Caption + CTA:** A 3-5 line memory story. / Comment **THEN** plus your year.
- **EN (business coach):** "2019 me pricing my first package at $97 'to be fair'" (old Canva page) → "2026 me: still nervous every time I raise prices. I do it anyway."
- **VN (coach cho chủ shop online):** "Hồi 2018 mình livestream bằng điện thoại vỡ màn hình, 3 người xem, 1 người là mẹ" → "Bây giờ: vẫn run khi live, nhưng biết mình đang nói với ai."
- **Modeled on:** v1 nostalgia; the underdog backstories of Bino Chém Tiếng Anh and Eric Thỏ.

### E22. Origin storytime / "Vì sao mình bỏ [lựa chọn an toàn] để [con đường khó hơn]?"
- **Mechanic:** A storytime with one real cost, one belief (your polarity) and what you do now. It's an Admirable sacrifice that viewers will debate. Cut it to 30s; a 60-90s long version is optional.
- **Stage:** Admirable. **Field:** value + vision. **Tier:** talking.
- **Buyer filter:** Your "safe option" mirrors the buyer's current one, so the story shows their possible future rather than your biography. State the cost in relationships or time, not salary, to avoid reading as a flex.
- **On-screen text:** `Why I left [safe option] to [harder path]` / `Vì sao mình bỏ [X] để [Y]?`
- **Beats:** 0-3s the decision day → 3-15s the cost → 15-25s the belief → 25-30s "Now I help [buyer] [outcome] without [cost]."
- **Caption + CTA:** The full story, plus "Not everyone should do this. Here's who should." / Comment **STORY** for part 2 or the decision worksheet.
- **EN (career-pivot coach):** "Why I turned down a promotion to coach people who are scared to." Cost: "My mom asked if I'd been fired. Twice." Belief: "A career you're too tired to enjoy isn't safe. It's just slow."
- **VN (tư vấn tài chính hộ kinh doanh):** "Vì sao mình nghỉ trưởng phòng ngân hàng để đi tư vấn cho hộ kinh doanh?" Chi phí: "Tháng đầu thu nhập bằng 1/5." Niềm tin: "Hộ kinh doanh không thiếu tiền, họ thiếu một cuốn sổ đúng."
- **Modeled on:** Bino Chém Tiếng Anh ("Vì sao mình nghỉ việc ở Úc để về Việt Nam?", 6.3M); Eric Thỏ (PhD underdog post); v1 storytime.

### E23. A real day / behind-the-work vlog-skit / Một ngày thật làm việc cùng [khách]
- **Mechanic:** Real work footage (a client site, workshop or trip) with timestamps and one complaint or confession joke on top. The setting is the proof; the words don't have to claim anything.
- **Stage:** Admirable + Likable. **Field:** trait (how you work). **Tier:** face no talk (B-roll plus text); a talking variant works.
- **Buyer filter:** Show work done with buyers, with their consent and with faces and screens blurred. The joke is about you or the logistics, never the client.
- **On-screen text:** `A real day coaching [buyer type] (nobody films this part):` / `Không hiểu sao đi [việc] mà [chuyện hài] 😭`
- **Beats:** 0-3s hook → 3-24s 5-6 clips of about 4s with time labels → 24-30s an honest closer.
- **Caption + CTA:** "Real day, shared with permission. We run [service] btw." / Comment **DAY** for the worksheet used in the workshop.
- **EN (leadership trainer):** "A real workshop day (nobody films the 6am part):" 6:02 printing name tags; 9:00 the room is cold and so are the managers; 11:40 someone admits they've never given feedback; 16:30 lunch.
- **VN (trainer doanh nghiệp):** "Không hiểu sao đi dạy ở Đà Nẵng mà coach cho em ăn mì gói 3 ngày 😭" Trợ lý quay: lớp học thật phía sau, coach chữa bài đến 10h tối. Caption: "Bên mình có lớp [X] tháng sau nha."
- **Modeled on:** Phương Thử Việc × Sếp Việt (Germany and Korea trip skits, where the trip is the proof); Bino ("Một ngày làm việc trong ngân hàng ở Úc").

### E24. "Are you even a real coach?" reply / Trả lời "Có thật là coach không?"
- **Mechanic:** A reply-to-comment sticker on a skeptical comment, then "Yes," one proof and one honest flaw. Admitting the flaw is what keeps it from reading as nổ.
- **Stage:** Admirable (repairs trust). **Field:** flaw (admitted) + trait. **Tier:** talking.
- **Buyer filter:** Reply only to skepticism about outcomes your buyer cares about. The proof is years, client count or a consented case, never lifestyle.
- **On-screen text:** comment sticker `"Are you even a real coach?"` → `Yes.` / `"Có thật là coach không hay chỉ nói?"` → `Có.`
- **Beats:** 0-2s sticker → 2-5s "Yes." → 5-15s the proof → 15-25s "What I got wrong in year one…" → 25-30s invite questions.
- **Caption + CTA:** "Skepticism is healthy. Ask me anything about [topic]." / Comment **ASK**, which feeds a Q&A post.
- **EN:** "Yes. [9] years, [300+] clients. What I got wrong in year one: I said yes to anyone who could pay. Now the first call is me checking whether we're a fit." Fill in your real numbers.
- **VN:** "'Coach gì mà trẻ vậy?' Có. [6] năm, hơn [200] chủ quán. Điều mình sai năm đầu: dạy những gì mình đọc được, chưa phải những gì mình đã làm. Giờ mọi bài mình dạy đều đã chạy trên quán thật."
- **Modeled on:** Law By Mike ("Yes, I'm a real lawyer…").

### Merge map (v1 → v2), no duplicates
- POV → E01. Types of clients → E02. IYKYK and "Ai từng ___ sẽ hiểu" → E03. Expectation vs reality → E04.
- Me explaining X to Y, Tết questions and con nhà người ta → E05; con nhà người ta also feeds E13.
- Boss messaging at 11pm → E01, E10 (flipped into a principle) and E14.
- Things nobody tells you → E15. Nostalgia → E21. Storytime → E22. Daily life → E23.
- **Green screen and skits are now production techniques or tiers**, not formats. They are used inside E07, E09-E13 and others.
- **Moved to the Educational catalog**, because the payoff is information rather than recognition and they would inflate the entertainment cap:
  - myth reversal (Law By Mike's "things you think are illegal…")
  - collective mistake (Bino's "Người Việt hay sai…")
  - "N questions to ask your ___" (Therapy Jeff)
  - newsjacking with an expert verdict (Cô Long Truyền Thông)
- **Retired**, because the scouts saw them underperform or carry risk:
  - movie parodies (Corporate Bro's remix got 16K plays against 3.7M for his identity joke)
  - lifestyle POVs outside the buyer's world (Eric Thỏ's tennis POV got 8.2K)
  - celebrity or tragedy drama-jacking
- I didn't have the names of the remaining v1 formats. Apply the same rule to each: if its mechanic matches a v2 card, fold it in as a variant; keep it as a separate card only if the mechanic is genuinely new.

## 2. Moment-mining prompt (VoC bank + Character Card → 30 relatable moments)

```markdown
# ROLE
You are the Moment Miner for Content Machine 1.0. You turn a coach's real research into
30 relatable moments that entertain the RIGHT buyer, not everyone.

# INPUTS
- EDITION: {{EN | VN}}  → write every moment, hook and caption in this language.
  VN: default "mình – bạn"; use em/anh/chị only inside character skits; never call adult clients "các em".
- RESEARCH / VOC BANK: {{paste: verbatim quotes, DMs, reviews, call notes, objections, desires,
  words they use, tools, rituals, calendar/seasons, family pressures — each with an ID like V01}}
- CHARACTER CARD: {{traits, principles, values, enemy, quirks, flaws, vision, polarity}}
- ICP (who pays): {{one paragraph}}
- DREAM FOLLOWER (who follows and shares): {{one paragraph}}
- OFFER + FREE RESOURCE: {{what you sell; what a comment keyword can deliver}}
- FORMAT CATALOG: E01–E24 (Entertainment Format Catalog v2)

# STEP 1 — Find the overlap
List 5 "shared worlds" where the ICP and the dream follower live the same moments
(e.g. client work, money/pricing, family & social pressure, time/body/energy, calendar/seasons).
Discard any world only one of them lives in.

# STEP 2 — Mine raw moments (aim for 50, keep 30)
Each moment must come from the VoC bank and cite its source ID. You may add at most 5 from
the coach's own memory; mark those "COACH".
Write each moment as a scene: WHO + WHEN (time/day/season) + WHERE + the EXACT line or object
+ the feeling + what they do next.
BAD: "Clients don't respect boundaries."
GOOD: "10:47pm, a client texts 'quick question 🙂' while you're putting your kid to bed."

# STEP 3 — Four filters (score each 1–5; keep only 15+/20 with no 1s)
1. Specificity: contains at least 2 of time / exact phrase / object / number / place.
2. Overlap: would the dream follower SEND it to someone, AND would the ICP pay to fix it?
   (Test: the ICP tags a friend; a random 19-year-old scrolls past.)
3. Character: which Card field does it express, and what stance does the coach take?
   If any other coach could post it unchanged, it fails.
4. Safety: no punching down (the buyer or their clients are never the butt); no "nổ" or
   money flex; no identifiable real client; no health/finance outcome promises; no tragedy,
   no celebrity pile-on; nothing that makes the coach look lazy or incompetent.

# STEP 4 — Balance the 30
- Mix of worlds: 8 client/work · 6 money/pricing · 5 family/social (VN: Tết, con nhà người ta,
  họ hàng, mẹ chồng) · 4 calendar/seasonal · 4 identity/self (rituals, flaws) · 3 memory/then-vs-now.
- At least 3 moments per Character Card field (trait, principle, value, enemy, quirk, flaw, vision).
- Stage ratio ≈ 60% Likable / 40% Admirable.
- At least 10 moments filmable at the "no face" or "face no talk" tier.

# STEP 5 — Map and write
For each kept moment, output one table row:
| # | Moment (scene) | Source ID | World | Character field + stance | Stage (L/A) |
| Best 2 formats (E-IDs) | Tier | On-screen hook (≤12 words, edition language) |
| Comment keyword (VN: no diacritics, e.g. COC, GIA) | Bridge: the educational post that solves it |
| Score /20 |

# STEP 6 — Self-critique and rank
- Rewrite any hook that explains the joke, uses "100%", "they don't want you to know",
  revenue numbers, or a generic trend phrase ("Mondays be like").
- Flag anything that might read as mocking clients; rewrite it so the coach is the butt.
```

## 3. Rules: trust, cap, measurement

### 3.1 Five gates before any entertainment post goes out
1. **Buyer:** The moment comes from the VoC bank and passes the tag-or-scroll test.
2. **Character:** It names one Character Card field. If another coach could post it unchanged, rewrite it.
3. **Stage:** It is tagged Likable or Admirable, and that week's mix follows 3.3.
4. **Trust:** It clears every row of the trust-killer table in 3.2.
5. **CTA:** The joke stays in the video and the CTA goes in the caption, in one line, with one keyword.

### 3.2 What kills trust

| Killer | What it looks like | Scout evidence | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cringe** | Acting beyond your range, explaining the punchline, forced trend dances, high-cost productions on a DIY budget | Corporate Bro's movie parody got 16K plays against 3.7M for his phone-and-sidewalk identity joke | Pick the lowest tier you can do deadpan. Start with face-no-talk. One location, one joke. |
| **Nổ / khoe (bragging, showing off)** | Revenue screenshots, credential stacking, "100% người Việt…", fake social proof, scarcity lines ("chỉ còn 1 suất"), promised results | VTC on course sellers using sock-puppet accounts; Znews on real-estate FOMO; the Kera candy case (70M VND fines each, criminal case in 2025); Dược sĩ Tiến's "ChatDST" claim; Eric Thỏ's credential stacking flagged as "khoe" | Let the setting carry the proof (E23). Present numbers as your own experience with context. Use questions or "hầu hết" (most). Label E13 "illustrative." |
| **Punching down** | Mocking clients, a generation or junior staff; real client screenshots | Corporate Natalie's Gen Z clip drew backlash; scout flagged spa skits that mock clients | The buyer and their clients are never the butt. Roast yourself, the situation or the system. Anonymise and get permission (E11). |
| **Generic trends and off-world posts** | Trend audio with generic text, lifestyle POVs, drifting off-niche | Eric Thỏ's tennis POV got 8.2K and his lifestyle posts 3-8K; Natalie's engagement dropped off-niche; Corporate Bro's parodies flopped | Remove-the-audio test (E20). Every hook names a buyer moment. |
| **Money flex as the hook** | Expense-account excess, "money in the streets" jokes | Corporate Bro's bro excess; Phương's "tiền đầy đường" was flagged as an overselling risk | Show the value, not the purchase (E17, E21). |
| **Undercutting your competence** | Jokes about slacking or sleeping at work, being unreliable | Phương's sleeping-on-the-job skits ("don't copy" for coaches) | Use flaws you have fixed, or flaws that come from caring too much (E10). |
| **Conspiracy or outrage framing** | "They don't want you to know" on everything, celebrity pile-ons, tragedy newsjacking | Erika Kullberg's style called "Karen-esque"; Cô Long's tragedy coverage | Run E12 at most once a month. Never tragedy. Newsjacking lives in the Educational catalog. |
| **Hard sell in the hook** | Price lists or "inbox ngay" (DM now) in the video | VN reach collapse: Tun Phạm 3.9-4.4K views on product posts vs 173K on a non-product post; Lệ Thủy Bảo Hiểm 461-871 views vs 1.2M+; Đạt Vairo 300-900 views on course promos | Joke in the video, one soft caption line ("Bên mình làm ___ nha"), as Phương × Việt do |

| **Facelessness** | Meme-only accounts | Consulting Comedy (622K on IG) sells merch, not trust | Your name or face goes in the end frame of every no-face post. Never post two no-face posts in a row. |

### 3.3 Cap and cadence (20-30% of output)
- **Default: 25% entertainment.**
  - 3 posts/week = 12/month → **3 entertainment posts a month**.
  - 4 posts/week = 16/month → **4**.
  - 5 posts/week = 20/month → **5**.
- **Hard ceiling: 30%.** You may go above 25% only after four straight weeks in which entertainment beats your educational posts on both follows per 1,000 views and ICP comment share.
- **Floor: 20%.** Go below it only to fix a "wrong crowd" signal (see 3.4).
- **Never post two entertainment posts in a row.**
- **Run each moment through the bridge loop:**
  1. **Entertain:** name the moment.
  2. **Educate (within 48h):** solve the same moment.
  3. **Convert:** offer the next step, with the comment keyword linking all three.
- **Stage mix within entertainment:**
  - About 70% Likable / 30% Admirable for the first 8 weeks, while you're reaching new audiences.
  - About 50/50 after that.
  - Cover at least 4 different Character Card fields each month.
- **DIY batching:**
  - Keep a phone notes idea bank, as Corporate Natalie does.
  - Film the month's 3-5 entertainment posts in one 30-45 minute session.
  - Re-shoot proven winners after 3-6 months; Erika Kullberg remade her Nike hit after 3 years and it got 1.0M plays.
- **VN keywords:** short, uppercase, no diacritics (COC, GIA, TET, KICHBAN), so comment automation matches what people actually type.

### 3.4 How to measure
**Three primary metrics, per post, read at 7 days:**

| Metric | Formula | What it tells you |
|---|---|---|
| **Sends per 1,000 views** | (shares + DM sends) ÷ views × 1,000 | The moment is relatable enough to pass on. Primary for Likable posts. |
| **Follows per 1,000 views** | follows from the post ÷ views × 1,000 | The right strangers want more of *you*. Primary for all posts. |
| **ICP comment share** | ICP comments ÷ total comments, from the first 50 comments | You're attracting buyers, not just an audience |

An **ICP comment** is any comment that:
- (a) describes a situation from the buyer's world in the first person ("this is literally my Friday"), or
- (b) tags someone who is a buyer, or
- (c) asks about the method, price or offer, or
- (d) uses the post's keyword.

Count them by hand in 5 minutes a week. Secondary metrics: saves per 1,000 (for Admirable formats), keyword DMs and profile visits.

**Benchmark against yourself, not the internet.** The scouts' hits show share rates from about 0.2 to about 30 per 1,000 views, and the very large posts sit at the bottom of that range:

| Post | Shares | Views | Per 1,000 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Phương | 702 | 3.2M | ~0.2 |
| Việt "Mặt tối" | 2,388 | 2.9M | ~0.8 |
| Bino | 2,176 | 2.8M | ~0.8 |
| Cô Long | 3,602 | 692K | ~5.2 |
| Eric "+1" | 1,199 | 132K | ~9 |
| 3am D'spa | 7,923 | 264K | ~30 |

Niche posts earn far higher send rates, which is exactly the signal a coach wants. Use the **median of your last 10 posts** as your baseline.

**Decision table:**

| Pattern | Diagnosis | Action |
|---|---|---|
| High views, low follows/1k, low ICP share | Too generic; strangers laughed and left | Make the moment more specific: the buyer's own lingo, a time, an object |
| High sends, low ICP share | Right emotion, wrong crowd | Move the archetype or lingo into the buyer's world (E02/E06). Drop to the 20% floor for 4 weeks. |
| Low views, high ICP share or follows/1k | Right buyer, weak hook | Keep it. Rewrite the first 2 seconds of text and remake. |
| High ICP share plus keyword comments | Working | Turn it into a series with a hashtag universe, and publish the bridge educational post within 48h |

- **Pause a format** after 3 runs below your median on all three primary metrics. Retry it in 8 weeks with a different moment.
- **Double down on a format** when it reaches at least 1.5× your median on both follows/1k and ICP share twice. Make it a series and plan a remake.
