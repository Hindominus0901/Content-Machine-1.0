## 2) Patterns + templates

### 2.1 Hook types

**H1. Number-list.** About 40% of his LinkedIn posts open with a number [3P magicpost].
- Examples:
  - "10 unsexy habits that make me serious money:" (P2)
  - "I've hired 90+ high performers in the last 3 years. Here are 8 rare traits I see in every high performer:" (L11)
  - "10 ways to use LinkedIn to 10x your revenue:" (L2)
  - "33 habits that (quietly) changed my life forever:" (X)
  - "35 habits that make you instantly magnetic:" (X)
- Note the unexpected adjective: "unsexy", "(quietly)", "rare", "deadly".
- **Template:** `[N] [unexpected adjective] [habits/traits/systems/mistakes] that [specific outcome]:`. Authority variant: `I've [done X] [N+] times in the last [period]. Here are [N] [rare] [things] I see in every [winner]:`
- **Coach version:** "I've run 400+ discovery calls in 3 years. Here are 7 quiet signals that a client will actually buy:"

**H2. Age stamp ("I'm 3X") / letter to a younger self.**
- Examples:
  - "I'm 35. In my 20s, I had 0 followers and was a toxic workaholic. Today? …" (X)
  - "I just turned 34. … 10 lessons for 20-year-old Matt:" (L1)
  - "I'm 34. When I was younger, I was obsessed with shit that didn't matter. You can learn from my fuck ups. If you're 20-something, read this 👇" (L9)
  - "I'm 33. … 13 life lessons at 33 I wish I knew at 23:" (X)
  - "Life hacks I know at 36, I wish I knew at 19:" [3P]
- He re-runs this hook every birthday. Per magicpost [3P], at 35 it was "11 lessons for 21-year-old Matt" and at 36 "11 lessons for 18-year-old Matt", with nearly the same opening sentence.
- **Template:** `I'm [age]. [3 dramatic life facts, comma-separated]. [N] lessons for [younger age]-year-old [Name]:`
- **Coach version:** "I'm 41. I left a corporate job, lost my first 3 clients, and built a $40k/month practice. 9 lessons for 30-year-old me:"

**H3. Famous quote, then a bridge, then the promise.**
- Examples:
  - "Elon Musk once said: "Being an entrepreneur is like eating glass and staring into the abyss." No one talks about how lonely and confusing it gets. Here's how to deal with it:" (L8)
  - "Tony Robbins once said: …" How to design your dream life in 2025:" (T3)
  - "Mark Zuckerberg once said: "The greatest risk in life is not taking one." Here's how to build a profitable one-person business (in 90 days):" (T4)
  - Steve Jobs and Mark Twain versions [3P]
- **Template:** `[Famous person] once said: "[quote]." [One line naming the unspoken pain]. Here's how to [outcome] ([timeframe]):`
- **Coach version:** "Peter Drucker once said: "What gets measured gets managed." Most coaches measure nothing. Here's the 5-number dashboard I give every client:"

**H4. Money/metric proof up front.**
- Examples:
  - "I run a $15M portfolio where 97% of the cash comes from organic content." (P3)
  - "I generate $264,000 per month from content." (T list)
  - "I make $1.1M per month while taking 4-day weekends." (T list)
  - "In 2023, I made $8.7 million." (X)
  - "Twitter is an unfair advantage. In 2022, I added 172,204 followers and did $67,000/month…" (T5)
- Numbers are exact and often written out in full ("$264,000", "1,460 days"). His LinkedIn course reportedly says to write "$1,000,000" rather than "$1M" [3P Kruk].
- **Template:** `I [run/make/added] [exact number] [unit] where [surprising %/condition]. The [systems] behind it are almost embarrassingly [boring]. Here are the ones [actually doing the work]:`
- **Coach version:** "82% of my clients come from one LinkedIn post format. The system behind it is almost embarrassingly simple:"

**H5. Contrarian / reframe.** About one-third of posts contain an "it's not X, it's Y" contrast [3P].
- Examples:
  - "If you don't understand webinars, you don't understand marketing." (P1)
  - "You're not depressed; you're just in a sh*t situation." (L12)
  - "95% of founders are doing marketing wrong. … (Hint: It's not what you think)" (X)
  - "Sales are simple when you understand this secret: The moment you start selling, you've lost." [3P]
  - "Most founders think their product is more important than branding. That couldn't be farther from the truth." (X)
- **Template:** `If you don't understand [underrated thing], you don't understand [big category].` / `You're not [label]; you're just [reframe]. How to [get out]:` / `[N]% of [audience] are doing [X] wrong. I built [result] using this instead:`

## 3) What the Content Machine should copy

1. **Hook library skill with 12 typed templates** (H1–H12 above).
   - Rules: the hook ends in ":", carries one exact number or proof point, and uses an unexpected adjective ("unsexy", "quiet", "rare").
   - Re-run proven hooks with a new age or year.
   - The skill should output 3 hooks per post across different types.
2. **Item micro-structure as a hard rule.** Each item is: head (2–5 words), 1–3 short lines, three "•" bullets (optional), then a quotable payoff line.
   - Post budget: 250–450 words, about 8–10 words per sentence, no hashtags, at most 1 emoji, no exclamation marks.
3. **Six body archetypes** (A–F) as selectable scaffolds.
   - Default for coaches: **C (story, named method, handout, time-boxed instruction)**. It is his 2026 format and transfers best to service businesses.
4. **Named-IP generator.** Force the user to name every ritual, checklist and model ("Weekly Board Meeting", "Architect Move"), and reuse those names across posts.
   - Tie this to the existing "3 signature phrases" step and to his own "Create Proprietary Language" step.
5. **Contextual-CTA engine.** Map one lead magnet to each core topic, and generate the CTA by formula: "Want to see the complete [asset] I use to [result in this post]? Get the complete [asset] here:"
   - Add fallback CTAs: repost/follow, cheat-sheet PDF, dated workshop with "(N days away)", and an apply line.
   - Use per-platform tracked links (his pattern is `/x-…`, `/li-…`).
   - Name the assets "The [Adj] [Topic] Playbook/Scorecard".
6. **Image brief generator ("cheat-sheet image").** The image is the hook as title plus item heads, in a handwritten notebook or clean white style. It must make sense when reposted without the caption; stories and numbers stay in the caption.
   - Pair it with the "Want a high-res PDF of this cheat sheet?" opt-in.
7. **X thread builder (8–11 tweets).** Credibility hook, Title Case header tweets, a "Start tomorrow" tweet, then a "P.S. Speaking of…" CTA.
   - Also produce a hook-plus-image single post and a "What would you add?" list post from the same list.
8. **Humility and proof lines** (2026 pattern), useful for coach compliance: "These numbers come from [N years]… yours will look different. Start with [smallest step]."
9. **Recycling calendar.** Yearly re-dates ("…in 2027"), birthday "I'm X" lists, and a monthly top-5/bottom-5 review using reposts as the main metric [3P Kruk].
10. **Long-form shell** for newsletters and X Articles: a "Happy [Day]!" opener, a 2-line thesis, "Here's what you'll learn:", then PS (lead magnet) and PPS (tool or offer). Lowercase confessional titles.
11. **Do not copy blindly:**
    - Income-heavy hooks ($1.1M/month) can hurt coaches who don't have those numbers. Swap in client results, call counts or years.
    - Profanity hooks (L9, L12) were 2023–24 and are absent in 2026.
    - Some of his own numbers conflict across posts (waterfall 14/22/24/30; lead magnets 300+/500+; threads 2/week or 4/week). The pack should teach the patterns, not his numbers.
12. **VN edition.** H2, H3, H6 and H7 translate directly, for example "Tôi 35 tuổi. …", "[Tên] từng nói: "…"", "Mỗi tối Chủ nhật lúc 7 giờ…", "Cách [kết quả] (mà không cần [nỗi sợ]):". Keep the ":" ending and the exact-number rule. [Adaptation suggestion, not from his content.]
