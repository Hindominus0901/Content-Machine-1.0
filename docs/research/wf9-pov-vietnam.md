# Scout Vietnam: relatable, POV and skit creators for Content Machine 1.0 (VN edition)

**How this was researched (as of 2026-10-05).** Web search is US-centric and found few Vietnamese results. So I checked each creator's existence, audience and activity directly against TikTok's public profile, embed and oEmbed endpoints, and against Vietnamese press. All view counts were pulled on 2026-10-05.

What I could and could not verify:
- **Verified per post:** captions, upload dates, durations and the audio track used.
- **Not verified:** what happens on screen. "What happens" is inferred from captions and press descriptions.
- **On-screen text:** not checked frame by frame. Caption hooks usually mirror it.
- **Facebook and YouTube:** I did not verify the creators' Facebook or YouTube cross-posts.

**Recurring formats.**
- Boss vs intern banter (both accounts tag each other).
- "Không hiểu sao sếp…" complaints ("Why does the boss always…").
- Business-trip vlog-skits from Korea and Germany.
- Payday and money jokes ("Kiếm đủ ăn là em nghỉ làm😂", "Once I earn enough to eat, I'm quitting").
- Series hashtags #phuongthuviec, #vietldpt, #vutrumd ("MD universe").

**How it connects to sales.**
- The ICP is young Vietnamese thinking about study or work abroad, and their parents.
- The dream follower is the Gen Z office worker on a low salary.
- The overlap is the buyer: someone stuck at 3M VND a month is exactly who considers going abroad.
- Trips abroad act as proof without bragging.
- CTAs live in captions: "inbox em Phương nhé ạ" ("message me, Phương").

**Voice.**
- Intern says "em" and "mn" (everyone), ends lines with "ạ" and "nha", and calls herself "Phương" in the third person.
- The boss uses "tôi", "anh" and "bọn trẻ giờ…" ("kids these days…"), with Gen Z slang such as "đại bàng" (big-shot boaster).
- Emoji: 😭😂🥰🤩.
- No trending music; 100% original dialogue audio.

**What a coach can borrow.**
- Coach × client (or coach × assistant) as recurring characters.
- Let yourself be the butt of the joke.
- Put your service in one caption line.
- Use real work trips and sessions as settings.
- Name your universe with a hashtag.

**What NOT to copy.**
- An "anti-boss" bio; press noted it reads as negative.
- Jokes about sleeping or slacking at work, which undercut a coach's competence.
- Their posting volume (1–2 posts a day with a team) doesn't fit a 1–2 h/week DIY plan.
- "Money in the streets" jokes can drift into money-flex or overselling going abroad.

## 3. Vietnamese style guide: what reads as trusted and what reads as "nổ"

**On-screen and caption text.**
- Short and spoken-style.
- Ending particles: ạ, nha/nhaa, nhé, ơi, hông, thui.
- Emoji used as punctuation: 😭😂🥰🤩👀⚠️🫶.
- Chat abbreviations: mn, e, k.
- Third-person self-name ("Phương đã đến…").
- Stock frames:
  - "Không hiểu sao…" ("I don't get why…")
  - "Mặt tối của…" ("The dark side of…")
  - "Góc khuất…" ("The hidden side of…")
  - "Tất cả dựa trên câu chuyện có thật ⚠️" ("All based on a true story")
  - "…& cái kết" ("…and the ending")
  - "+1 tín hiệu…" ("+1 sign that…")
  - "Người Việt hay sai…" ("Vietnamese often get … wrong")
  - "Liệu có thành sự thật không mn😅" ("Will it come true, everyone?")
- Soft CTA inside the caption: "Bên … làm … nha" ("We do … btw"), "inbox em".

**Forms of address (xưng hô).**

| Speaker | Typical forms |
|---|---|
| Intern or underdog | "em", "ạ" |
| Boss character | "tôi", "anh", "bọn trẻ giờ…" ("kids these days…") |
| Spa team | "em", "chị", "sếp ơi", "cả nhà ơi" |
| Peer expert | "mình – bạn" |
| Educator | "mình – các bạn", "người Việt" |

Recommendation (my judgment): default to "mình – bạn" for coaches; use em/anh/chị only inside character skits; avoid calling adult clients "các em".

**Humor styles that work.**
- Self-deprecation about money and payday.
- Role reversal, where the boss or expert is the joke.
- Gen Z slang ("đại bàng", "check VAR", "buff ảo").
- Real workplaces rather than studios.
- Gentle teasing that is never mean to clients.

**Music and trends.**
- Office skits (Phương, Việt) use 100% original dialogue audio.
- Spa skits get their spikes from trending sounds ("CỐT SENORITA", "Không Buông").
- Bino's early hits used trending V-pop and remixes under voiceover.
- Commentary posts use original voice.

**What reads as "nổ" or cringe (with evidence).**
1. **Revenue flex and fake social proof.** Course sellers flash revenue, post from sock-puppet accounts that answer their own questions, and bait-and-switch on price ([VTC](https://vtcnews.vn/that-gia-khoa-day-ban-hang-chot-nghin-don-tren-tiktok-shop-ar704747.html)).
2. **FOMO scarcity lines in real estate.** "Chỉ còn đúng một nền" ("only one plot left"), "suất nội bộ cuối cùng" ("final insider allocation") breed skepticism ([Znews, May 2026](https://znews.vn/moi-gioi-bat-dong-san-do-bo-tiktok-san-khach-post1650295.html)).
3. **Exaggerated claims carry legal risk.** "1 viên bằng 1 đĩa rau" ("one candy equals a plate of vegetables") in the Kera candy case led to 70M VND fines each and a criminal case in 2025 ([Znews](https://znews.vn/toan-canh-vu-keo-rau-cu-truoc-khi-quang-linh-hang-du-muc-bi-khoi-to-post1543388.html), [Báo Văn Hóa](https://baovanhoa.vn/bao-chi/xu-phat-hang-du-muc-va-quang-linh-vlogs-140-trieu-dong-do-quang-cao-sai-su-that-124848.html)).
4. **Grandiose self-claims.** Dược sĩ Tiến's "ChatDST beats ChatGPT" (Jul 2025) was denied by the Nvidia distributor and pulled ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D%C6%B0%E1%BB%A3c_S%C4%A9_Ti%E1%BA%BFn)).
5. **Over-the-top ads.** "Lố" (overdone) product ads drew backlash ([GameK, Oct 2026](https://gamek.vn/lien-tuc-phu-song-cac-video-quang-cao-san-pham-18-hot-tiktoker-bi-nhac-dung-lam-qua-lo-178261003122109697.chn)).
6. **Hard-sell captions collapse reach.** The pattern holds across four accounts; the first two comparisons are only a few days old, so treat them as indicative.
   - **Tun Phạm (4.9M followers):** product posts on 4 Oct get 3.9–4.4K views, while a non-product post the day before got 173.3K.
   - **Long Chun (7.7M followers):** old skits reached 13–19M views; today's sale posts sit at 1.4–2.7K. These sale posts are only hours old.
   - **Lệ Thủy Bảo Hiểm (@lethuy.baohiem, insurance):** 2024 explainer reviews got 1.2–1.7M views; Sep 2026 package-list captions got 461–871 views.
   - **Đạt Vairo (video-marketing course):** course-promo posts get 300–900 views.

**What reads as trusted.**
- Underdog honesty about salary.
- A boss who can be teased.
- Real premises and team.
- Admitting bad years.
- Concrete corrections.
- An expert verdict on news the viewer already cares about.
- The CTA kept in the caption, not the hook.

## 4. Fill-in templates

Each template has a stage tag (A = Admirable, L = Likable) and a Vietnamese coach example with an English gloss.

**T1. Boss × underdog duo** (from Phương × Việt) [L, or A+L]
- On-screen: "Không hiểu sao [authority] cứ [habit from the buyer's world] 😭" ("I don't get why [authority] always…")
- Beats: the underdog complains → the authority reveals the standard or value behind it → the underdog gets a self-deprecating last word.
- Caption: "[punchline]. Bên [brand] làm [service] nha."

*Sales coach example.* On-screen: "Không hiểu sao coach cứ bắt em gọi lại khách đã 'để chị suy nghĩ' 😭" ("Why does my coach keep making me call back the clients who said 'let me think about it'"). The coach shows that 3 of last month's deals came from second calls. The student: "Thôi em gọi, em gọi…" ("Fine, I'll call, I'll call…"). Caption: "Ai làm sale sẽ hiểu. Bên mình kèm sale follow-up không bị lố nha." ("Anyone in sales gets it. We coach follow-ups that don't feel pushy.")

**T2. "Mặt tối của [coach/sếp]"** ("The dark side of [coach/boss]") [L]
- On-screen: "Mặt tối của [role] mà học viên không biết" ("The dark side of [role] that clients never see")
- The assistant or client exposes the coach's quirk (11 pm voice notes, rewriting slides 7 times). End on a caring reveal.
- *Business-coach example:* "Mặt tối của coach: 11h đêm vẫn soi số liệu của học viên" ("Coach's dark side: still checking clients' numbers at 11 pm").

**T3. Payday or calendar joke** (from 3am D'spa) [L]
- On-screen: "Cảm ơn [công ty/khách hàng] đã giúp em biết [lesson] trước ngày [mùng 10 / Tết / deadline]" ("Thanks to [company/clients] for teaching me [lesson] before [payday on the 10th / Tết / the deadline]")
- *Spa consultant example:* "Cảm ơn khách bùng lịch đã giúp em biết thu cọc trước Tết 🫶" ("Thanks to the no-show clients for teaching me to take deposits before Tết")

**T4. "Tất cả dựa trên câu chuyện có thật ⚠️"** ("All based on a true story") trend-audio skit [L]
- 10–15 s lip-sync to a trending sound.
- On-screen line 1: [client-world situation]; line 2: [your reaction].
- Laugh at yourself or the situation, never the client.
- *Insurance agent example:* "Khi mình giải thích điều khoản lần thứ 3 và chính mình cũng bắt đầu hiểu ra" ("When I explain the policy terms for the third time and finally understand them myself").

**T5. "[Nhóm của khách] nào cũng từng sai điều này"** ("Every [client group] has made this mistake") collective-mistake hook (from Bino) [A]
- On-screen: "Chủ [spa/shop/công ty] nào cũng từng sai điều này khi [moment]" ("Every [spa/shop/company] owner has gotten this wrong when…")
- Keep it a question or "hầu hết" ("most"). Avoid "100%".
- *Marketing coach example:* "Chủ spa nào cũng từng sai điều này khi đăng ảnh trước–sau?" ("Hasn't every spa owner gotten before/after photos wrong?")

**T6. Origin storytime** (from Bino and Eric) [A]
- On-screen: "Vì sao mình bỏ [safe option] để [harder path]?" ("Why I left [safe option] for [harder path]")
- Show one real cost, one belief (the polarity) and what you do now.
- *Consultant example:* "Vì sao mình nghỉ làm trưởng phòng ngân hàng để đi tư vấn tài chính cho hộ kinh doanh?" ("Why I quit as a bank department head to advise household businesses")

**T7. "Những bài học ngốc nghếch khi mới [stage]"** ("Dumb lessons from when I first [stage]") (from Eric) [L → A]
- 3 numbered mistakes, then one sentence on what you teach now.
- *Coach example:* "Những bài học ngốc nghếch năm đầu mình làm coach: nhận khách không hợp, giảm giá, không có hợp đồng" ("Dumb lessons from my first year coaching: wrong-fit clients, discounting, no contract")

**T8. "+1 tín hiệu…" / "…& cái kết"** ("+1 sign that…" / "…and the ending") (from Eric) [L]
- On-screen: "+1 tín hiệu bạn đã [milestone the buyer wants] 🤣" ("+1 sign you've reached [milestone]") or "Có 1 ngày để [task] & cái kết…" ("One day to [task], and the ending…")
- *Sales coach example:* "+1 tín hiệu bạn đã lên trình sale: khách tự nhắn hỏi khi nào ký" ("+1 sign you've levelled up in sales: clients message you asking when to sign")

**T9. Newsjacking with an expert verdict** (from Cô Long) [A]
- On-screen: "Vụ [trending industry incident]: nếu là [your role], mình sẽ xử lý thế nào?" ("The [incident] case: how would I handle it as [your role]?")
- Stick to your buyer's industry. Skip tragedies and celebrity attacks.
- *SME consultant example:* "Vụ quán bị tố chặt chém: nếu là chủ quán, 3 câu mình sẽ nói ngay" ("A restaurant accused of price gouging: the 3 lines I'd say right away as the owner")

**T10. Business-trip or behind-the-work vlog-skit** (from Phương and Việt) [A+L]
- Film real work (client site, workshop, a trip) with one complaint joke from your sidekick.
- The proof sits in the setting, not in the words.
- *Trainer example:* "Không hiểu sao đi dạy ở Đà Nẵng mà coach cho em ăn mì gói 3 ngày 😭" ("Why did my coach feed me instant noodles for 3 days on our Đà Nẵng teaching trip"). The real workshop shows in the background.

The founder's localized hooks plug straight into these. "Ai từng ___ sẽ hiểu" ("If you've ever ___, you'll get it") works as a caption on T1, T3 and T4. Boss messaging at 11 pm fits T2. Tết questions and "con nhà người ta" (the perfect neighbor's kid) fit T3 and T8.
