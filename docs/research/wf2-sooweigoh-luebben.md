## A. Soo Wei Goh ("SooWei Goh")

### 1. Identity and business model
- **Identity confirmed.** His YouTube channel @sooweigoh links to instagram.com/sooweigoh and to his company, Goh Consulting. No other candidate fits.
- **Platforms:**
  - Main YouTube channel: 39.6K subscribers, 334 videos, 2.37M views, joined Sep 2021.
  - Second channel "SooWei Goh Biz" (@sooweigohbiz), which carries the tactical content videos.
  - Threads: about 4K followers. TikTok: 659 followers.
  - Instagram size could not be checked. Viewer comments about two months ago ask "what happened to your IG?", so the account may have had a problem.
- **Background:**
  - Malaysian. Started at 16 with Instagram theme pages.
  - Worked as a DM appointment setter (12-hour days on 5% commission), then closer, sales manager, and "growth operator" scaling high-ticket offers.
  - Founded Goh Consulting while at university in London.
  - Claims $151k/mo at 19 and $408k/mo at 20. In a 2026 podcast he cited about $450k/mo revenue and $310k/mo cash collected, all from organic content until ads were recently added.
- **What he sells:** "Brand Architect," a done-with-you program for coaches and consultants already above $30k/mo. His target client has since moved up to founders making multiple six figures a month. The program installs:
  - positioning
  - a content system (training modules and SOPs, a personalized strategy from his Head of Content, and 1:1 support from his leadership team)
  - sales infrastructure
- **Pricing:** not published. On the podcast he and the host model $30–40k price points, and a viewer comment cites "$20k–50k."
- **His mission:** "build the world's biggest personal branding event."

### 2. Content philosophy
- **Content and revenue are one system.** Content should "sign clients, not just get views." Optimize for buyers, not views.
- **Offer before content.** "Fix your offer before you touch your content… pick one buyer, sell them one thing."
- **Be everywhere.** A daily YouTube video and daily Instagram stories, so that "by the time someone DMs you, they've already decided… you're not selling, you're confirming."
- **Run your brand like a media company,** with an in-house team, SOPs and production workflows.
- **Stop performing, start documenting.** Pathos (connection) beats pure expertise.
- **Speaking only to buyers kills reach.** Content at the top of the funnel has to reach a wider "dream follower," not just the ideal client ("from puddle to ocean").
- **Premium perception is earned visually.** Visual identity, filming setup and consistent editing "subconsciously prove your fulfillment doesn't suck." His advice: "Don't look like a course creator."
- **Volume compounds.** "One video planned = ten made. One reel idea = ten reels."
- **Patience in selling.** He would rather a lead buy late after four months of content than be hard-sold in month one.

### 3. His content system
- **Funnel stages (adapted from Hormozi's funnel):**
  - Top of funnel: reach.
  - Middle of funnel: nurture and trust, which he frames as building "value debt."
  - Bottom of funnel: close, using case studies and proof.
  - Fans have asked him for a dedicated video on middle-of-funnel content.
- **Formats:**
  - Long-form YouTube is the core nurture asset.
  - Instagram Reels and Shorts for reach.
  - Instagram story sequences that pre-sell (the "$10k story strat" and "$151K/mo story strategy").
  - Client case-study interviews (about 35 in his playlist).
  - Reaction and breakdown videos.
  - "Private recording / 1-1 session" leak videos.
  - Day-in-the-life vlogs.
  - Long free courses (a 7-hour "leaked $9.5k course" and a 10-hour "full blueprint").
- **Cadence and batching:** he claims "100+ content pieces with 4 filming sessions" a month. He also teaches "creating endless short-form without filming more," "repackaging your winning content," and pre-production for predictable views.
- **Team:**
  - A Head of Content (a chapter covers how to stop micromanaging them).
  - A long-form editor (about $2k/mo).
  - A thumbnail designer.
  - An underrated "$500 hire."
  - His hiring matrix is "donkeys, camels, stallions."
  - He uses a clipping SOP for cutting long-form into shorts.
- **Tools:** an AI ideation scoring system and a Notion content dashboard. Both are lead magnets.

### 4. Signature frameworks
All of these come from chapter titles and his own posts. The step detail is reconstructed, because the transcripts were blocked.

**a) Admirable → Likeable → Credible (the three stages of a brand people "beg to buy from")**
1. **Admirable (foundation):** define your three unique mechanisms or zone of genius, your "moat."
2. **Likeable (a real person):** specific beats generic. Use "Buyer's Mirror" feedback.
3. **Credible (show results):** proof and results. Avoid the course-creator look.

His action step is to analyze content rather than just consume it.

Template for a coach:
- *Admirable:* "I'm the only [identity] who helps [ICP] get [outcome] using [mechanism 1 / 2 / 3]."
- *Likeable:* "This week I'll show [real moment or struggle] that my ICP recognizes because they say '[their exact words]'."
- *Credible:* "[Client] went from [before metric] to [after metric] in [time] by [mechanism]. The part nobody tells you is [insight]."

**b) Buyer Mirror (a form and SOP)**
- He uses a form to capture how real buyers describe themselves, their problems and their desires, then mirrors that language in content.
- The actual questions are gated behind his opt-in. The template below is my adaptation:
  - What were you struggling with right before you reached out?
  - What had you tried?
  - What almost stopped you from buying?
  - Describe the result in your own words.
- Every hook and script then uses those phrases word for word.

**c) Ideation Scoring System (with AI)**
- He scores ideas before filming. He also argues "your best content ideas have already been done": repackage proven outliers.
- The rubric is gated. Proposed criteria for a coach, scored 1–5 each:
  - buyer relevance
  - curiosity gap
  - proof available
  - prior outlier evidence
  - funnel-stage fit
- Film only ideas scoring above a set threshold.

**d) The four-stage content strategy for $50k+ clients (from the video "my exact $3M content strategy")**
1. Nobody cares yet, so earn attention.
2. Out-earning competitors won't save you, so position.
3. Stop giving regurgitated industry advice, so say something original.
4. Kill the boring interview format, so reinvent case studies.

**e) Brand trinity: ethos, pathos, logos**
Credibility, connection and logic. His emphasis is that pathos (connection) beats expertise.

**f) Value problem versus curiosity problem**
Content that gets no views usually has a curiosity problem, not a value problem. Find your "curiosity lever," package unsexy topics as sexy outcomes, and "stop selling your vehicle" (sell the result, not the method).

**g) His nine steps to add $100k/mo (from a YouTube community post)**
1. Fix the offer.
2. Stop building around your cheapest clients.
3. Sell the high-ticket offer you're scared to sell.
4. Cut "broke" language.
5. Simplify the offer until it can close with a screenshot plus a voice note.
6. Post with intention and fix your visual identity.
7. Be everywhere.
8. Upsell existing clients.
9. Do 10x the volume.

### 5. Hook and script structures
Observable title and hook formulas (lowercase, specific numbers):
- "[result] at [age]… here's how" ("i made $100,400 in 1 day at 20…")
- "i [did inner shift], now i make $[X]" ("i removed my limiting beliefs, now i make $151k a month at 20")
- "if i had to [goal] from scratch/again, this is what i'd do"
- "exactly how i [result] [constraint]" ("…working 4 hours/day")
- "f*ck it… here's how to [result] (private recording)"
- "$5M worth of content knowledge in 31 mins"
- "you're wasting 81% of your time (here's how to fix it)": his most-viewed video at 623K views
- Case-study hooks: "he was posting everyday and nothing clicked… until he did this" and "from $30k to $124k/mo in 30 days… here's how"
- Relatable and entertainment shorts: "pov you made $100k in a day with 0 ads," "closing a $15k deal live," "asian mom vs $2k chair," "graduating for my mom"

He has a chapter on "the script structure that weeds out non-buyers." He also uses explicit filter lines, for example: "if you don't make at least 30k/mo, don't click… it will not help you."

**Example outline: client case-study video**
1. Hook: before/after plus a curiosity gap ("posting daily, nothing clicked, until one change").
2. Who they are and their starting metrics.
3. The wrong belief they held.
4. The named shift or mechanism.
5. Three implementation steps.
6. The result in numbers.
7. Filter line ("this is for coaches already at $X").
8. CTA: watch the next long-form video, or DM a keyword or apply.

**Example outline: reaction / meta-breakdown reel**
1. Show the viral clip.
2. "Here's why it worked" (one principle).
3. "Here's how you'd do it as a coach" (a repeatable template).
4. CTA.

### 6. How content turns into sales
- **The path he described on the Guided Growth podcast (2026):**
  1. Instagram and YouTube content.
  2. Instagram viewers are always pushed to YouTube long-form, through story sequences announcing each new video and a YouTube link in bio.
  3. YouTube videos carry mid-roll CTAs to a free resource.
  4. Setters DM new followers, start a genuine conversation, nurture with videos and resources, and ask "would you want help?" ManyChat is used only for comment-keyword CTAs.
  5. Booking, then a post-booking video, then a data-driven B2B sales call.
- **Numbers he gave on that podcast:** 36 qualified calls a month and a 75% close rate. The host argued that close rate was too high, meaning prices should go up.
- **Disqualifier:** prospects with private Instagram profiles are not taken on.
- **Lead magnets, each behind a 3–7 question qualifying form:** Content Dashboard (Notion), Ideation System, Buyer Mirror SOP, Clipping SOP, and a "$408k/mo Content Playbook."
- **Other conversion assets:**
  - Free live trainings (he cited 1,000+ signups from one CTA).
  - A video sales letter page at gohconsulting.com/training: "Step 1: watch, Step 2: book."
  - An application page.
  - A wall of client wins.
- **Ascension:** "make customers pay you three times," with a $24.2k-per-client lifetime value and an ascension path.

### 7. What to take into Content Machine
- The Buyer Mirror step: capture customer language first, then write every hook in the buyer's words.
- Scoring ideas before scripting, as a simple rubric.
- Tagging every piece by funnel stage (top, middle or bottom), plus the "value debt" idea.
- The Admirable → Likeable → Credible trust ladder. It maps closely to the founder's "domino/series" belief and could become the series-sequencing logic.
- Case-study content as a repeatable converting format.
- Filter lines that repel non-buyers.
- Batch filming (about 4 sessions producing a month of content) and repackaging winners.
- "Offer before content."
- "By the DM they've already decided, you're confirming."

### 8. What does not fit a DIY coach
- In-house media team, setters and closers.
- Premium cinematic production.
- A target client already at $30k+/mo, and $20k+ tickets.
- Income-and-age flex packaging ("$408k/mo at 20"). It is risky for credibility and compliance, and not authentic for most coaches.
- 7–10 hour free courses.
- Ads.

## B. Brian Luebben

### 1. Identity and business model
- **Identity confirmed.** YouTube @brianluebben links to actionacademy.com, and brianluebben.com matches. No competing person was found.
- **He is not a content strategist by trade.** He is a former Fortune 500 sales rep (#8 of 5,079) who left corporate in March 2022 and now runs a business-acquisition and real-estate community. His personal brand is the acquisition engine. He is useful as a practitioner example of a coach whose content builds a paid community.
- **Platforms:**
  - Instagram: about 275–300K followers in his own 2024 statements, "~1M across social," about 5M monthly views. Not verified now.
  - Action Academy podcast: 1,026 episodes, "3M+ downloads."
  - YouTube: 3.14K subscribers, 813 videos.
  - TikTok: 7K followers.
  - Book: *From Passive to Passion*.
- **What he sells:** the Action Academy community, "700+ members." It targets W-2 earners making $100–300k who want $10–30k/mo of cash flow by buying small businesses or commercial real estate.
  - Revenue was $1.4M/yr recurring in 2024. He now claims about $15M/yr across his portfolio of companies.
  - Membership was about $2.5–3k/yr in 2023; a 2024 niche video produced two sales at $16k each.

### 2. Content philosophy
- **Build in public.** "Content isn't 'I bought 100 units.' Content is the journey of building the 100 units." The credit goes to the one in the arena (Roosevelt's "Man in the Arena").
- **Raw and fast.** Phone front camera, auto-captions, posted 90 seconds later, no team. "The more raw, the more transparent, the more people engage."
- **Short-form is for attention only.** "You will not make dollars off short-form." Long-form (podcast, YouTube, book, newsletter) is where trust and money are made.
- **Give everything away and charge for implementation.**
- **Email is the only owned audience.** Social is "borrowed." Instagram reaches about 10% of your followers even when a post is doing well.
- **Vulnerability and underdog stories** (the hero's journey). No "plastic influencer."
- **Followers stay for personality.** "They'll follow you for the education, but they'll stay because of your personality."

### 3. His content system
- **Platform choice: the "2-Year Rule."** Only choose a platform you would post on weekly for two years without earning a dollar. Pick what feels "light, not heavy."
- **Cadence:**
  - 2 short videos a day, cross-posted to Reels, TikTok, Shorts and Facebook.
  - 5 podcast episodes a week: 3 short solo episodes and 2 interviews.
  - 2–3 guest appearances on other podcasts a week.
  - A weekly newsletter.
  - Earlier, 10 shorts cut from each podcast episode, across 3 platforms (30 pieces).
- **Mix:** he rotates entrepreneurship, then travel/lifestyle, then something funny. About two-thirds of his content is not about his niche.
- **Seasons:**
  - First, broad "end-destination" content to grow.
  - Then niche middle-of-funnel content to convert. One niche video got 12K views versus his usual 60K–1M, yet produced 5 leads, 4 calls and 2 sales at $16k.
- **Volume rules:** make 100 videos before hiring anyone, and 1,000 videos before you develop intuition for what works.
- **Clipping:** the founder picks the clips. He says offshore VA clipping "never works"; he spent over $100k learning that.

### 4. Signature frameworks

**a) Enthusiastic Beginner versus Grizzled Veteran**
Choose your stance. A beginner documents the journey, which is likeable and attracts fewer haters. A veteran teaches as an authority.

Template: "My name is [name]. I'm going to [specific goal with number] by [date]. I've never done it before. Follow along."

**b) Avatar: three hopes and three roadblocks**
1. Name one specific person.
2. List their three biggest hopes and dreams.
3. List their three biggest roadblocks.
4. Every piece of content is either the bridge to a goal or the removal of a roadblock.
5. Set the change you want in a follower's life after six months of watching.
6. "Sell to who you were 3 years ago."

**c) The 100-idea grid**
Point A → Point B → list 10 roadblocks between them → 10 ways to beat each one = 100 content ideas. Action Academy's own core roadblocks are capital, confidence, community and clarity.

Template: "How to [beat roadblock N] as a [ICP] without [common fear]."

**d) The Amazon Box**
The box (the format or trend) stays the same; only the contents change. Scroll 10 minutes a day to find about 20 trends. Follow 100 creators in your niche and copy their formats, then put your own story inside.

**e) Bear on a Unicycle ("Trillion-Dollar Venn Diagram")**
Combine 2–3 rare, valuable traits until you are one in a billion. His is travel + financial savvy + humor.

Template: "[Trait 1] + [Trait 2] + [Trait 3] who helps [ICP] [outcome]."

**f) Content funnel**
- Top: short-form, selling the end destination.
- Middle: niche problem content.
- Bottom: long-form niche podcast or YouTube.
- Then email, DM and call.
- "Attract in public, sell in private."
- Grow each channel inside its own medium: be a guest on podcasts to grow a podcast, write in other newsletters to grow a newsletter.

**g) Offer validation**
- He took 100 free 15-minute calls over two months, with no offer, and categorized the problems he heard.
- He then built a course and emailed the same people. That produced $100k in 48 hours.
- The course later became a recurring community.
- He frames the offer with Hormozi's Value Equation.

### 5. Hook and script structures
**His only script, used on every short:**
- Hook, 5–10 seconds.
- Three bullet points, 30–40 seconds ("3 reasons, 3 steps, 5 ways…").
- CTA with an identity line, about 5 seconds: "If you like this, my name is Brian, I quit my job last March. If you're pursuing financial freedom, follow."
- He uses ChatGPT for titles ("write me 50 viral video titles on X").

**His end-destination short:**
- Show the lifestyle or outcome (for example, a hot-air balloon over Mexico).
- One line on how he got there.
- CTA: "comment SELF STORAGE and I'll send the 60-minute episode."

**Example for a coach: build-in-public series**
- Title: "Day 12 of signing 10 clients from content."
- Hook: "30 videos, zero clients. Here's what I'm changing."
- Three bullets: what failed, what I learned, what I'm testing tomorrow.
- CTA: "Follow the series. Comment PLAN for my exact posting plan."

**Example for a coach: roadblock short**
- Hook: "Busy moms don't fail at fitness because of willpower."
- Three bullets: the three real roadblocks and a fix for each.
- CTA: identity line plus follow.

### 6. How content turns into sales
- **Customer journey he has tracked:**
  1. Hears Brian as a guest on another podcast, or sees a reel.
  2. Link in bio (Linktree) leads to his podcast.
  3. Binges 3–4 hours of episodes within 2 days.
  4. Books a 15-minute "freedom call."
- **Supporting mechanics:**
  - A self-promotion ad at the start and end of every episode instead of sponsors.
  - ManyChat comment keywords that send people to episodes.
  - Lead magnets:
    - A 20-step podcast-launch checklist: 42 minutes to make, 412 emails.
    - "W2 to World Travel."
  - ConvertKit newsletter with a 76% open rate.
  - A text-message list.
  - A free live webinar: "7 Steps for High-Earning Professionals to Buy a $1M+ Business in 12 Months."
- **Calls:**
  - 12 minutes of discovery, 3 minutes of pitch.
  - An application filter ("read the book, send us your favorite chapter") screens out about 80%.
  - A 72-hour "kill switch" on offers.
  - Close rate: 61% of offers made, 37% of all calls.
- **Guest-podcast machine:** 20–30 pitches a week to land about 3. Criteria: ListenNotes top 2%, a weekly show, 100+ episodes, and a niche fit.

### 7. What to take into Content Machine
- The Enthusiastic Beginner stance. It directly solves the "I'm not an expert yet" block for non-experts.
- Three hopes, three roadblocks, then the 100-idea grid, as the ideation engine. It is easy to automate with a prompt.
- Hook + 3 bullets + identity CTA as the default short-form script.
- An Amazon-box trend template library.
- Bear-on-a-unicycle positioning.
- The rotation mix of education, personality and fun. It matches the founder's three content types.
- Short-form sells the destination; long-form sells the method.
- Build-in-public "Day N" series. This is his concrete version of the founder's "dominoes" belief.
- 100 free calls as the research method.
- Comment-keyword links into long-form.
- Seasons of content: grow first, then niche down.

### 8. What does not fit a DIY coach
- Five podcast episodes a week plus 2–3 guest spots a week.
- His advice to wait about two years of content before making any offer. That is too slow for a coach who needs revenue.
- Travel and lifestyle content, which requires actually having that life.
- The 72-hour kill switch, which needs strong demand.
- Podcast-first growth; he admits it is the hardest format to grow.

**Frameworks his guests brought, not his own:** Caleb Ralston's three levers (contrarian belief, delivery, "wrapping paper"); Sam Gaudet's Hook, Explain, Illustrate, Teach.
