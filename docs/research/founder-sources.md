# Founder-provided source digests

Digests of the transcripts and channel screenshots the founder shared during planning (Oct 2026). These outrank web research where they conflict. Public creators only; no private individuals.

### Soo Wei Goh (5 transcripts + channel screenshots; the primary reference, outranks web research)
- **4 stages of trust:**
  - Admirable → Likable → Credible → Trustable.
  - "Trust is built in sequences"; most people skip to Credible and it falls flat.
  - Mapping: Attract = Admirable + Likable (TOFU); Trust = Credible (MOFU, belief shift); Convert = Trustable (BOFU, shown proof, objection-killing).
- **ICP vs Dream Follower:**
  - TOFU sits in the overlap of the ICP and a wider dream follower (10–20× the TAM), because dream followers push distribution.
- **Funnel-shaped MOFU script:**
  1. The hook (first ~20%) speaks to dream follower + ICP.
  2. A credibility line.
  3. Niche keywords, so dream followers drop off while the ICP stays.
  4. The belief shift.
  5. The close.
  - 3-question check: does it break a limiting belief? Is the hook broad enough? Is there one clear message?
- **Three hooks per short:** title (on-screen), visual, verbal. Great hooks open loops beyond the topic.
- **Buyer Mirror form:**
  - where did you first find me;
  - the first content that made you take me seriously (→ TOFU);
  - the last content that pushed you to book (→ MOFU);
  - why reach out when you did;
  - how convinced you were before the call;
  - your hesitations;
  - why me over others.
  - Plus "5 whys" with favorite clients.
- **3 reasons to admire you** that only you combine = the unique mechanism. Lean into ONE extreme trait. Show, don't tell.
- **Objection mining:** sales-call transcripts → objections → limiting beliefs → MOFU/BOFU. "An objection on a call = content failed to do its job."
- **Swipe file + remix:** save pieces that trigger strong emotion and 10× outliers; rip the proven format with a twist ("if everyone goes left, go right"). Hard-to-replicate topics are a moat.
- **Formats:**
  - the "I made it" moment;
  - paycheck/spend breakdown remix;
  - client DECISION breakdown (the top converter);
  - one-on-one breakdown (long-form);
  - mastermind/event clips;
  - "what did you do this week that was interesting?";
  - zone-of-genius capture.
- **Tier list for consultants:**
  - S: scripted talking head, storytelling, mastermind/event.
  - A: long-form highlights, client montages.
  - B: transformations, whiteboard, podcast.
  - C: green screen, voice-over B-roll.
  - D: trending audio + caption, street interviews.
- **Credible value:**
  - only-you-can-say-it;
  - "take what's behind paywalls and be first to put it free";
  - borrowed credibility.
  - Packaging and positioning > raw value.
  - Lower the guard: "I have nothing to sell you".
- **Long-form first for high-ticket:** YouTube converts; 70–80% of short-form is clipped from long-form.
- **Media-company loop:** pre-production (past winners, objections, Buyer Mirror → ideas → score TAM/packaging → package → script) → film → post → weekly outlier review. The founder only does ideas + recording.
- **Hub:** a Notion pipeline: idea → validated → scripted → filmed → edited → posted.
- **YouTube packaging:**
  - specific $ + timeframe;
  - "if i had to start over";
  - "avoid these 3 mistakes";
  - "(full breakdown)", "(just do this)";
  - client breakdowns;
  - thumbnail text of 2–4 words that adds to the title.
- **Out of scope for v1:** visual identity, lighting/camera/audio, editing style, team SOPs.

### Nik Setting (4 transcripts + channel screenshots)
- **3 pillars:** Traffic (qualified followers) / Educate (how you think) / Convert. People buy from people.
- **Profile funnel:**
  - reels + comment-keyword posts (22k likes → 6.5k leads) + story sequences ("sell without selling");
  - the bio as a landing page (face; who you help + proof + DM keyword; a link to a YouTube "organic VSL"; a "Start here" highlight) → DMs.
  - Carousels = TOFU; reels = MOFU; long-form = BOFU. Ads only amplify.
- **Reel mix (25% each):** credibility, value (the exact SOP), educational (how I run X), social proof/transformation.
- **Call out the stage above them:**
  - numbers in hooks set who you attract;
  - outcome > mechanism;
  - show proof, never say "with proof".
- **Ad/VSL rules:**
  - hook the PAIN, not the person;
  - "follow to learn more" is dead;
  - odd specificity (11K, not 10K);
  - VSL: hook → pain → solution → small CTA at ~65% → social proof → CTA.
- **Messaging sheet:**
  - 1–3 avatars;
  - a clear, tangible, easiest outcome;
  - your story;
  - 5 reasons to choose you.
  - Clarity = speed to trust.
- **Funnel balance:** TOFU curiosity / MOFU how you think / BOFU hard proof. Missing BOFU → "I don't know if this works".
- **Long-form:** every minute adds value. The Live Consult / hot-seat breakdown is the signature format.
- **Title examples:**
  - "$402K/Mo Business Asked Me How To Hit $1M/Mo (Live Consult)"
  - "How I'd Take This $30K/Mo Barber Coach To $150K/Mo"
  - "I spent $540,000 on my personal brand. Here's what was worth it"
  - "If I Wanted to Scale as a Fitness Coach In 2026, This is What I'd Do"
  - "$10M of Content Knowledge in 22mins"
  - "(Full Masterclass)", "(step-by-step w/ a customer)"
- **36 lessons → operating rules:**
  - write for ONE person; one idea per post; change ONE belief per post ("you think you need X, you actually need Y");
  - boring is the only real mistake; say the quiet truth; document > create;
  - the best content already happened; talk to customers more than cameras; show, don't explain;
  - sell to buyers, not followers; reach without trust is noise; a repeatable format beats a lucky hit;
  - repurpose everything; if you're sick of saying it, they haven't heard it; trends = rented attention;
  - niche until it scares you; lower production, raise clarity; faces > graphics;
  - buyers decide silently; publish before ready; don't check numbers for an hour; don't quit on a bad week;
  - confidence = reps; do less, with intention; post the one that scares you;
  - AI copies output, never intention.
- **Consult lessons for the machine's UX:**
  - speed to trust, speed to execute (a task in the first hour), speed to outcome (a quick win) → a ready-to-film piece in session 1;
  - people pay for accountability + confirmation → the machine approves ideas and holds the coach to weekly rhythm;
  - a Monday AI report = the weekly review;
  - offer options + voice-match.
- **Out of scope for v1:** DM-setting KPIs, setter/closer ops, ad-account structure.

### Alex Hormozi (6 transcripts + channel screenshots)
- **Pillar → distribution:**
  - The main channel holds long pillars (keynotes, whiteboards, live consults).
  - Secondary channels hold 4–12-minute single-idea cut-downs with plain "How I'd…/Why…" titles and 2–4-word concept thumbnails, plus shorts and podcast.
  - Volume negates luck (35,000 pieces in 40 months).
- **SPCL = influence:** Status, Power (say-do correspondence; the most important), Credibility (third-party proof), Likeness. Stack all four.
- **Brand** = the deliberate pairing of you with outcomes the ideal customer likes. Measure it by reach, influence and direction.
- **6 shifts** (RPM +68%, subs/view +25%, opt-ins +26%, book sales 2×):
  - edutainment → education;
  - for us → for the avatar;
  - wide → narrow;
  - views → a revenue-quality metric;
  - shorts → longs (keep wide shorts for recognition + retargeting);
  - assume more → assume nothing: title for strangers, re-introduce yourself, repost greatest hits.
- **Long-form intro:**
  - Proof, Promise, Plan.
  - Packaging goes from vague to clear.
  - Language > razzle.
  - Pre-search > post-production.
- **Trust economy:**
  - show, don't tell; education is the door;
  - instructions must be easy to follow and believable;
  - tell the WHOLE truth, including where you look bad;
  - "show what only you can show, say what only you can say".
- **Content is the targeting.** Judge it by IRL buyer responses, not views. Set view expectations from real market size.
- **QUAL via Musk's algorithm:** question requirements → delete → simplify → accelerate → automate LAST. This applies to the machine itself.
- **Tension resolved:** entertainment viewers rarely become buyers (Hormozi), yet TOFU must hit ICP ∩ dream follower (Goh) → entertainment must pass the Buyer Filter: relatable moments from the buyer's world, never generic humor.
- **Out of scope for v1:** agencies, audience monetization, live-stream ops.

### Matt Gray (7 transcripts + channel screenshots; packaging + hooks)
- **Title formulas:**
  - "how to X (and Y)"; "X in N minutes"; "(in just N steps/days)";
  - "If I Wanted to X In 2026, I'd Do This"; "If You Only Watch One X Video, Make It This";
  - "the N levels of X"; "the psychology of X"; "N X I stole from N billionaires";
  - "how to X without Y"; "X is hard until you build systems like this"; "(do this first)"; "(the COMPLETE X)".
  - Casual lowercase, with a parenthetical payoff.
  - Thumbnail text = 2–4 words that ADD to the title.
- **Long-form hook:**
  1. A contrarian line.
  2. A named structure.
  3. Mirror the pain.
  4. Proof (numbers).
  5. A promise, with an open loop paid off at the end.
  6. "even if you haven't …".
  7. "Let's go".
  - Re-open loops between sections.
- **His system, renamed in our own words:**
  - 4 content categories, 3 signature phrases (= signature keywords), 2 formats, 1 core platform + email;
  - name ≥5 frameworks, plus 3 authority pillars;
  - become the category champion.
- **Signal positioning:** who are you 5 years ahead of? What do people already come to you for? What are you quietly intolerant of (→ the enemy)?
- **Point of view as a campaign:** 3 big ideas, an old way vs new way, and every post ladders up.
- **Monthly ritual:** a voice memo answering 15–25 authenticity questions → transcript → raw-material pack → posts. (= the machine's monthly capture.)
- **Resonant copy:**
  - 10–20 pain signals + 10–20 dream signals written in what the buyer sees, feels and senses.
  - The 5-line story: Mirror → Friction → Realization → Shift → Invitation.
  - The CTA closes the loop the story opened (a contextual lead magnet).
- **Funnel:**
  - traffic → contextual lead magnet → nurture email → apply page → offer;
  - CTA mix ≈ 60% lead magnets / 20% direct offers / 20% DM keywords;
  - profit per piece is the metric.
- **Repurposing:** 1 pillar → reels/TikToks/Shorts/LinkedIn/X/newsletter/podcast. Repurpose the WINNERS, not everything.
- **Carousels:**
  - copy first;
  - 10–12 slides;
  - slide 1 is the hook in big type;
  - one message per slide;
  - the last slide = a contextual lead magnet + one trigger word.
- **LinkedIn:** reposts are algorithm fuel; images whose text differs from the post.
- **5 levels:** Dreamer → Tinkerer → Beautiful Prison → Media Company CEO → Iconic. Usable as a maturity diagnostic.
- **Full sweep** (`wf8`):
  - packaging before production (50–60 titles → 6 → rank);
  - retitle toward the viewer's outcome;
  - odd exact numbers;
  - the parenthetical removes an objection;
  - re-run winners;
  - one contextual lead magnet per piece.
  - **Do NOT copy:** his framework names, inflated value math, or fake scarcity.
