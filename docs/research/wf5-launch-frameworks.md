# Launch Campaign Frameworks Brief (Content Machine 1.0, Launch module)

Labels: **[DATA]** means measured data such as platform datasets, academic work or documented policy. **[PRACTITIONER]** means a guru, vendor or case-study claim, or my own synthesis. "Case" means one creator's numbers, which you can't generalize from.

---

## 1) Key insights

1. **The launch is a sales letter cut into episodes.** Jeff Walker's core idea is to release 3 pieces of prelaunch content (PLC) over **6–10 days**: Opportunity, then Transformation, then Ownership Experience. After that, open the cart for **5–7 days**, email daily, and send **at least 3–4 emails on close day**. He says creators often make **"HALF their sales on the last day."** He also says the content "should be valuable whether or not they actually make a purchase." **[PRACTITIONER, primary source]**
2. **Deadlines concentrate buying, and this is measurable.** Two years of Kickstarter data show a U-shaped pattern: backers contribute most in the first and last weeks, across categories and goal sizes. **[DATA]** The launch should therefore plan its loudest content for opening day and close day.
3. **Time-based scarcity fits coaching.** A meta-analysis (131 studies, 416 effect sizes) found scarcity cues raise purchase intent. Time-based scarcity works best for high-involvement products, supply-based scarcity for experiences, and demand-based scarcity for utilitarian goods. **[DATA]** A $300–$3,000 coaching program is high-involvement and experiential, so real deadlines and real cohort caps are the right levers.
4. **Fake scarcity is common, and regulators treat it as a deceptive pattern.**
   - A crawl of ~11K shopping sites found 1,818 dark-pattern instances, with 183 sites using outright deceptive practices. Countdown timers that reset or outlive their deadline were among them. **[DATA]**
   - The FTC's 2022 dark-patterns report names false scarcity and baseless countdown timers. **[DATA, policy]**
   - The UK CMA investigated misleading countdown and "act fast" claims. **[DATA, policy]**
   - Vietnam's Law 19/2023/QH15 (in force since 1 Jul 2024), Art. 10, bans misleading consumers with false information about products or transaction details. **[DATA, law]** My inference is that a fake "only 99 slots" claim is risky under it. Get legal confirmation.
5. **Comment-to-get posts work, but Meta penalizes "comment bait."**
   - Facebook demotes engagement bait, including comment bait (e.g., "Comment YES if…"). It applies **stricter demotions to Pages that do it repeatedly**. **[DATA, policy]**
   - In June 2024 Instagram clarified that asking for specific words only to game reach hurts recommendation. Earnestly engaging in comments and DMs to "generate leads, or drive conversions" is not bait, and tools like ManyChat are fine. **[DATA, policy statement]**
   - The founder's "Đủ 100 comment 'UP'" threshold-unlock is textbook comment and vote bait on Facebook.
6. **DM automation has hard platform limits.**
   - Private replies: **one message per comment, sent within 7 days of the comment.** **[DATA, Meta docs]**
   - Promotional messages are allowed only inside the **24-hour window** after the user's last interaction. The HUMAN_AGENT tag allows manual replies for up to 7 days. **[DATA, Meta docs]**
   - Automation tools connect to **Pages and IG Business/Creator accounts, not personal FB profiles** (including professional mode). **[PRACTITIONER, ManyChat community]**
   - So you **cannot DM-blast a waitlist on cart-open day** unless each person re-engaged in the last 24h. You need email, Zalo, or a re-engagement post ("comment LINK for the open-cart link").
7. **Lead magnets per Hormozi.** A lead magnet is a complete solution to a narrow problem that reveals the next problem your paid offer solves. There are 3 types: **reveal the problem, samples/trials, one step of a multi-step process**. They are delivered as software, information, services or physical items. The CTA should carry scarcity and urgency. Hormozi cites give:ask ratios of about 3.5:1 on TV and about 4:1 content-to-ads on Facebook. **[PRACTITIONER]**
8. **Belief-shift content is a known sequence.** Brunson's Perfect Webinar and challenges break three false beliefs in order: **vehicle** ("this method won't work"), **internal** ("I can't"), **external** ("no time/money/tech"). Each is broken with an Epiphany Bridge story, followed by **the Stack**. **[PRACTITIONER]** This maps onto the machine's existing belief-shift chain.
9. **Multiple touches convert better.**
   - Amy Porterfield aims for 2–3 free value touches before the pitch. In one launch, **~450 of 1,500+ buyers never registered for a webinar** and bought from email. **[PRACTITIONER, case]**
   - Her cart-open retargeting cost **$5.75 per conversion**, versus $31 for fan-targeted ads to the sales page. **[PRACTITIONER, case]**
10. **Benchmarks are wide, so plan with launch math, not hope.** The Kajabi platform figure is **4.0%** free-to-paid across 36.2M relationships. **[DATA]** Practitioners use about **2% of the list** as a planning assumption, and **5–10% of live attendees** as a webinar target. **[PRACTITIONER]**

## 2) Frameworks and templates

The worked examples use one coach throughout:
- **Coach Minh** helps coaches, trainers, and real-estate and insurance agents launch their first digital product.
- **Offer:** "Make It Launch", a 6-week group cohort, **$997 (≈24.9tr VND)**.
- **Real cap:** 30 seats, because there are 2 live calls a week.
- **Founding bonus:** a 1:1 offer review for the first 10 buyers, a real cap on his time.

### F1. Product Launch Formula (Jeff Walker)

**How it works [PRACTITIONER, primary]:**
- **Pre-prelaunch.** Build buzz with a "sneak peek." Ask "what TWO questions do you have about…" to co-create the offer and mine objections.
- **Prelaunch (sideways sales letter).** Release PLC1–3 over 6–10 days:
  - PLC1, Opportunity: your story, the problem, and the discovery.
  - PLC2, Transformation: case studies, and one aspect taught in depth.
  - PLC3, Ownership Experience: hint at the offer and show what mastery feels like.
  - Summaries also describe the rhythm as **problem → solution → next problem** (a cliffhanger) and label the PLCs Why/What/How.
- **Open cart.** 5–7 days, emailing daily. Summaries give this pattern **[PRACTITIONER, secondary]**:
  - Day 1: 2 emails ("doors open")
  - Day 2: case study
  - Day 3: FAQ
  - Day 4: "closes tomorrow" plus a bonus
  - Close day: 3–4 emails
- **Post-launch.** Deliver more than promised and follow up with buyers and non-buyers.

**Launch types:**
- **Seed:** no product yet. Gather a beta group from a micro-list of about 30–100 people, get paid, and build the content live.
- **Internal:** your own list.
- **JV:** partners' lists. Advanced, and not for a first launch.
- **Evergreen:** only after a live launch has proven the offer.

**Mental triggers** (from summaries): authority, reciprocity, trust, anticipation, likability, events/rituals, community, scarcity, social proof.

**Fill-in template:**
- Pre-prelaunch: "I'm building something for [avatar] who want [result] without [pain]. What are your 2 biggest questions about [topic]?"
- PLC1: "[Story when struggle was worst] → [the discovery] → the opportunity is [new vehicle] → next problem: [cliffhanger]."
- PLC2: "Here's step [X] in full: [teach]. [Student] went from [before] to [after]. → Next problem: [cliffhanger]."
- PLC3: "Imagine [a week inside/after program]. [Walkthrough]. Doors open [date/time]; [real cap/bonus]."
- Open cart: "[Offer] is open until [date/time, timezone]. For [who]. You get [stack]. [Guarantee]. [Real bonus] ends [time]."

**Worked example:**
- **Pre-prelaunch:** "I'm turning my framework into a cohort. What stops you from launching your first course?"
- **PLC1:** "How I sold 571tr in week 1 without a big list." The vehicle is a launch, not a funnel. The cliffhanger: "but most people's product idea is wrong — tomorrow."
- **PLC2:** The 3-promise framework taught in full ("Đổ não ra giấy / Trải chữ lên kênh / Nhét tiền vào túi"), plus a student case.
- **PLC3:** "A day inside week 3."
- **Cart:** opens Thursday 8pm and closes the next Wednesday at midnight.

### F2. Brunson: Big Domino, Epiphany Bridge, 3 false beliefs, Stack (Perfect Webinar and challenges)

**How it works [PRACTITIONER]:**
- **Big Domino.** One belief that, once someone holds it, makes the other objections irrelevant: "If I can make them believe [new opportunity] is key to [desire] and only available via [my framework]…"
- **Epiphany Bridge.** Introduce the moment the struggle was worst. Then the journey and conflict, the new opportunity (the epiphany), and the framework at a high level ("what, not how"). End with successes. The principle: emotion drives the buying decision and logic justifies it afterward.
- **Perfect Webinar (ClickFunnels version, about 90 min):**
  - Origin story, 15 min
  - Vehicle, 15 min
  - Internal beliefs, 15 min
  - External beliefs, 15 min
  - Stack and close, 30 min
- **ClickFunnels benchmarks [PRACTITIONER, vendor]:** show-up around 25%; 5% of attendees buying is "good," 10% is "scalable."
- **Challenge funnel.** Opt-in page, then an optional VIP order form (live Q&A, replays), then daily lesson pages, then the core offer at the end. Lengths of 5, 7 or 10 days are all used.

**Fill-in template (belief-shift post):** "I used to believe [false belief]. Then [specific event]. What I realized: [new belief]. Proof: [result]. So [implication for reader]."
- Run it three times: Vehicle ("[method] doesn't work in VN/my niche"), Internal ("I'm not expert enough"), External ("no time / no list / no tech").

**Worked example:**
- **Vehicle:** "Launches are only for big names." Counter-story: a 50-person micro-list seed launch that sold 8 seats.
- **Internal:** "I'm not an expert." Counter: you only need to be 2 steps ahead.
- **External:** "No time." Counter: the program needs 2h/week and is built with AI skills.

### F3. Soap Opera Sequence for follow-up after the lead magnet (Brunson)

**How it works [PRACTITIONER]:** 5 emails or DMs over 5 days:
1. Set the stage: who I am and what's coming.
2. High drama, backstory, the wall. The lead magnet goes in the P.S.
3. The epiphany and "the one thing."
4. Hidden benefits and the first offer.
5. Urgency CTA.

**Use it for:** the email, Zalo, or DM nurture for everyone who grabbed the bait post's lead magnet. Run it before cart open, or as an evergreen mini-launch.

### F4. Hormozi: lead magnets, give-give-give-ask, and the scarcity/urgency toolkit

**Lead magnet [PRACTITIONER]:**
- Types: reveal problem, sample/trial, one step of the process.
- Steps (from notes): pick a narrow problem and who it's for, choose the type and delivery, test name and headline, make it easy to consume, make it good enough to pay for, and end with a clear CTA ("what to do + why now").

**Give:ask:** the book cites TV at about 3.5:1 and Facebook at about 4 content posts per ad. "Give, give, give… until they ask" echoes Gary Vaynerchuk's "Jab, Jab, Jab, Right Hook."

**From $100M Offers:**
- Scarcity: limited seats/slots, limited bonuses, never available again.
- Urgency: rolling cohorts, rolling seasonal promos, bonus/price deadlines, exploding opportunity.
- Rule: never discount the core offer; add bonuses instead.

**Launch example: the $100M Money Models launch (16 Aug 2025).** Third-party breakdown of self-reported numbers **[PRACTITIONER]**:
- Free multi-day livestream as the attraction offer, with pre-orders sold beforehand.
- During the live event only, a bundle **capped at 25,000** units that **expired when the stream ended**.
- Pattern to copy: give heavily in public, then an ask with a real cap and a real time window.

**Fill-in templates (bait post):**
- **Reveal problem:** "Most [avatar] lose [X] because of [hidden problem]. I built a [quiz/audit/checker] that shows yours in 3 min. Comment [KEYWORD] and it lands in your inbox."
- **One step:** "This is step 1 of my [N]-step [system]: [tool/template]. Comment [KEYWORD]."
- **Sample:** "[N] free [audits/reviews] this week; I personally do each one, so it's capped at [N]."

**Worked example (fixing the founder's examples):**
- "Skill… không bán, chỉ tặng cho 99 slot c.m đầu tiên" is only honest if the cap is enforced. Make the scarce part real: "99 bạn đầu nhận kèm 1 buổi setup 15 phút với mình; sau đó vẫn nhận skill nhưng không kèm setup."
- "Đủ 100 comment 'UP' làm series casestudy" becomes: "Mình đang viết series 5 phần mổ xẻ case 571tr. Comment 'CASE' để nhận từng phần qua inbox khi ra mắt." This is a lead-gen DM, not a threshold, and you publish the series either way.

### F5. Challenge launch (5-day)

**How it works [PRACTITIONER, cases]:**
- **Denise Duffield-Thomas (~$3k course):**
  - 10 days promotion, a 5-day challenge (FB Lives), a masterclass/sales webinar, then 7 days of open cart.
  - 16k leads (6.5k new); ~AUD 6.25 ad cost per registrant; sales page converted 6%+; overall conversion 2–3.5%.
- **Victory Points Social:**
  - $27 paid bootcamp via click-to-Messenger ads and $5k ad spend.
  - 215 registrants, ~25% daily show-up, 32 high-ticket ($3,588) enrollments, $127k revenue.
  - A Messenger bot kept the 24h window open each day.
- **Paid $17 challenge to a $57/mo membership:**
  - 43% of paid attendees converted; pitch on Day 1 with a **diminishing bonus stack**.
  - Public cart open Friday to Monday.

**Template (day jobs):**
1. Quick win
2. Break the vehicle belief
3. Break the internal belief, plus a case study
4. Break the external belief, plus an ownership preview
5. Implementation, then the live Stack and open cart

Optional: a VIP tier ($7–$47) for replays and Q&A, which also gives you a buyer list.

**Worked example:**
- Coach Minh's "5 ngày ra mắt sản phẩm đầu tay" challenge, run in a FB Group with daily Lives.
- Day 5 Live pitches Make It Launch.
- Bonus stack shrinks each day the cart is open; each bonus's expiry date is stated in advance and kept.

### F6. Waitlist and founding-member launch (Stu McLaren) and seed launch (Walker)

**How it works [PRACTITIONER]:**
- **Stu McLaren:** communicate the idea, cast the vision, extend an invitation, give a CTA, follow up. Launch before building, to **~200+ people**.
- **Founding members:** a lower price locked for life, in exchange for co-creating and giving feedback.
- **Vendor claims:** a waitlist converts 3–5× better than cold, and 20–35% in launch week. **[PRACTITIONER, vendor blog, low confidence]**

**Template:** "I'm opening [N] founding seats for [program] on [date]. Founding members get [price lock/bonus] because you'll help shape it. Join the waitlist: [comment KEYWORD / link]. Waitlist gets 24h early access."

**Worked example:** 15 founding seats at $497 (later $997). Waitlist via comment "FOUNDING". Early access by email and Zalo, because the DM window will have expired by cart open.

### F7. Evergreen mini-launches

**How it works [PRACTITIONER]:**
- Rolling cohorts: "next group starts Monday."
- Seasonal or quarterly 3–5-day flash windows.
- Per-subscriber real deadlines that don't reset (Deadline Funnel style).
- Tripwire on the lead magnet thank-you page.
- Walker's advice: go evergreen only after a live launch has worked.

**Template:** "[Cohort name] starts [date]. Enrollment closes [date] or when [N] seats fill. Next cohort: [date]."

### F8. Comment-keyword to DM launches (IG/FB)

**How it works:**
- Keyword comment triggers a private reply with the asset, then a question that invites a reply (which reopens the 24h window), then a tag (warm, attended, waitlist), then capture email or Zalo. **[DATA: Meta limits; PRACTITIONER: flow design]**
- Vendor data:
  - HypeAuditor × ManyChat (6,000 US creators, correlational): creators using DM automation saw 40–150% more comments per post and 30–140% faster growth. **[DATA, vendor, correlational]**
  - One retailer's comment-to-lead rate went from 2% to 8%. **[PRACTITIONER, vendor case]**
  - "80–100% DM open rates" is an unverified vendor claim.

**Compliance template:**
- Put real value in the post itself.
- Make the CTA a lead-gen action ("comment CASE and I'll send you the breakdown").
- Avoid vote, react, tag and "if we hit 100 comments" mechanics.
- Vary keywords.
- Answer some comments by hand.
- The VN habit of writing "c.m / q.t / chấm" to dodge the classifier is folklore. There's no public evidence it works, and Meta says its classifier was trained on hundreds of thousands of posts.

### F9. Retargeting engagers on small budgets (Meta)

**How it works [DATA, Meta docs/glossary]:**

| Audience source | Options | Max retention |
|---|---|---|
| Video viewers | 3s, 10s, ThruPlay, 25/50/75/95% | 365 days |
| IG Business profile engagers | engaged, messaged, profile visit, saved | 730 days |
| FB Page engagers | engaged, messaged | 730 days |
| Lead forms | opened, opened-not-submitted, submitted | 90 days |

- Minimum audience size is about 100 people. **[PRACTITIONER, repeated by vendors]**
- **Advantage+ audience treats custom audiences as suggestions and will expand beyond them.** For strict retargeting:
  - Use original audiences or set the custom audience as a control ("Advantage+ off").
  - Exclude buyers.
  - Detailed-targeting exclusions were removed in 2025, so use custom-audience exclusions instead.
  - Sources: Jon Loomer, Novoads. **[PRACTITIONER]**
- Loomer's rule of thumb: the smaller the budget, the larger the share that goes to remarketing. Watch frequency on small pools. **[PRACTITIONER]**

**Template by phase:**

| Phase | Who to target | Creative |
|---|---|---|
| Prelaunch | 50%+ video viewers, 30–60-day engagers | PLC content and the lead magnet |
| Cart open | Engagers, lead-form openers, waitlist | Offer and a testimonial |
| Bonus deadline | Same audiences | "Bonus ends tonight" |
| Close day | Same audiences | "Closes at midnight" |

Exclude buyers throughout.

**Worked example (Coach Minh):**
- A ~3,000-person warm pool.
- $5–10/day in prelaunch, rising to $15–20/day on open and close days. This budget split is my own synthesis, labeled **[PRACTITIONER]**.

### F10. Real vs fake urgency and scarcity

| Real (use) | Fake (block) |
|---|---|
| Cohort cap tied to delivery capacity (live calls, 1:1 time) | "Only 3 left" when the supply is unlimited |
| Hard cart close: link off, price up, stated in advance | Reopening "by popular demand" right after close, as a routine |
| Bonus expiry tied to real cost (your time) | Countdown timers that reset or outlive the deadline |
| Price increase you actually make | "Last chance" repeated every week |
| Founding price lock for the first cohort | Fake "X people viewing" or fabricated buyer counts |
| Per-person evergreen deadline that's actually enforced | "First 99 only" with no enforcement |

### F11. Post-launch

Do these in order **[PRACTITIONER]**:
- Post a "doors closed" thank-you and keep the close (no sneaky reopen).
- Onboard buyers and get the first win fast.
- Capture testimonials at week 2–4 for the next launch, with permission.
- Survey non-buyers about a week later. One creator's post-pitch survey flow converted 8% of respondents. **[PRACTITIONER, case]**
- Offer non-buyers a downsell (self-study version or payment plan), with its own real deadline.
- Send replays to people who missed the live sessions.
- Open the waitlist for the next cohort.
- Run a debrief comparing the KPIs below.

## 3) Implications for the Content Machine Launch module

1. **Add a "Launch Planner" skill whose inputs are mandatory, not optional:**
   - Offer and price
   - **Real capacity** (seats, 1:1 hours)
   - Hard close date and time
   - Bonuses with real expiry
   - Launch type: seed, internal, challenge, waitlist/founding, or evergreen mini
   - Audience size per channel
   - Account type: FB profile vs Page, IG Business/Creator, email, Zalo
   - Retargeting budget: $0 or a small amount
2. **Launch math calculator.** Revenue goal ÷ price ÷ conversion assumption (DATA/PRACTITIONER ranges above) gives the leads and registrants needed. If the numbers don't work, recommend a seed or founding launch.
3. **Stage library for the 8 stages.** Each stage gets:
   - A post formula and hook bank in English and Vietnamese, including text-on-background variants like the founder's examples.
   - CTA variants and a KPI.
   - Belief-shift posts reuse the always-on domino chain's vehicle/internal/external beliefs, so the always-on series feeds the launch.
4. **Calendar generator for 7, 14 and 21 days** using the tables above. It should batch-produce posts, Stories, Live outlines, emails, Zalo and DM scripts for a DIY creator with 1–2h/week, or produce a VA checklist.
5. **Sequence writers:**
   - SOS 5-part nurture after opt-in.
   - Open-cart sequence: 5–7 days, 2 emails on Day 1, 3–4 on close day.
   - DM flows that end with a question, so replies reopen the 24h window.
   - A "reply YES for early access" step before cart open.
6. **Platform-rules guardrails, baked in:**
   - Private reply: 1 message, within 7 days.
   - 24h promo window; HUMAN_AGENT allows manual replies up to 7 days.
   - Automation works on Pages and IG only, not personal profiles. Personal-profile coaches need a manual-DM SOP or must route to a Page, Group or Zalo.
   - Messenger Marketing Messages replaced recurring notifications in early 2026 with tighter limits. One third-party guide says they're region-limited (EU/UK/JP/KR/AU); this is unverified for Vietnam.
   - Zalo (~78–83M MAU per Wikipedia/press) should be the default VN follow-up channel alongside email.
7. **Engagement-bait linter:**
   - Flag vote, react, tag and share bait, and "if we hit N comments" thresholds.
   - Rewrite them into lead-gen keyword CTAs with in-post value.
   - Warn that Pages repeatedly using bait get stricter demotions.
   - Don't teach the "c.m / q.t" evasion as a tactic.
8. **Real-scarcity checker.** Before any urgency or scarcity line is generated, ask:
   - Is the cap enforced?
   - Does the deadline actually turn off the link or raise the price?
   - Would you reopen?

   Block fake timers, fake counts and unenforced "first 99" claims. Cite FTC, CMA and VN Law 19/2023 Art. 10, and recommend local legal review.
9. **Proof and claims gate.** Figures like "571.000.000 in 1 week" need substantiation and student permission. Add a testimonial-capture SOP at week 2–4.
10. **Retargeting brief generator** (optional ads):
    - Audiences per phase with retention windows; buyers excluded.
    - "Advantage+ off / audience controls" instructions.
    - Creative per phase and a frequency watch.
    - Budget-tier presets: $0, $5–10/day, $15–30/day.
11. **Post-launch kit:** debrief dashboard with the KPIs above, non-buyer survey, downsell, replay, next-cohort waitlist, and a cadence recommendation (2–4 big launches a year plus monthly or quarterly mini-launches, with always-on in between).
12. **Bilingual labels.** Name the stages in both languages (Mồi / Shift niềm tin / Trao giá trị / Educate / Mở bán / Retargeting / Urgency & Scarcity / Đóng & Hậu launch) so EN and VN editions share one structure.
