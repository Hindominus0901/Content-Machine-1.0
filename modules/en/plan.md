### The week's plan (Talk weeks; Week 1: after FILM TODAY, a piece per "next"; topics: content pillars + your expertise, facts only theirs)
1 Week n = weeks since plan_start, mod 4, +1; it leads with step n: 1 the real problem and its cause · 2 the better way, their method · 3 proof, "I can, even though…" · 4 all three + the offer. Content pillars rotate.
2 7 posts a week, one a day, Week 1 too (Day 0's piece is day 1), cut from all the week's material (§CM-REPURPOSE 1). Their hours set how many are filmed; the rest are written: Light (unheard or under 2 h): 2 filmed + 5 written · Lean 2–3 h: 3 + 4 · Standard over 3 h: 4 edited + 3 (§CM-CALENDAR 2). ≥4 written: one long post 500–700. Thin: §CM-HUMANIZE 7; fewer filmed: one plain line, in Needs you or NEXT (§CM-REPURPOSE 6). Text-first (LinkedIn too): filmed ones but one become posts. List 300+: email asks for replies; else a message to 3 buyers (§CM-MESSAGES 4). Never a zero day.
3 Each piece passes FOCUS (ship check 0) · one material, several formats: each its own angle, hook, ask (ledger: item 10) · line 1 fills a §CM-FORMATS 1 frame, any format · one framework fitting tier and format (§CM-COPY) · ≥1 bank item (§CM-BANKS) · NOT NOW never the hook or main idea.
4 Keywords: §CM-CTA-KIT 1. The Season keyword's spot rotates: on-screen, payoff, caption, long-post opening.
5 WHY line, stored with every piece, printed only on "{{t:cmd.why}}": {{t:why.prefix}}: "{old belief, their words}" → "{new belief}" · next: {the step it leads to}.
6 Ask by tier (§CM-CTA-KIT 1): ATTRACT → save, send ("{{t:series.part2_tomorrow}}") · TRUST → the comment word (quiet: message me) · CONVERT → week 2+ (week 1: asks a reply, no offer), only with proof (an OK'd result; none: item 9), ≤1 a week (weeks 3-4: 2). ≥3 gives per ask.
7 The mix (default 40/40/20 ≈ 3/3/1 a week): ATTRACT = reach + relate, TRUST = teach + proof, CONVERT = offer, objection, decision, ask. Character, fun ≤20%, with a trait or belief. ≥4 formats; no format 3 in a row; repeats: §CM-HUMANIZE.
8 A liked shape: ≤1 slot a week (Standard 2), the coach's topic and story.
9 No proof yet: proof slots tell their process or a founding offer, unexplained; + "ask 3 past clients" (§CM-MESSAGES).
10 Print each piece under "N{n} · {day} · {format} · ATTRACT|TRUST|CONVERT · {n} words" + its copy box; under it only §CM-EDGE's lines. A chat table, ≤4 columns: Day · Format · Idea, hook · Ask; the full one to the file and hub (§CM-CALENDAR). Idea column = ledger: ≤3 posts an idea, asides count; the keyword rides more, a new idea each time; the card's client profile is the audience, not an idea (§CM-REPURPOSE 10).

2 A new result: proof check per §CM-GUARDRAILS. Then the message check, 3 lines: pieces and buyer replies per big idea, the keyword said back, the best piece. Recommend KEEP (default): same message, big ideas and keyword; new angles, new proof. Or sharpen ONE line with a phrase buyers said back. "{{t:month.decide}}" Their answer already says keep → step 4. Never "lock" or "90 days".
3 A parked topic joins only with 3+ buyer mentions in 30 days, a changed offer or a launch: as a new angle inside a pillar (one big idea each); no new pillar mid-season, never a 6th. Keyword: kept unless unsaid 60+ days while an alternate was said 2+ times.

New slot ≤20%: their own best piece in a new form, else a backed YOU CAN SAY, else a saved liked shape. Then Brand Card v{n+1} (§CM-CARD): its top reprints the Map and the voice line (§CM-VOICE).

### Your angle (optional; offer once: "{{t:angle.offer}}")
{{t:angle.tag}}
EVERYONE SAYS: only a claim seen in 2+ accounts; account names OK, never commenters or counts.
NOBODY SAYS: a buyer need from 2+ people in 2+ places (comments, DMs, the coach's notes); less → label it "{{t:angle.hunch}}" and end the next post with a question to test it.
