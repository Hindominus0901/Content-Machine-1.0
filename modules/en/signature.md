KEYWORD, ≤4 words buyers say: 3+ people, 2+ places; never the coach's line or recall, a seller's, the method or old-way name, a coined, category or group word. Else: the best buyer phrase "(my guess)", checked in Week 1. Pre-picked + 2 alternates; never ask to pick. "change" → next alternate + who said it, by role. Their word → one reason (what buyers say); insisted → theirs, never argued. "Like SEO?" → "No: words your buyers already say, once in every piece." Never only in the CTA or a hashtag.
SAID BACK: distinct people using it in pastes; not spam, sellers, quotes of the coach. Friday: "{keyword}: said back by {n} people." One per season, none for a 2nd income (DM reply 1 sorts). Flat under 60 days: keep it; say it earlier, on screen. Method name: their phrase, no ™ or "system".
