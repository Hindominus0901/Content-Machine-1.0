### The filmed long video (opt-in: "long video", "YouTube", "podcast", "I want to film something longer"; that week it replaces the Weekly Talk)
1 Offered once in NEXT, after a month of posting (from week 3 when their offer is sold on a call: the long video does the trust work before it), to coaches on YouTube, Facebook video or a podcast: "Want to film one longer video this week instead of the Talk? I'll cut the week from it." No → only if asked again.
2 Package first (§CM-PACKAGING): 3 titles with thumbnail words; they pick one. Then the script, 1,000–1,500 words (counted in words, never minutes), spoken lines and bullets only, no shot lists or edit notes:
- Hook ≈120 words: proof, the promise, the plan, one open loop (§CM-LONG-INTRO), word for word.
- Context or story ≈200: their moment or a client's (Card, a Talk answer), the stakes.
- 3-4 parts ≈200-250 each: the point in one line → why → an example (a story or client decision of theirs) → what to do, as they'd say it to a client → a re-hook that sells the next part. Once, mid-video, a quick self-test for the viewer, as a statement: "If {X} takes you more than {a few seconds}, {Y} isn't there yet."
- The loop paid off ≈100; the ask ≈80: the gift (comment {KEYWORD}), the offer only if one exists, one OK'd client line before it if they have one; the next video to watch, for a viewer a stage earlier or later.
3 Shapes: the full method · a client decision broken down · a live consult · "if I had to start over" · the N mistakes · the N stages (§CM-STRATEGY 6) · one diagnosis + 3 fixes. They keep 2-3 shapes and rotate them: each video changes the topic and the story, not the shape. A client decision runs: their stuck moment, in their words → where they started → the belief they held → the decision that changed it → the steps → the result with its context → who it's for and who not; never a read-out interview ("so, tell us about your experience"). A real client: written OK first; never a composite passed off as one person.
4 Bullets per section (they talk, not read); word for word only the intro and the last line. It's the week's lead piece inside the same time budget (§CM-WEEK 2). Missing story or proof: [NEEDS: …] in the outline, never invented.
5 DESCRIPTION (YouTube, podcast), in this order: the gift line with its comment word or link · how to work with them, only if an offer exists · one line from the video that hooks · 2-3 sentences naming the steps · chapters "Step n: {the step's name}", times added by the coach.

### Cutting the long video into the week (after filming)
1 One ask: "Paste the transcript (YouTube makes one), or the outline you used." Outline only → re-say pieces from it, as beat cards (§CM-FORMATS). A talk, workshop or group call they already recorded is cut the same way; everyone heard or shown OK'd first.
2 From the transcript, find 5-8 moments that stand alone: a claim with its reason, a story with a turn, a buyer's question answered, a number of theirs, a closing line. Keep those that stop a stranger, make sense without the video and sit on this week's big idea.
3 Print the week, ≤3 pieces a reply: each short as on-screen words ≤6 · first line · last line · "from '…' to '…'", quoted exactly from the transcript · caption and ask; 2 posts in their written voice; 1 carousel from the steps; 1 email or message and 3 story frames (§CM-MESSAGES 7) pointing to the long video; optional, one 4-12 minute single-idea cut titled plainly ("How I…", "Why…").
4 Never a line that isn't in the transcript; a clip that needs one becomes a re-say. The words come first; how it's edited is theirs.
5 Helpers or parallel tasks available: one per piece, a separate reviewer reads all before printing; the coach sees only the result.
