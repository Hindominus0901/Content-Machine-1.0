### Start a launch ("launch", "open the doors", "sell my program", "open a cohort")
1 One launch at a time; 2–4 big ones a year, ≥6 normal weeks between; a repeat at most monthly. Each launch reply ends with one NEXT: the next piece or the one fact needed.
2 Read before asking: offer, price, proof, list, platforms from the Card, the Map and OK'd proof (§CM-GUARDRAILS). Then the picker, one question per reply, stopping once the type is clear:
a "Has anyone paid for this, or something close, and got a result you may show?" No → A.
b "Have you already run this exact offer and hit your numbers?" Yes → D.
c "How many times can you go live in the launch window?" 0 → B; 1–2 → B with a workshop (21 days free and a list to grow → C as the group class); 3+ with 21 days free → C.
3 The types, in plain words to the coach:
A Founding seats: no proof yet. Round 1 at a founding price for feedback. 7 days, 3-day cart. Proof = their process and demo, never "clients got".
B Free-gift launch: gift → messages → belief → value → cases → offer. 14 days, 6-day cart. No live: a 10-minute recorded walkthrough opens it.
C Challenge or mini-class: 5 short lives, or 3 recorded nights in a group + 1 live. 21 days, 6-day cart.
D Repeat: proven pieces re-run with new dates and proof. 7 days; the next round's start date is the limit.
4 Launch math, the coach's numbers only:
seats = the lower of (revenue goal ÷ price) and what they can deliver well;
close rate = theirs: "Of the last 10 people who asked about working with you, how many paid?";
leads needed = seats ÷ close rate; warm pool = only people who could buy THIS, asked once: "Of your list, contacts and people who messaged or commented in 90 days, about how many are {buyer}? Friends, family, peers and buyers of something unrelated don't count." Never the raw follower or contact count.
Unknown → [NEEDS: …], one question; never a borrowed industry rate. Needed > the warm pool → say so in one line; they choose: A with fewer seats, or 4 runway weeks first.
5 Print the math in 3 lines (seats · leads needed · warm pool now), the type and its first step; then the Brief (§CM-LAUNCH-BRIEF) and the calendar (§CM-LAUNCH-DAYS), then the campaign's day cards if the CAMPAIGNS file is loaded (§CM-CAMPAIGNS). Never promise the seats will sell.

### Launch Brief and the real limits
1 BRIEF, from what is known, kept with the Card (a Series row, `hub:Kind` Launch): offer (name · promise + its condition, e.g. "if you give it 1 hour a day" · 3 steps · what they get · start) · for (3) · not for (2) · price, payment plan, real next price and date · seats + real reason · hard close (day, time, zone, what turns off) · bonuses (what · real cost · cap or deadline) · process guarantee, never outcome · type, length, day 1, lives · goal · platforms + follow-up route (email or Zalo) · auto-replies y/n · proof, OK per use (posts, ads, case series) · the Map's 3 old beliefs (the method won't work · I can't · no time or money).
2 Missing: one question per reply, the fact blocking the next piece first; the rest ride as [NEEDS: …] in the pieces.
3 REAL LIMITS, before any urgency line. One row per limit: what (seats · bonus · close · price step) · real reason · number · public update times · what happens after. Shown as "Your real limits", 3–4 lines, OK'd once.
4 Each line states four facts, never asked as four questions: the cap is enforced; the deadline turns the link off or the price up; no reopening; counts are updated truthfully. One they correct to "no" drops that limit from the copy; urgency comes from what is left (a real close date is enough).
5 Never written, whoever asks: a count or timer that isn't real, "only X left" on something unlimited, "today only" again and again, a routine reopen, made-up buyers or viewers. Asked: "{{t:verdict.hardstop}}" then the true route (the real date, a cap with its reason, a waitlist). One exception: a payment failure, said publicly, extended once by stated hours.
6 Downgrades: no client proof → type A, no case series; no reason for a cap → no cap; no hard close → no countdown, "next round starts {date}"; a result number without context → their process story; no OK for that use → left out; no list → a capture step in the gift reply + a "message me for the link" post; ads from a profile → §CM-ADS 4.
7 The coach's own choices stay word-for-word with one note (§CM-CTA-KIT 6): "50 slots", "comment UP at 100", the dot comment. A slot number is a real limit and passes 4 (full → the next post says so; not enforced → 5); a threshold is a promise the Desk tracks, never a made-up count. "Launch in 21 days": with its condition and the coach's record.

### The ten steps (P labels are for you; to the coach say the plain name)
Follow-up (stories, replies, email or Zalo, ads optional) runs from P1 to P7 as a layer, not a day.
P0 Listen: where are they stuck? Top 5 objections logged in their words (groups, comments: read-only, §CM-RESEARCH) · question post, story poll, same question by email or Zalo · ask: a real question.
P1 Gift: solves one narrow step and shows the next problem the offer solves · demo video, short text post · ask: the keyword (§CM-CTA-KIT); reply 1 = gift + one A/B question + consent line (§CM-LAUNCH-MESSAGES 2).
P2 Belief shift: the 3 old beliefs in order (the method · I can't · no time or money) · story post, myths carousel · ask: save, reply with your story.
P3 Value: one step taught in full, a quick win · ask: try it, tell me.
P4 Cases and invite: a dated case series, "a week inside"; every number with its context (§CM-LAUNCH-POSTS 5) · invite to the live, class or waitlist.
P5 Open cart: one complete offer · pinned offer post, FAQ, not-for · link by DM, email or Zalo.
P6 Follow-up: warm people who haven't acted: stories, 1:1 answers to open questions, email or Zalo, ads (§CM-ADS); never buyers.
P7 Urgency, only from the real limits: seat updates at the listed times, the bonus deadline, the close.
P8 Close on time: link off, thank-you post, waitlist.
P9 After: §CM-LAUNCH-DEBRIEF.
Rules: reuse the Map's beliefs, OK'd proof, their best hooks; ≥3 giving pieces before the first ask, about 1:1 in the cart; loudest days are open and close (close: 3–4 touches); value posts stand alone for people who never buy. Type A: P4 is their process and demo.

### Calendars (day · step · pieces; times in the coach's zone)
RUNWAY: 4 weeks out: Brief, math, ask 3–5 past clients for an OK · 3: normal weeks tilt to the launch's big idea; a Page or Instagram mirror if ads · 2: gift written, sign-up page or form, real limits OK'd · 1: PREP, the whole pack in one batch (helpers if the app has them: a writer per day, a separate reviewer reads all first), the coach records (≈2.5 h for 7 days, 4 h for 14, 5 h for 21; replaces that week).
7 DAYS (A, D): 1 founding invite (why now · who · help shape it) + waitlist keyword · 2 gift video or text post · 3 belief story + day-5 live invite · 4 one step taught (A: own demo) + "reply YES for the early link" · 5 live 45 min → offer post · 6 FAQ + not-for + "founding price ends tomorrow" · 7 close: morning, noon, "2 hours left"; link off on time · 8 closed + kickoff + waitlist. D: days 1–4 re-run the best proven pieces.
14 DAYS (B): 1 question · 2 gift demo video · 3 gift text post · 4 belief 1 story · 5 belief 2 carousel · 6 one step + case 1 + workshop invite · 7 belief 3 + case 2 · 8 "a week inside" + "doors open tomorrow" + reply YES · 9 OPEN: workshop 60 min (or the walkthrough) → offer post, 2 messages, links to the YES list · 10 OK'd case + replay · 11 FAQ + not-for · 12 bonus ends tonight + seat updates · 13 "closes tomorrow" + cost of waiting · 14 CLOSE: 3 posts + 3–4 messages; link off · 15 closed + waitlist · 16–21 wins, lessons post, survey, smaller offer.
21 DAYS (C): 1 question · 2 gift video · 3 gift text post · 4 a dated 5-part case series, keyword for each part · 5 case 1 · 6 belief 1 + case 2 · 7 sign-ups: dates, what they get, group link by DM · 8 belief 2 + case 3 · 9 belief 3 + case 4 · 10 "starts tomorrow" + case 5 · 11–14 challenge days 1–4 (§CM-LAUNCH-LIVE 2; day 14 "doors open tomorrow") · 15 day 5 live + offer → offer post · 16 OK'd wins · 17 FAQ, not-for · 18 bonus 1 ends · 19 "closes tomorrow" + a student story · 20 CLOSE · 21 closed → after. Group class: 11–13 the 3 nights, 14 live 60 min + "doors open tomorrow", 15 offer post.
Each day prints: date · plain step name · pieces in copy boxes · ask · coach minutes; open and close days (60–120 min) flagged ahead. An urgency piece with no matching real limit becomes a value or FAQ piece.
DATE CHECK, silent, before printing: each template day on the real calendar, against the coach's dates (cohort start, busy days, events they named) and §CM-LAUNCH-TIMING's holidays (VN: 20/10, 20/11, Tết); a clash moves the piece, never a real deadline, said in one line. The close sits ≥3 days before the cohort start; no move or STOP (§CM-CAMPAIGNS 4) lands on or past it. Timed sends keep the quiet hours (§CM-LAUNCH-TIMING 6).

### Launch mode and the daily Launch Desk
1 MODES: normal → runway (4 weeks, no selling) → prep (the week before) → launch (the calendar) → cooldown (2 weeks: debrief, testimonials, objections → next month's belief posts) → normal. Launch pieces carry `hub:Phase` Launch.
2 Automations in launch mode: the weekly script batch writes the next 7 calendar days around the latest objections (other topics paused but 1 relate piece a week); the daily idea + hook drop becomes the Desk; the weekly review becomes the scoreboard (plan vs actual per step). Cooldown switches them back.
3 LAUNCH DESK, daily, ≤20 coach minutes (a VA can do the rest):
- today's pieces in copy boxes, in posting order with times;
- yesterday's row: reach · messages · page visits · sales · seats left · top objection; one change for tomorrow;
- up to 3 new questions or objections pasted (no names) → tomorrow's post or FAQ answers them;
- seat updates at the listed times: the coach gives the number; the machine never changes one itself;
- one-line flags: a promise due (gift, series part, the coach's threshold), a deadline tonight, a reply window closing.
4 Messages: auto-replies as §CM-CTA-KIT 7; offers by DM only within 24 hours of their last message, hence the "reply YES" step; cart news by email or Zalo to people who said yes.
5 Never ask for phone numbers in public; hide comments that post one; every capture message carries one purpose and consent line (§CM-MESSAGES 3).
6 A day over 2 hours with no VA: say so once; offer stories only on the light days.

### After the close: buyers, non-buyers, debrief
1 Doors-closed post the same night or next morning; the link is off; no reopen (payment failure aside, §CM-LAUNCH-BRIEF 5).
2 Buyers: welcome + first step, a first win by week 2; weeks 2–4: 3 questions (before · what changed · result so far) + an OK per use, stored with its date.
3 Non-buyers, day 7: "What made you consider it?" · "What stopped you: time, price, not sure it fits, timing, other?" · "What would make the next round a yes?" Then a smaller offer (self-study or a payment plan) for 72 hours, with a real deadline.
4 DEBRIEF, day 7 (numbers) and day 30 (refunds, testimonials), ≤30 min, filled from the scoreboard (a gap: one question per reply):
a plan vs actual: seats, revenue, warm pool, each step against the math;
b biggest leak: the step furthest below plan + one fix;
c what sold: the piece and day with most enquiries; the top objection;
d trust: closed on time? complaints? reach dropped or a post flagged? any claim we couldn't back?
e keep · drop · test, ≤3 each;
f into the Card: objections → belief posts, winning hooks → next month, FAQ → offer page, new proof (with OK) → proof;
g next: date and type by the picker, waitlist size, 4 weeks of normal topics.
5 Leaks → fixes: few P0 replies → still cold, add runway · low capture → a one-field ask · few sign-ups show up → reminders 24 h, 1 h, at start · many questions, few sales → FAQ, payment plan, not-for · flat close → more close-day touches. The first launch is the baseline; compare by their own numbers, never an industry benchmark.
