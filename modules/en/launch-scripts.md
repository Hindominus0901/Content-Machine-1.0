### Launch posts and videos
Every piece: the coach's voice and platform (§CM-VOICE), one idea, one ask; facts from the Brief or OK'd proof, else [NEEDS: …] in the line; a copy box, under it only what §CM-EDGE prints, e.g. "{{t:verdict.needs}}".
1 Opening shapes, rewritten in their words: P0 "Building something for {who}. What's stopping you from {result}?" · P1 "Free, not for sale: {gift} that does {step} in {time}." · P2 "I used to think {old belief}. Then {moment}." · P5 "Doors are open: {offer} for {who}. {N} seats, closes {day, time}." · P7 "{time} update: {n} of {N} seats left." · P8 "Doors are closed. Thank you, all {n} of you." Seats, counts and dates only from the real limits.
2 SHORT VIDEO, 30–40 s: on-screen hook (0–2 s) → the real before (to 6 s) → the method working (to 20 s) → "this is step 1 of 3" (to 28 s) → the ask. P2, P3: the belief or step in the middle.
3 SHORT TEXT POST (Facebook background): ≤130 characters, no link: result · effort or time · "free, not for sale" · a cap only from the real limits, with its reason · the ask. P5, P7, P8: offer, seats, close time.
4 STORY POST (P2): old belief → what it cost → the moment (a real person, day and their words, with OK) → new belief → proof with context → what it means for the reader → "tomorrow: …".
5 CASE SERIES (P4): 5 parts on fixed dates: start · stuck point · method · result · lessons + invite. A money or result number carries its context in the same post (audience and years, ad spend, price, buyers, refunds, team), "individual result, not a promise" and what most got; no context → process story, no number.
6 MYTHS CAROUSEL (P2), 6 slides: "3 reasons you haven't {result} (all 3 wrong)" · one slide per old belief: myth · truth · do this today · what really stops most people · save + the ask.
7 CLOSE DAY: 3 posts (morning "last day", noon FAQ, evening "2 hours left") + stories: countdown · seat update · OK'd proof · not-for · question box · last hour · closed + waitlist. Offer post: §CM-LAUNCH-LIVE 3. The coach's own bait lines: §CM-LAUNCH-BRIEF 7.

### Launch messages: email, Zalo, DMs
Opted-in people only; one ask, no guilt; follow-ups carry an exit line; facts from the Brief or [NEEDS: …]; copy boxes, under each only what §CM-EDGE prints, e.g. "{{t:verdict.needs}}".
1 PUBLIC REPLIES under a keyword post: ≥5 short, rotating ("Sent! Check your message requests."), some by hand.
2 DM REPLY 1, the one automatic reply: the gift + one A/B question that sorts where they are + "I'll only use this to send the material and launch reminders. Reply STOP anytime."
3 DM REPLY 2, after they answer (the window is open): a 2-minute tip for their answer + the event + "Want a reminder? Leave your email (or Zalo)", with its purpose.
4 EARLY LINK, the day before the cart: "Doors open tomorrow after {event}. Want the link an hour early? Reply YES." To each YES within 24 hours: the link, what's on the page, the real bonus, "Any question before you decide?"
5 HUMAN FOLLOW-UP, only to people with an open question: their question, a short answer, the close, "If it's not the right time, no worries."
6 AFTER THE GIFT, 5 messages in 5 days: gift + who I am + a one-word question · backstory and the wall · the moment it changed · what else it changes + event invite · waitlist + early link. Email: 3 subject lines each.
7 CART: open (what · for whom · price, next price · real bonus · guarantee · close · link · 48-hour replay) · case · FAQ · bonus ends tonight · closes tomorrow · close day 3–4 (morning to last hour). Last call: short; who it's for and not; "After tonight the link goes off. Next round: {date}."
8 AFTER: welcome + first step · non-buyers: a gift, the survey (§CM-LAUNCH-DEBRIEF 3), the smaller offer with its real deadline · waitlist.

### The live, the class and the offer page
1 LIVE OR WORKSHOP, 60 min, their spoken voice: 0–5 promise, who it's for, "At the end I'll tell you about my program; if it's not for you, the first 45 minutes are still yours." · 5–15 their story: what changed the result → the one big idea · 15–25 the method works (OK'd case) · 25–33 "you can" · 33–41 "no time, no tech" + live demo · 41–55 offer: recap → the fastest way with help → each part and why → price → guarantee → seats and reason → bonus deadline → how to join → close date twice · 55–60 Q&A from logged objections, the date again.
2 CHALLENGE OR GROUP CLASS: 5 days (quick win · the method · "I can" + case · "no time" + what's inside · do it + the offer) or 3 nights in a group (one ≤7-minute video + one exercise a night, recap each morning) + 1 live. Reminders 24 hours, 1 hour, at start; replay for 24 hours.
3 OFFER POST OR PAGE, in order: headline (offer · round · seats) · promise + condition · for (3) · 3 steps, each a deliverable · what's inside · price, real next price and when · bonus, its cap or deadline and reason · why {N} seats · "Not for you if…" · process guarantee, terms · one ask, close day, time, zone · FAQ: the top 5 logged objections. No guarantee terms: "{{t:verdict.needs}}"
4 Hard stops as §CM-GUARDRAILS; a result promise always carries its condition ("if you give it 1 hour a day") and the coach's record.
