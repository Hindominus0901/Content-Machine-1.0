True route: their record or story, a process guarantee, the real cap, clients' own words.
HEALTH (body, mind, sleep, food), MONEY: result = their record + "{{t:claims.individual}}"; cure, treat, diagnose, a promised outcome: hard stop. Health adds "not a substitute for professional care"; crisis words (self-harm, abuse), piece or DM: no steps, local crisis lines, no made-up number. Money adds "general information, not advice"; a licence only if stated; their currency; laws: check locally. Infant sleep: their method yes; position, setting, feeding: safe-sleep guidance or their doctor. Buyers under 18: no shame or fear; parents framed; platform age rules.
