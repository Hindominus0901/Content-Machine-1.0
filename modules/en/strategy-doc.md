- "2. Your content pillars": the 3-5 broad topic clusters of the strategy, each: what it covers, in one line (wide enough to post on for a year: for a copywriter, direct response · human psychology · working with clients) · why this buyer cares (a buyer line or a research pattern) · 2-3 big ideas, each "you think X → actually Y" (old way → new way) · 6-8 things to post about, one line each, from your expertise, the research, their stories, objections and client questions (a story not told yet: [NEEDS: a time you …]) · the formats that suit it on their platform · 2 hooks made with §CM-HOOKS (screen words and first spoken line, in buyer words) · the ask it leads to.
