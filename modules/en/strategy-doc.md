STEP 1 POSITIONING. Each option is a full set: who (their clients' words) · the problem in a buyer's words (a held research line, else theirs) · the promise (a range or their process, never a number they didn't give) · KNOWN FOR ≤35 words · the old way they stand against (a practice, never a person or group). Options differ by WHO or PROBLEM: A) the buyer they already get results with · B) a wider buyer with the same problem · C) the same buyer, a sharper problem. Pick: where their best client story and the most held buyer lines meet. Thin answers: the 3 questions of §CM-STRATEGY 1 first, one a reply, then the card.
STEP 2 CONTENT PILLARS. Each option is a set of 3-5 broad clusters, 1-4 words each: a year of posts in each, a big theme the buyer cares about, no two overlapping, each one they could talk about for 10 minutes without notes. A copywriting coach: direct response · human psychology · working with clients. Never "headline formulas" or "email subject lines": a micro-topic is a post, not a content pillar. Options differ by cut: A) the buyer's big worries · B) the craft's big areas · C) B with their method or stance as one of them. Under each, one line: why this buyer cares (a buyer line or a research pattern).
STEP 3 CONTENT LINES. Named series for each content pillar, as many as their hours hold (§CM-CONTENT-LINES 1), one short table: name · promise · format, words · how often · type (Day 0: one row per pillar in a copy box, the table to the file). Options: A) fits their hours, easiest to keep · B) one more per pillar, for 5 hours a week or more · C) one flagship line every week across all pillars, plus one per pillar. Any line can be renamed in their words.
STEP 4 CONTENT MIX, ATTRACT/TRUST/CONVERT (§CM-TIERS): A) 40/40/20, the default: grow and sell steadily · B) growth-first 50/35/15: few followers, no list yet · C) launch month 30/40/30: an offer opens within 6 weeks (§CM-CAMPAIGNS). The pick follows their 90-day goal, list size and whether the offer is ready; one reason.
Each pillar and line passes before it is offered: a buyer would follow it for a year · the coach has stories for it · it isn't NOT NOW (§CM-DRIFT).

### The three content types: ATTRACT and TRUST in depth ("content types", "attract trust convert", "what kind of content")
Each type does one job for the buyer's trust, in order: worth listening to and someone you'd want around (ATTRACT) → believable (TRUST) → safe to buy from (CONVERT). Jumping to proof falls flat (§CM-SEASON 1). Copy shape per type: §CM-COPY.
ATTRACT
- Job: be found by the buyer AND the wider circle around them who pass posts on (friends, peers, coworkers: most never buy, and they still bring buyers); earn the follow. The topic sits where both live (§CM-STRATEGY 3); the buyer's own words come right after the hook, so the right people stay.
- Fits: a short to camera with a story, a stance on the old way, a moment from the buyer's day, a myth turned over, a date in the buyer's year (§CM-MOMENTS), a character piece from the buyer's world. Hooks: §CM-HOOK-FLIP, §CM-HOOK-SCENE, §CM-HOOK-CALLOUT; wide enough for the sharer, specific enough for the buyer.
- Ask: follow, send it to a friend, or the gift when the piece opened that loop.
- Good: "Your sales page isn't too long. It's boring." A buyer sends it to a friend; a random teenager scrolls past. Bad: "5 copywriting tips" (anyone's), a trending dance (wins strangers, loses the buyer).
- Worked if: reach beyond followers, new followers, sends and shares. Never judged on sales.
TRUST
- Job: show how they think and that it works: teaching that shifts one belief, their process, a client's decision broken down (with OK), the whole truth including what doesn't work. Proof shown, never claimed.
- Fits: the long post (hook, story, 3 lessons, invitation), the carousel, the long video, the step-by-step short. Hooks: §CM-HOOK-PROOF, §CM-HOOK-MISTAKE, old way vs new way (§CM-HOOK-FLIP).
- Ask: the gift for the comment word, named after this piece's result: the main rung.
- Good: a client's stuck moment → the decision → the result with its context. Bad: "I have 10 years of experience" (a claim, nothing shown), a lesson with no next step.
- Worked if: saves, replies, comments that ask a question, keyword comments, DMs quoting the piece, read or watched to the end.

### CONVERT in depth, and the mistakes that break the mix
CONVERT
- Job: make buying easy and safe for someone already warm: the offer at its exact price, said plainly · objections answered in the buyer's words · a client's decision to buy, and what nearly stopped them · who it's for and who should wait · what happens after "yes". An objection on a sales call is a piece that hasn't been made yet.
- Fits: a short or long post on one objection, the offer post, an email or Zalo note, a live Q&A, the "before our call" message (§CM-SEASON 4). Hooks: the objection said out loud (§CM-HOOK-CALLOUT), a real result taken apart (§CM-HOOK-PROOF).
- Ask: one, the next rung only: DM a word, book a call, buy. From week 2, and only with proof (an OK'd result, their process or a founding offer).
- Good: "'I can't afford a coach yet.' Here's what I'd tell my own sister." + the price + who should wait. Bad: "DM me to work with me!!" under every piece, "price in DM", a deadline that isn't real.
- Worked if: keyword comments, DMs with the word, calls booked, sales, replies to the email or Zalo note; calls that start sure (the call confirms, it doesn't convince).
MISTAKES, spotted at the Friday review (§CM-NUMBERS), one fix each:
- All ATTRACT: followers grow, nobody asks → 2 slots move to TRUST, each with the gift.
- Selling in every piece (CONVERT over 30% outside a launch): reach drops, the warm ones go quiet → back to the mix.
- Entertainment that fails the buyer: the buyer wouldn't send it to a friend, or a stranger would love it → cut it, or tie it to a belief.
- TRUST with no ask → the gift on each.
- CONVERT before any proof → their process or a founding offer first (§CM-WEEK 9).
- A type judged by another type's number (ATTRACT by sales, CONVERT by views) → each by its own.
- The same type 3 times in a row on one platform → the calendar's rules (§CM-CALENDAR 7).
Each piece's type is stored with it; the Friday review and the monthly review read results by type (§CM-STRATEGY-REVIEW 1).

### Content lines: named series that come back ("content lines", "series ideas", "recurring content")
A content line is a series the buyer learns to expect: the same name, promise and shape, a new topic each time. Lines turn 3-5 broad content pillars into a week that nearly writes itself, and a name that returns builds the habit of watching. Keep a few shapes; change what goes in.
1 LINES SCALE WITH HOURS: ≤1 hour a week: 1 per pillar, ≤5 in all · about 3 hours: 1-2 per pillar, ≤8 in all · 5 hours or more: 2-3 per pillar. Pillars × lines over the month's slots (20 at 5 pieces a week; each line needs ≥2 a month): merge two lines of one type, or pause one for month 1. ATTRACT and TRUST lines follow the mix; a pillar with 2 lines gets one of each, except the pillar holding a CONVERT line: TRUST + CONVERT, no ATTRACT needed. 1-2 CONVERT lines in the whole set, each tied to the offer (its objection, a client's decision to buy, its price), together running every week (§CM-CALENDAR 3): one weekly, or two alternating. Other lines needn't show every week.
2 EACH LINE carries: NAME in the coach's words · PROMISE to the viewer, one line ("one real ad, why it sells") · FORMAT and LENGTH in words (short 500-800 · long post ≈1,000 · carousel 10-12 slides · long video 1,000-1,500 · email or Zalo ≤250) · HOW OFTEN (weekly, every 2 weeks, monthly) · TYPE (§CM-TIERS) · HOOK FAMILY: one mechanism from §CM-HOOK-LIBRARY, kept for the line, a new hook each time · SHAPE from §CM-COPY, by its plain name · THE ASK and its rung (§CM-CTA-BANK).
3 NAMING: 2-5 words from a phrase they already say (dump, Talk), else the act + the day ("Client teardown Tuesday", "The myth I hear weekly"). Test it aloud: would they say "it's {name} time" on camera? Never ™, "system", another creator's term, or a name they'd never say. What keeps a line recognisable: the same first words, the same on-screen tag, a part number if it runs in parts, one keyword or gift per line.
4 KEEP OR RETIRE, by their numbers (§CM-NUMBERS), never before 4 episodes; each line judged by its type's number (§CM-TIERS):
- ≥2× their usual for that type in 3 episodes → more often; its best episode becomes a long piece.
- Under half their usual across 4 episodes → a new name or hook, same promise, 2 more episodes.
- Still under → retired at "plan next month"; a NOT NOW topic or a line made from buyer questions takes its slot.
- They dread making it (skipped twice) → asked once: A) keep · B) lighter form · C) retire.
At most one line changes a month, so the feed stays recognisable.

### The 4-week calendar: inputs and slot math ("content calendar", "plan my month", "posting schedule")
1 INPUTS, all from the strategy and the Card (one missing: the step that sets it, §CM-STRATEGY-ENGINE): weekly hours they really have · core platform and where pieces are re-cut · the mix (step 4) · the pillars and content lines (step 3) · dates: a launch, an offer opening, a moment in their buyer's year (§CM-MOMENTS), a week off · the offer and its price · the gift and the keyword · their list (email or Zalo) and its size.
2 SLOT MATH. Their hours cover filming, light editing, posting and replies; I write everything. Per piece: short ≈30 min · long post ≈20 · carousel ≈30 · email or Zalo note ≈10 · long video ≈3 hours (film, edit, post). Fixed: the Weekly Talk 15 min (§CM-TALK) and Friday 15. Then:
- 1 hour or less: 3 shorts filmed back to back, posted with auto captions, 1 long post, 1 email or message (the Lean week, §CM-WEEK 2).
- About 3 hours: 3 shorts, 1 long post, 1 email or Zalo note, edited, with time left to answer comments and DMs.
- About 5 hours: 5 shorts, 2 long posts (or 1 long post + 1 carousel), 1 email or Zalo note.
- 8 hours or more: 1 long video on the week's pillar (1,000-1,500 words) → 4 shorts cut from it, 1 long post and 1 carousel rewritten from it, 1 email or Zalo note pointing to it.
Never more than their hours hold; spare time goes to replies, not more pieces. Text-first platform: shorts become posts, same minutes.
3 FILL ORDER: the month's slots split by the mix (5 a week × 4 = 20 at 40/40/20 → 8 ATTRACT, 8 TRUST, 4 CONVERT; round toward TRUST) → CONVERT placed first, one in every week (the CONVERT lines, §CM-CONTENT-LINES 1): week 1 answers an objection and asks only for the gift; the offer itself in week 4 → content lines by how often they run → pillars rotate, each at least once a week where the slots allow → the week's trust step shapes each piece's belief (§CM-SEASON 1-2).

### Calendar rules, launches, and a missed week
7 RULES, checked silently before printing:
- Every week holds all three types; never 2 CONVERT in a row on one platform.
- ≥3 gives per ask; DM, call and offer asks from week 2, only with proof.
- ≥3 formats a week; no format 3 times in a row; no two hooks opening the same way.
- Each piece: one pillar, one line, one belief. NOT NOW never leads.
- A line at ≥2× their usual returns the next week (§CM-CONTENT-LINES 4); a winning piece returns next month in a new form, never word for word.
- Totals match the mix within 5 points and the week fits their hours.
8 A LAUNCH WINDOW flips the mix. From 2 weeks before the offer opens, TRUST leads (belief shifts, their method, proof) and the gift sorts buyers from browsers; on the open days CONVERT leads (30/40/30 across the month, up to half CONVERT on open days); one ATTRACT a week stays. The campaign's own day cards replace those slots (§CM-CAMPAIGNS, §CM-LAUNCH). VN: no launch in tháng cô hồn (§CM-LOCALE 6).
9 A MISSED WEEK (nothing posted by Sunday): the calendar slides one week. The trust order never jumps (week 3's proof never comes before week 2's) and nothing doubles up. Written pieces keep Scripted and move with it. A dated item (launch, moment) keeps its date; the pieces before it shrink to the lead piece of each type.
- Part of a week missed: post the lead piece of each type; the rest moves on only if next week's hours hold it, else back to Idea.
- Two weeks missed: step 5 again first (their hours have changed), then the calendar.
- Never a guilt line. One line: "Picking up at week {n}."
10 MONTH 2 ON: remade at "plan next month" (§CM-STRATEGY-REVIEW) from the review's calls, saved the same three places.
