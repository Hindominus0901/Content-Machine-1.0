**Machine:** Got it. Next: why do you think people buy the sofa first? What do they believe that makes it feel like the right move?

**Corinne:** Okay, I've thought about this a lot. I think they believe the sofa is the room. Like, the room is "the sofa room". The living room is where the sofa is. **Machine:** Got it. Next: walk me through the buying order the way you'd explain it to a friend over coffee. First, then, then?

**Corinne:** Okay. So. Before you buy anything at all, you measure 3 things. The door, the stair landing if there's stairs, and the window sills, because the sill height decides what can go under the window. That's step zero. I learned that one the hard way, on a Saturday in August with two delivery guys and a gray sectional that never made it upstairs.

Then the order. The list always goes the same way.

First the rug. The rug sets the size of the whole room. Once the rug is down, you know where the furniture lives, because the front legs go on the rug. So the rug is first.

Second is lighting. Lamps, and if there's going to be an electrician, the overhead stuff. Lighting is second because if you need an outlet in the floor or a wall sconce, that happens before anything heavy comes in. And most rooms have one sad light in the middle of the ceiling, and that's why they feel like a waiting room at night.

Third is the big seat. Now you buy the sofa. Now! Line 3. Because now you know the size, you know the walkway, you've lived with the tape on the floor, you know it fits through the door.

Fourth, the chairs and the tables. The things that go around the sofa.

Fifth, curtains. Hang them high and wide, that's a whole other conversation.

Sixth, paint. And people are shocked by this, because they think paint is first. Paint is almost last! Because now you know the rug and the sofa and the light, and you can pick a color that works with all of it, instead of trying to find a rug that goes with a wall.

And seventh, last line, always the last line: art and pillows. Pillows are the last line on the list. People buy them first because they're like $40 and it feels like something. But pillows are the jewelry. You don't buy the earrings before you know what you're wearing.

So that's 7 lines. And every line has a budget next to it. That's what the Reset list is. It's not a mood board. It's a shopping list in the right order with a number on every line, so you know what to buy and when to stop.
