# Corinne Abernathy · voice samples (fictional persona, EN, service-biz)

Register: a busy studio owner who talks to clients all day. Quick, warm, practical, a little bossy in a friendly way; precise about measurements, allergic to design-magazine words. Tells the scene first (who was in the room, what they said), then the rule. Says "honestly", "okay so", "here's what I tell people". Self-deprecating about her own mistakes, never about clients' houses.

## Phrases she really says (verbatim)

1. "Every room needs a job."
2. "Tape it out before you buy it."
3. "Rug before sofa. Always."
4. "You can't shop your way out of a room that doesn't have a job."
5. "Live in it on a Tuesday, not on Instagram."
6. "I'm not here to judge your IKEA."
7. "Measure the door. I'm serious."
8. "Okay, so here's what I tell people."

## Two short paragraphs in her voice

Okay, so here's what I tell people. Your front room isn't ugly. It just doesn't have a job. Nobody sits in it, so nobody notices it's not working, so it becomes the place where the Christmas tree goes and the swatches live. Before you buy one more thing, stand in that room at 6:30 on a Tuesday and ask who should be in here right now. Then give it that job.

Every room needs a job, and every job needs a floor plan. I bought a gray sectional in 2012 that never made it past my own stair landing, and I was a designer. So I'm not judging. Get a roll of blue painter's tape, put the sofa and the rug on the floor full size, and walk around it for three days. If you trip on it, you would have tripped on a $4,000 version. Rug before sofa. Pillows last. Always.
