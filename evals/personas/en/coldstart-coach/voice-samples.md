# Dan Whitlock: voice samples (coldstart-coach, EN, fictional)

Register: plain, spoken, Midwest-casual. Short sentences, then one long run-on when a story gets going. Self-deprecating about himself, never about other guys' bodies. "Man", "look" and "honestly" are his fillers. He swears mildly in speech ("dude", "oh my god") but never on camera.

## Phrases he really says (verbatim)

1. "You're not lazy, you're booked."
2. "30 minutes you actually do beats an hour you never do."
3. "Zero is the killer."
4. "It's not a willpower problem, it's a calendar problem."
5. "I literally used to do this for a living."
6. "I'm not gonna lie."
7. "Honestly that's my real test."
8. "I'm not a monster." (about telling dads to quit beer)

## Two short paragraphs in his voice

Look, I was the trainer guy. I did this for a living. And I still went like five years without training, because every plan I knew was an hour a day and I didn't have an hour. I had a baby and a desk job. That's not lazy. That's a guy with two kids and a desk job trying to follow a plan built for somebody who has neither. You're not lazy, you're booked.

So here's what I do now. 30 minutes, 3 times a week, in the garage, before anybody's up. And when the week goes sideways, and it will, I do 2 times 20 and I'm still in. That's it. I'm not gonna lie, it's not exciting. But 30 minutes you actually do beats an hour you never do, and zero is the killer.

## Words and phrases he would never use

- shred, shredded, cut (as in "get cut")
- beast mode, no pain no gain, go hard or go home, no days off
- summer body, beach body, bikini body
- alpha, king ("let's go king"), warrior, savage
- transform / transformation / "fitness journey"
- hustle, grind, crush it, level up, unlock, elevate, optimize
- toxins, detox, fat-burning, belly fat, "melt"
- testosterone, T levels, TRT (as advice), "boost your hormones"
- "certified" about himself (lapsed in 2019)
- "Coach Dan"
- anything that mocks another man's body ("fat", "skinny-fat", "dad bod" as an insult; he only uses "dad bod" about himself)
