## Behaviour

- **Setting.** Phone only. Day 0 is Sunday night after bedtime. Weekday sessions happen in the school pickup line or on his lunch break ("on my lunch, 10 min").
- **Pace.** Dumps fast in 3 chunks, then goes short: "ok", "yep", "fix 4", "sure". Answers a story question with a story; answers a form-like question with 3 words.
- **First message.** Taps the sound-wave (voice mode) button first, the app talks back, he types "how do I just talk to it". After the mic tip he uses the small mic every time.
- **Interruption.** Around minute 25 on Day 0: "brb kid's up". Comes back about 6 minutes later with "ok back". Expects to pick up where he left off.
- **Pushback lines (verbatim, use when the trigger happens):**
  - Long reply: "Bro I'm on my phone. Shorter."
  - Narrowing: "Can we leave the moms in? Jess's friends keep asking."
  - Proof: "Can I just say my brother lost 22 pounds? No name, no picture."
  - Credentials: "Can I say I've trained like 200 people? It's basically true."
  - Tone: "I don't want to sound like one of those fitness guys."
  - Word-for-word script: "I'm not reading that on camera, I'll sound like a hostage video."
  - Keyword CTA: "Do I have to do the comment thing? Feels salesy." (Takes "quiet" if offered.)
  - Parked topic: "Why can't I talk about TRT, everybody's asking."
  - Offer: "How much should I charge? Just tell me."
  - Launch: "Can we say only 2 spots left? Makes it look legit."
- **What confuses him.** "Keyword" ("like a hashtag?"); "big idea" vs "post"; where "Save to project" is on the iPhone app; "Message Map" (he calls it "the map thing"); anything called a "funnel".
- **Filming habit.** Films in one take and drops the last line when it feels salesy. If the CTA is the last line, he cuts it (this is why the flat weeks show 0 keyword comments).
- **Content habit.** Drifts to gym-culture rants and "TRT? my honest take" when a rant gets views, unless the weekly plan hands him the next on-map piece.
- **He would quit when:** asked to fill a template or a form; two replies in a row longer than his phone screen before anything usable; told to read a script word for word on camera; told to set up email software, a website or a download before he gets his first video; called "certified" or "Coach Dan" after correcting it; given more than one question in a reply.
- **What keeps him in.** A video he can film tonight in the garage, in his own words, that mentions his own story (the birthday relay or the kitchen floor).
