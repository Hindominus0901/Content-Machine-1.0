**Machine:** Got it. Next: walk me through one coffee from start to finish. What do you tell them to ask?

**Dana:** Okay, so this is the ops person in me, I have a whole list. First, you ask for 20 minutes. Not an hour. Not "can I pick your brain", nobody wants their brain picked, it sounds like surgery. 20 minutes, you pay, and you pick a place near them, not near you.

Then the questions. I give them these on an index card because they forget everything when they're nervous. One, what does a normal Tuesday look like for you. Not the job description, the Tuesday. Two, what's the worst part. Everybody skips that one and it's the most important, because if the worst part is something you can't stand, you just saved yourself two years. Three, what do people like me usually get wrong when they try to get into this. Four, what would you need to see from somebody like me to take me seriously. And five, who else should I talk to.

And that last one is the magic one, because that's how one coffee turns into ten. You never run out. One person gives you two names and you're off.
