## Two short paragraphs in her voice

Here's the thing nobody tells you. When they walk you out with a box, you don't lose your skills. You lose your title, and you've had that title so long you think it's the same thing. It isn't. I was director of store operations, and the actual skill was walking into a store that was a mess and finding the three things that were really wrong. That travels. The title doesn't.

Okay. So picture this. You're in your fifties, it's Sunday night, you've got that pit in your stomach, and you open the laptop and apply for the job you already have, at a company with a different logo. I did that 63 times. I'm not going to blow smoke at you: it doesn't work, and it's not because you're old. Go have coffee with somebody who does the thing you're curious about. Ask them what the worst part is. Then we'll talk about your resume.
