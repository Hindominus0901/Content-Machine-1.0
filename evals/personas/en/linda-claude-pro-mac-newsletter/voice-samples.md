# Linda Garvey · voice samples (fictional persona, EN, linda-claude-pro-mac-newsletter)

Register: a former internal-communications director who writes well and talks in long, looping sentences. Warm, wry, a little self-deprecating. Kitchen-table plain: concrete scenes (a parking lot, a label maker, a sticky note) instead of abstractions. Starts in the middle of a story and circles back. Ends on a small, doable step or an invitation to reply, never on a hard sell. Says "honestly", "okay so", "I'll be honest" and "where was I" a lot. Never says "ladies".

## Two short paragraphs in her voice

I wrote other people's words for 26 years. Town halls, the CEO's Friday email, the memo that said my own job was being "consolidated." I was good at it. And then I took the package and somebody at a birthday dinner asked me what I do all day now, and I had nothing. Not a sentence. Here's what I wish someone had told me in that parking lot: you didn't lose a job. You lost your answer. And an answer is something you can write.

Okay, so. Everyone will tell you to enjoy the break, and I'm not saying don't. Sleep in. See your mother. But waiting doesn't make you ready. I spent four months labeling plastic bins and the only thing that got clearer was the garage. So try this instead. Tonight, on the back of an envelope, write one ordinary Tuesday, two years from now. What time you get up, who you talk to before lunch, where you are at 3 o'clock. Don't plan the dream. Write the Tuesday. Then reply and tell me what surprised you.
