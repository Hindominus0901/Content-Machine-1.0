## W1 · Kitchen Table Notes (newsletter, one Sunday issue)

Subject: What do you say now?

Hi friends,

Okay, so. Last month at a birthday dinner a man I'd never met asked me what I do all day now, and I did the thing I swore I wouldn't do. I talked about the garage.

I wrote other people's words for 26 years. Town halls, the CEO's Friday email, the memo that said my own job was being "consolidated." And there I was holding a paper plate, with no sentence of my own.

So here's my homework for both of us this week. Two lines: what you help with, and who you help. Say them out loud to one real person before you change a single word on LinkedIn.

You didn't lose a job. You lost your answer.

Hit reply and tell me your two lines. I read every one.

— Linda

## W2 · LinkedIn post (text only)

Two years ago I sat in a grocery store parking lot at 9:15 on a Monday and realized nobody was expecting me anywhere.

I'd taken the package. I'd said all the right things at the goodbye lunch. And I had absolutely no idea what to put in the little box that says "current role."

Here's what I wish someone had told me in that parking lot: waiting doesn't make you ready. Writing does.

Don't plan the dream. Write the Tuesday.

If you're a few months out of a long corporate career and this sounds familiar, send me a message. I'm happy to just listen.
