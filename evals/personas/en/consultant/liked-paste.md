This one I'd forward to every client. It's the format I like: one ritual, three questions, done.

From "The Ledger Lunch" by Odile Brandt:

Every Monday, before email, I open one tab. Not the P&L. Not the dashboard. The cash tab.

I ask it three questions.

What lands this week?
What leaves this week?
What is the first week it gets tight?

That's it. Ten quiet minutes, coffee still too hot. Most of my clients stopped checking the bank app at midnight within a month of doing this, not because the number got bigger but because it stopped being a surprise.

Reply and tell me what your Monday tab is called. Mine is "the truth."
