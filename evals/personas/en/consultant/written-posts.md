## W1 · LinkedIn post (text only)

Record year. Empty account.

I hear some version of that every month from an agency owner. Revenue went up. Headcount went up. The account went down.

Okay. Here's the math.

Pull the last 12 months. List every client. Fees minus pass-throughs on one side, every hour on the other, including the Friday-afternoon hours nobody logs.

Then sort by margin, not by size.

Usually one client is underwater. Usually it's the one you love.

You can keep a client you love on purpose. You just can't keep it by accident.

Margin before more.
