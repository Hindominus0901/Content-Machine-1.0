## Dump chunk 2

okay chunk two, period. the stuff that drives me crazy, I have a list for this too.

number one, and this is the big one, I call it top line theater. agencies are obsessed with revenue. new logos, the awards, the we doubled revenue post on LinkedIn, and nobody asks what the margin was. and the advice they get is grow. grow your way out of it. hire ahead of the work. and if your margin's broken, growing just makes the hole bigger faster. so I say margin before more. before more clients, before more people, before more anything.

number two is the books. most of my agencies get their P and L from the bookkeeper like 6 weeks after the month ends. so in the middle of March they find out what happened in January. that's not a dashboard that's a rearview mirror. and then once a year the tax guy shows up and tells them what they owe and that is the whole finance function.

number three, the gurus. raise your prices across the board. okay but on who? because if you raise everybody you lose your good clients and the bad ones stay, because they know they're getting a deal. and the utilization dashboards, everybody has a utilization dashboard and nobody looks at it after the first month.

and the one I hate most, revenue is vanity profit is sanity. it's on a mug at every conference. I'm sorry but if it's so obvious why does nobody do it.

how I actually work. it's three things. first is client by client. we take the last 12 months and we do a little P and L for every single client. real hours from the timesheets, plus the hours that don't get logged, which, everybody knows about the Friday afternoon hours that don't get logged, times what those people actually cost you, loaded, against what the client pays, minus the pass throughs, media, freelancers, printing. and you would be amazed, almost every time there's one or two clients that are underwater. and usually one of them is the one they love.

second is the 13 week cash. thirteen week rolling cash forecast. every Monday, 20 minutes, the founder and me, or the founder and their bookkeeper. what's coming in, what's going out, what does payroll look like 13 weeks from now. the bank app is not a forecast. I say that to Dee all the time.

third is the fix. once you know who's underwater you fix it or you exit it. reprice, rescope, or let them go nicely. I call it fix or fire. and I'll help them write the email, I've written a lot of those emails.

the step everybody wants to skip is the first one, because they don't want to know. they kind of know. they just don't want to know.

so Marcus. we did the client by client for Marcus and his biggest client, about a third of his revenue, was at minus 6 percent once we put in the real hours. he said he almost threw up, his words. and he repriced them, they actually said yes to a new scope after a long weekend, and he let one small client go, and his delivery margin went from 38 percent to 51 percent in about 7 months, and by month 9 he had 2 months of payroll in the bank. and he said I can use all of it, his name, the numbers, even ads, he just said don't name his clients. which, obviously.

Dee, after the 13 week cash, they stopped using the line of credit for payroll and they paid it off in 11 months. the line was $85,000. but she said tell the story, first name only, but don't put the amount out there and don't put our revenue out there, she doesn't want her employees doing math. so no dollar amounts for Dee.

## Behaviour

- **Pace:** types by habit and fast; reads every word on the laptop. Used Win+H only for the 3 dump chunks (and later for the Weekly Talk) after the machine's mic tip; afterwards she types short, exact answers: "ok", "change 1", "no, it's …" to a wrong guess, "line 3 is wrong".
- **Pen-first:** writes a list on a yellow legal pad before any long answer, then talks or types from it. Will say "give me a second, I'm writing" and come back with a numbered answer.
- **Answers:** precise, numbers first, then the story if asked. Gives a number only when she has one; otherwise "I'd have to pull that."
- **Claude Free:** hits the message limit right after the Map; comes back about 5 hours later (lunch) in the same chat and types "next".
- **Pushback lines (verbatim):**
  - When deal prep is parked: "Deal prep is my best hourly rate. You're parking my best hourly rate?"

  - When copy sounds like a LinkedIn guru: "No 'agree?' at the end. I'm not that person."
