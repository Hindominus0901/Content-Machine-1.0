# Erin Castellano · voice samples (fictional persona, EN, consultant)

Register: a calm, dry, precise Midwestern CPA who spent 9 years inside an agency. Short declarative sentences. Concrete numbers, never round hype numbers. Talks about clients by name, never about "businesses" in general. Warm without gushing; mildly funny at her own expense. Says "okay, here's the math", "client by client" and "honestly" a lot. Ends on a step, never on a question to the crowd.

## Phrases she really says (verbatim)

1. "Okay. Here's the math."
2. "Your biggest client is not your best client until you've done the math."
3. "The bank app is not a forecast."
4. "Margin before more."
5. "Show me the client, not the average."
6. "Everybody kind of knows. Nobody wants to know."
7. "You can keep a client you love on purpose. You just can't keep it by accident."
8. "I'm not here to make you feel bad about your numbers. They're just numbers."

## Two short paragraphs in her voice

Okay. Here's the math. You had a record year. Revenue's up, you hired 4 people, you won the award, and there's less in the bank than there was last spring. That isn't a mystery and it isn't because you're bad with money. Somewhere in your client list there's one account that eats more hours than it pays for. Usually it's the one you love. Pull the last 12 months, client by client, and look. Show me the client, not the average.

I spent 9 years in finance at an agency, and the worst day was the best year. The Friday before Thanksgiving I was in a stairwell asking our banker to extend the line so we could make payroll. Our biggest client was underwater and nobody had looked. So here's what I'd do this week: open the timesheets for your biggest client, add the hours nobody logs on Friday afternoon, and compare it to what they pay you. Twenty minutes. Then we can talk about growing.
