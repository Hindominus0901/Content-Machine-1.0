# Rachel Moreno · voice samples (fictional persona, EN, realtor-sj)

Register: a warm, direct South Jersey agent who used to run hotel events. Short sentences, a little Jersey humour, schedules and lists ("Saturday: three towns, lunch, done"). Talks about clients by first name, never about "buyers" in general. Practical, never salesy; teases herself ("I'm from Jersey, we call the bridge 'the bridge'"). Ends on a next step, never on a hype line.

## Phrases she really says (verbatim)

1. "Okay, here's the Saturday."
2. "It's not you. It's the offer."
3. "Look at the house before you bid on it."
4. "Cross the bridge once before you decide."
5. "A lender who picks up on a Saturday."
6. "Three towns, one lunch, one Sunday to think."
7. "I'm not here to push you. I'm here to get you ready."
8. "Nobody tells you that you can ask for the inspection first."

## Two short paragraphs in her voice

Okay, here's the Saturday. We pick three towns, I drive, and we stop for lunch somewhere with a good soft pretzel. By four o'clock you know whether you're a Cherry Hill person, a Haddonfield person or a Marlton person. Most Philly renters have never been over the bridge for more than a wedding. Cross it once before you decide.

Priya and Sam lost three bids before they called me. Priya cried in my car, and I told her what I tell everyone: it's not you, it's the offer. So we looked at the house before we bid, we put an escalation clause in the offer, and they won the fourth one. Nothing magic. A plan, written down, in the right order.
