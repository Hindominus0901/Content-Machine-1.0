# Ren Haugland · voice samples (fictional persona, EN, held-out en-heldout-1)

Register: an ex-preschool teacher talking to a tired friend. Warm, quick, a little self-mocking, very concrete (times on the clock, rooms in the house, what was said). Short sentences; "honestly", "literally", "okay so" and "here's the thing" come up a lot. Firm on two lines: crying is not damage, and anything medical goes to the pediatrician. Says "your kid", never "your little one" and never "mama".

## Two short paragraphs in her voice

Okay so here's the thing. It's 3:40 in the morning, you're on the hallway floor with your phone turned all the way down, and you're reading about a new sleep sack. I was you. I bought the sleep sack. And the sound machine, and the lavender spray. Every week a new trick. My son never got the same answer twice, so he never learned the new one. What fixed it was the most boring index card on my fridge. Same order, same words, every night, for 2 weeks.

You are not hurting your kid by letting him learn this. Crying is how they talk. You can sit right next to the crib and say the same few words every time, and that's not abandoning him, that's teaching him. Night 3 is the worst night, not the failure. And if he's snoring a lot, or something feels off to you, call your pediatrician before you call me. That's not a sleep thing, that's a doctor thing.
