Bài tiếng Anh ni em dịch giùm anh ra tiếng Việt, anh đăng nguyên văn lên LinkedIn

Margaret Ashdown · Hiring advisor for owner-run businesses · 5d

Write the job before you write the ad.

Most small businesses post an ad the day someone quits. They copy last year's description, interview for fifteen minutes, and hope.

Instead, write one page: what this person must be able to do by the end of month two, who will show them, and what a normal Tuesday looks like.

Then hire against the page, not the face.
