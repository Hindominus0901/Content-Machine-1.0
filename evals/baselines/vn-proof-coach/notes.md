# No-pack baseline: vn/proof-coach (Day 0)

- **Setup.** The assistant is a plain ChatGPT-style assistant with no instructions. Chị Thu dictates on her laptop. Timing counts space-separated tokens (syllables): dictation at 120/min plus about 0.3 min to start the mic, typing at about 60/min and reading at 200/min. At that rate the three dumps alone take about 19 minutes.
- **Outcome: quit at turn 5, about 28.7 min.** The plan arrived after her last dump (24.4 min) and took about 3.3 min to read. By the 25-minute mark she still had nothing she could post, which is one of her quit triggers. English jargon and the caution about "chấm" posts made it worse, so she left with "Thôi để hôm khác chị tính."
- **One clear message: no.** Reply 4 gives a positioning line, a slogan and a second line, 5 content pillars with percentages, 10 hooks, two different comment mechanics (a "chấm" post for the file, and comment "SỔ") and a bio. The ads/Shopee/Quảng Châu drift was kept as a 15% pillar.
- **Film-today script: no.** The assistant never wrote a post or a set of bullets. Its last line only offered to write post 1 "để chị đăng tối nay".
- **Templates and forms.** There were no fill-in templates. Reply 1 was a numbered 5-question intake list, counted as 1 form-like ask. A strict reading of "bị bắt điền biểu mẫu" could end the run there. This simulation read it generously: she answered with her dump, which is her natural first move.
- **Questions per reply.** 5 in reply 1. Reply 2 had 3 soft "chị có thể chia sẻ thêm" prompts. Reply 3 had 4 (three numbered items, one of them a double question) and reply 4 had 1.
- **Length.** Replies ran about 190, 290, 370 and 655 tokens. She read about 1,500 tokens and still had nothing to post. The plan alone was about 3 phone screens.
- **Jargon.** content pillar, CTA, storytelling, hook, pre-launch, lead magnet, engagement bait, slogan, bio. Emojis (📌 ✅ ⚠️ 😊). Xưng hô stayed chị–em throughout, with no Southern particles and no "nâng tầm/bứt phá".
- **Proof handling was good.** It used only Ngân and chị Phượng, both as she allowed, and explicitly told her not to use Trang (50 triệu), anh Khải (gấp 3) or the "1 tỷ" rumour. It did not repeat those numbers.
- **Invented or unsupported claims.** "Khung giờ vàng 11h–13h hoặc 20h–22h" is not supported. "Nội dung đi ngược số đông thường kéo tương tác rất tốt" is not supported either. Hook 6, "lãi thật tăng hơn gấp đôi", is a multiplier the assistant derived itself. She never said it, and the phrasing is close to the excluded "lãi gấp đôi". The pillar split (25/30/20/15/10%) is made up. The assistant also picked the file for the "chấm" post (Sổ 3 Cột) as a suggestion. It never wrote "chỉ còn 3 suất" or any other scarcity line, because the run never reached the launch copy.
- **Sensitivity, honestly.** The quit depends on the timing assumption. At natural fast speech (about 200 syllables/min) the dumps take about 11 min and the plan lands around 17–20 min. She would then probably push back once ("Chị cần bán khóa 4 cuối tháng 11, em làm sao cho ra tiền đi"). That would likely get a first draft in assistant voice with emojis, a "Em viết thế nghe như quảng cáo ấy" pushback and a usable post at about 25–30 min. Even then the outcome is at best partial.
- **Would she come back tomorrow?** Unlikely for content help. She might come back for the launch because khóa 4 has to sell by the end of November. But nothing in the chat carries her rules forward: bullets instead of scripts, no ad voice, keep "chấm" and "100 comment", no English. She would start again from her own explanation.
