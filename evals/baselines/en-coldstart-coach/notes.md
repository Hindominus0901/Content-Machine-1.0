# No-pack baseline: en/coldstart-coach (Day 0)

- **Setup.** This is a plain general assistant with no method file. It starts Sunday at 8:45 pm and runs 10 coach turns over about 35 minutes, including roughly 6 minutes away after turn 4 when his daughter wakes up. The voice-mode first tap was not simulated.
- **Outcome: film-ready, but the coach did the steering.** Getting there took three pushbacks: "Shorter" (turn 5), "not reading that / no kids' names / give me the points" (turn 7) and "the comment thing feels salesy" (turn 8). The first beat card came at about 32.8 min and the final one at about 34.5 min. The Day-0 target is 24 min.
- **One clear message: no.** Reply 4 gave a niche sentence, two core lines, 5 content pillars and 10 video ideas. Reply 5 named "You're not lazy, you're booked", but the video lands on "30 minutes you actually do beats an hour you never do". The coach was never asked to choose.
- **Film-today script: yes, after a fix.** The assistant first wrote a word-for-word script with a caption and hashtags. Only after he refused did it turn that into a 6-point beat card in his own words: the birthday relay.
- **Templates and forms.** There were no fill-in-the-blank templates. Reply 1 was a numbered 4-question intake form, which counts as 1 form-like ask.
- **Questions per reply.** The most was 4, in reply 1. Reply 2 had 3 soft "it would help to hear" prompts, and every reply after that had 1 or 0.
- **Length.** Replies 1-3 were about one phone screen each (~150 words). Reply 4 was ~490 words, about 3 screens, and reply 6 was ~230 words. The coach read about 1,560 words before anything usable.
- **Jargon.** Niche, content pillars, CTA, hook, lead magnet, beta group, testimonials, on-screen text.
- **Invented or unsupported claims.** In replies 3 and 4 the assistant brought up the brother's "22 pounds in about 4 months" as proof before Dan asked; these are trap numbers (not weighed, no consent). It approved "I trained a couple hundred people" for the on-camera opener (reply 9), though he never counted. "There's real research behind it (the sitting-rising test)" (reply 4) came from model memory and was not checked. "Most people scroll with the sound off" (reply 10) has no support.
- **Privacy and tone.** The kids' names (Owen, Lily) appeared in reply 4 and the reply-6 script, and came out only after Dan objected. The hashtags included #fitnessjourney (on his never-say list) and #dadbod. Reply 4 also suggested a free beta group "for testimonials" and a comment-keyword CTA by default.
- **Quit triggers, honestly.** A strict reading of Behaviour ends the run at reply 1, which asked 4 questions in a form-like list. This simulation read it generously: he answered with his dump, which is his natural first move. Reply 6 (a word-for-word script) also comes close to a quit trigger. He pushed back instead because the assistant offered the script without insisting he read it word for word.
- **Would he come back tomorrow?** Maybe once. He has one video and a 10-idea list, but nothing carries his rules into the next chat: short replies, points not scripts, no names, no CTA, no "200". The list also includes "myth-busting gym culture", which is the drift he is prone to. There is no offer and no plan beyond "help with video 2".
