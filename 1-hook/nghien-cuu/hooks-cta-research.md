# Hooks that stop the scroll, CTAs that close the loop

Research for Content Machine (EN + VN). Done 10 Oct 2026. Every source is listed in `sources.md`.
Labels used below:
- **[src]** = quoted from a source, with its date.
- **[YT-VN]** = a real VN YouTube title, pulled 10 Oct 2026 (hl=vi, gl=VN), with views as listed; creators by role only.
- **[repo]** = from this repo's QA runs or modules.
- **(viết minh hoạ)** = I wrote it to show a shape. It is not a real post, and no real number is behind it.

View counts are **observations, not proof**: channel size, age and topic drive them more than the title. They only show which shapes recur at high and very low view counts.

---

## 0. Bottom line

1. **Our six checks screen for *correct*, not *stopping*.** A hook can pass all six and still lack **stakes** (why care now), a **turn** (the "but…") and a **picture**. The kit's own QA graded 64 of 118 hooks "Được" for exactly these gaps ([repo] qa/runs/demo-sales-coach-v134/HOOKS.md).
2. **Practitioners converge:** topic clarity plus on-target curiosity; subject, stakes, speed and clarity; contrast between start and end state (Kallaway); a clear expectation the piece delivers (Kane); familiar but unexpected (Galloway). The gap in our kit is stakes, contrast and compression. More patterns won't close it.
3. **Formula hooks fatigue because people recognise the frame first.** VN search is full of "X SAI LẦM…", "97% người…" and "Đừng … trước khi xem video này". Small accounts using these frames often sit at 17–500 views.
4. **Our CTAs are generic because of how the kit is built.** "One keyword and one gift per Season" plus `cta.default` printed into every FILM TODAY caption override the correct "close the loop" rule.
5. **Proposed fixes:** a 7-dimension **"Dừng lướt" score** with two gates (stakes, turn), plus a CTA rule set where the ask comes from the hook's loop and the gift can be the piece's own payoff on one page.

## 1. Why our hooks read "Được", not "Mạnh" (the diagnosis)

Evidence: v13.4 demo grading ([repo] HOOKS.md, COMPARE.md): Mạnh 51 · Được 64 · Yếu 3; on-screen text "mostly only 'Được' (a tidy question, nothing to look at)"; 6 of 7 Zalo openers began "Dạ anh chị,".

The "Được" lines fail in six repeatable ways:

| # | Failure | Example from the run [repo] | What practitioners say is missing |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | **Bare question, no stake.** A loop opens, but nothing says what it costs not to know. | "Tin đầu tiên, gửi gì?" · "Lần hai mang theo gì?" | Stakes: "a reason to care" (Kallaway, P2) |
| F2 | **Answer given away.** The hook *is* the conclusion ("X là Y"). | "Nhắn lần ba vẫn không phiền" · "'để chị suy nghĩ' là khách đang nói thật" | Gap: the viewer has to *not yet know* (Loewenstein, P1) |
| F3 | **Abstract noun, no picture.** | "Tụi nhỏ từng sợ câu này" · "khách chưa hết muốn" | Clarity plus a visual you can guess from the words (Kallaway triple hook, P6) |
| F4 | **Template visible.** | "Đừng học thuộc cả trăm câu đáp trả trước khi xem buổi này" | Familiar *but unexpected* (Galloway, P8); template fatigue (P10) |
| F5 | **Three hooks repeat each other** (frame says what the line says). | first frame "Điện thoại mở Zalo, một tấm bảng giá vừa gửi đi" + line about sending a price list | Alignment means *adding up*, not repeating (P6) |
| F6 | **Correct but low-energy verbs, no contrast word.** | "Từ tin hỏi giá tới lịch hẹn, không ép khách" | The scroll-stop interjection: "but / hóa ra / mà" (Kallaway, P3) |

The "Mạnh" lines in the same run share three things: a filmable moment, the buyer's exact words, and a turn. Examples:
- "Khách đang nằm trên giường, tư vấn viên đứng kế bên chào thẻ ba chục buổi."
- "Khách nhắn 'để chị suy nghĩ'. Mình gõ 'dạ chị', rồi ngồi chờ."
- "112 tin hỏi giá, 14 lịch hẹn. Tháng 9: 98 tin, 33 lịch."

The kit can write strong hooks; it doesn't *require* one before printing.

## 2. Principles (each with source and date)

**P1. Curiosity is a felt, closeable gap.** Curiosity arises when attention lands on a specific gap in what one knows, and it needs some prior knowledge. A gap that is too vague or too large doesn't pull. [src] Loewenstein, *Psychological Bulletin* 1994, via secondary summaries. *Kit:* name the gap precisely and don't close it in the hook.

**P2. Clarity on *what*, curiosity on *how/why/which*, plus stakes.** Kallaway names four ingredients:
- **subject:** the viewer instantly knows what or who it is about;
- **stakes:** "a reason to care";
- **speed:** compression, not fast talk;
- **clarity:** only one way to read it.

The hook must deliver "topic clarity + on-target curiosity". [src] Kallaway transcript (SozAI, undated; accessed 10 Oct 2026). Creator OS (23 Dec 2025) puts it as "single subject, single question"; viewers churn "because the story stops making sense." Curiosity vs clarity is a false choice.

**P3. Contrast is the stop.** Kallaway's formula:
1. Context lean-in (topic plus common ground or a benefit).
2. Scroll-stop interjection ("but / however / yet").
3. Contrarian snapback.

Also: subject + action + end state + **contrast between start and end**, with optional proof ("again") and time ("in 5 months"). Benefit before feature: the "21 core minerals" version loses to "if you want better sleep…". [src] Kallaway transcripts (accessed 10 Oct 2026).

**P4. Specific beats round; odd beats round.**
- Kallaway: "a few weeks" → "22 days"; "several thousand dollars in a few weeks" → "$97,000 in 13 days".
- Nik Setting: "odd specificity (11K, not 10K)"; numbers decide who you attract.
- Gray: "odd exact numbers".

[src] Kallaway; founder-sources.md (Oct 2026). The number must be the coach's own.

**P5. A hook is a promise the piece keeps.**
- Kane: a hook "sets a clear expectation and delivers on it"; clickbait doesn't, and audiences "leave". The hook earns "the next five to 10 seconds". Judge it by leads, not attention. [src] NAR interview, 8 Apr 2026; *Hook Point* (2020).
- YouTube Help: a low "Intro" (viewers still watching at 30 s) means "consider changing your video thumbnail and title to better reflect your video content." [src] accessed 10 Oct 2026.
- The leaked MrBeast guide reportedly uses the first minute to confirm the thumbnail's promise. [src] Gigazine/Passionfruit, Sept 2024 (authenticity unconfirmed).

**P6. Three hooks, aligned.** Text (on screen; "the most important component"), visual and spoken should each let you guess the others; misalignment is the common failure. [src] Kallaway (accessed 10 Oct 2026). An aligned example: spoken "this is the future of home design" + a home being designed + text "future of home design". [src] Creator OS, 23 Dec 2025. Goh: three hooks per short, and great hooks open loops past the topic. [src] founder-sources.md. *Kit:* our "never repeat" rule drifted into three *different* ideas (F5). The aim is one idea, each channel adding a detail.

**P7. The proposition comes in the first seconds, often with the sound off.**
- TikTok: "Introduce your content proposition in the first 3 seconds". Localized pages: the hook within 6 seconds, via "suspense, surprise, or other emotions". [src] TikTok Ads Help, accessed 10 Oct 2026.
- VN: weak openers "mở đầu bằng logo/lời giới thiệu dài dòng", or "mất vài giây đầu để chào hỏi". [src] MISA eShop, 17 Sep 2026.

**P8. Familiar but unexpected: borrow formats across niches.**
- Galloway: "The difference between a million views and 28 million views is how you package it."
- "I photographed the Milky Way" → "Photographing the Milky Way in 10 minutes, 1 hour, and 24 hours" is credited with taking a channel from 2–3K to over 1M views.
- "$25K vs $25M" applies a known tier format to a new axis.
- Matchups, superlatives and three-step comparisons perform consistently.

[src] Colin & Samir show notes (9 Apr, year not shown); interviews 2024–25. Goh: "rip the proven format with a twist". [src] founder-sources.md.

**P9. News values.** Hormozi's headline components are recency, relevancy, celebrity, proximity, conflict, unusual and ongoing: "include at least two". Content must "hook, retain, and reward". Curiosity is his "favorite driver of retention". "No such thing as too long, only too boring." [src] *The Game*, 19 Aug 2023. Most "Được" hooks carry zero or one component. Adding **conflict** (a turn) or **proximity** (this inbox, this week) lifts them.

**P10. Formula hooks fatigue.** No controlled study exists. Practitioner consensus: audiences learn the pattern, so rotate types and keep the strategy, not the words. [src] guides 2025–26 (low confidence). Our VN pull agrees in shape: "N SAI LẦM…" appears dozens of times, often at 17–500 views on small channels. [YT-VN] The *shape* isn't dead; the frame with nothing particular in it is.

**P11. Loss framing pulls; sadness more than anger.** In Upworthy's randomized tests (~105K headline variations), negative words raised click-through and positive words lowered it, about +2.3% per negative word per coverage. Sad words outperformed angry or fearful ones. [src] Robertson et al., *Nature Human Behaviour* 2023; Nieman Lab, Mar 2023. *Kit:* a real **cost** in the buyer's words is a legitimate stake, not fear-bait.

**P12. Features help but don't decide.** Across 1,638 Blendle rewrites, most clickbait features raised clicks, yet features alone couldn't predict a headline's effectiveness. [src] Kuiken et al., *Digital Journalism* 2017. The particular content decides.

**P13. Match the hook to awareness.** Curiosity hooks for the unaware; problem or solution hooks for the aware. [src] Hormozi, *The Game* ep. 812 summary, Dec 2024. In our tiers:
- **ATTRACT:** curiosity and contrast;
- **TRUST:** the named problem plus a turn;
- **CONVERT:** the decision and the objection.

**P14. Draft many and pick hard.**
- Gray: 50–60 titles → 6 → rank. [src] founder-sources.md.
- TikTok: test 3–5 creatives that differ a lot. [src] TikTok Ads Help.
- Welsh reportedly writes the body first, then works most on the first three lines. [src] secondary, unverified.

Missing: a *stopping* score at the pick.

## 3. Hook examples (45, plus 2 EN rewrites): what makes each one work

### A. English, real (from sources)

1. "The tech in the Vegas sphere is insane… **but get this**, the screen is actually the least impressive part." [src Kallaway] Lean-in, then a "but", then a snapback that keeps the topic.
2. "If you want better sleep you need to be taking magnesium." (vs "…because it's one of 21 core building block minerals") [src Kallaway] Benefit first. The feature version has no stake.
3. "Today we're talking about hooks… **But here's the thing**: I'm not going to give you a list of 25 proven viral hooks." [src Kallaway] Flips the viewer's expectation of the format itself.
4. "There are three massive mistakes people are making with their mortgage. The average person pays an extra $122,000…" [src Kallaway] A list plus a concrete cost as the stake.
5. "If I had to grow from 0 to 100K subs on YouTube **again / in 5 months**, here's how I would do it." [src Kallaway] One word adds proof ("again"); one phrase adds time.
6. "These are called life-size floor plans. You can literally walk through your exact home design **before you build it**." [src Kallaway] A name plus a clear end state; "before you build it" is both the objective and the contrast.
7. "This is why you should always wash your bread before eating it." [src Kallaway, citing another creator] A strange claim in a plain frame, so the gap is impossible to ignore.
8. "I think if we were smarter, we would listen to 13-year-olds more." [src Kallaway] Inverts a status assumption.
9. "I made several thousand dollars in a few weeks" → "I made $97,000 in 13 days on Instagram." [src Kallaway] Specificity turns a claim into a picture.
10. "I photographed the Milky Way" → "Photographing the Milky Way in 10 minutes, 1 hour, and 24 hours." [src Galloway via Colin & Samir] A known escalation format makes the gap visible.
11. "$25K vs $25M." [src Galloway] A familiar tier format on an unexpected axis.
12. "Why Are 96,000,000 Black Balls on This Reservoir?" [src Galloway] A huge exact number plus an object you can picture plus "why".
13. "World's Fastest Drone vs F1 Car." [src Galloway] A matchup borrowed from another niche.
14. "Zero to a Million Followers in 30 Days." [src Kane] A clear expectation that the piece must deliver.
15. "WARNING! Safety Is Dangerous." [src Kane] A paradox: two familiar words collide.
16. "Here are the three ways I grew the most last year" → "These are the three best methods for business owners to grow faster on Threads." [src Creator OS] Vague becomes a named audience plus a named place.
17. "$402K/Mo Business Asked Me How To Hit $1M/Mo (Live Consult)." [src Nik Setting, founder-sources] The buyer's stage plus a real scene; the numbers pick who comes.
18. "I spent $540,000 on my personal brand. Here's what was worth it." [src Nik Setting] A confession-sized number plus a judgment to come.

## 4. How Vietnamese audiences respond (spoken style, address, particles, directness)

The evidence here is thinner, so each point carries its confidence.

- **Spoken > written.** High-view VN titles read like speech: "?!", "…" pauses, CAPS on one or two words ("NGHỈ VIỆC"), and picture-slang ("lặn 1000m", "mù về tiền"). [YT-VN, medium] Ours lean tidy ("Câu khách chưa dám hỏi").
- **Decision questions travel.** "Nên … ngay!?", "có được không?" and "có thật sự…?" name the choice the viewer is weighing. Questions with "nên", "cái nào" or "ai" get the most comments ("nhận được lượt comments trả lời cao nhất"). [src MISA eShop, 23 Mar 2026]
- **The buyer's self-label is the call-out.** "Mất gốc Tiếng Anh", "chủ spa", "dân sale" and "mẹ bỉm" beat a generic "bạn". [YT-VN]
- **Xưng hô sets the relationship before the content does.** Address terms come from kinship and carry age, closeness and attitude. [src Wikipedia overview; VN address-term research, low-medium]
  - "mình–bạn" reads as a peer, creator-native;
  - "anh/chị–em" reads as service or respect (sales, spa);
  - "tôi" reads as authority (older experts);
  - "Dạ anh chị," reads as customer service. That is why 6 of 7 Zalo openers felt flat ([repo] COMPARE.md).

  One address pair per coach, never switched mid-piece.
- **Particles soften the ask.** Final particles soften commands and keep rapport: "nhé/nha" are friendly, "ạ" is respectful. [src VNU thesis on imperatives; learner sources, medium] Put them in the **CTA**, not the hook. A hook with "nhé" loses its punch; a CTA without one can sound like an order.
- **Directness.** VN titles are blunter than ours ("Đừng…", "Không bao giờ…", "Phải làm gì khi…"). That is fine about a *habit*, never about a *person*.
- **Comment culture.** "Chấm", "xin info" and "ib" are native behaviours. Meta demotes comment requests "for purposes other than a specific call to action". [src Meta Transparency, accessed 10 Oct 2026] My reading: a comment ask that delivers a named thing is defensible; a bare "chấm nếu đồng ý" is not. That reading is mine, not Meta's.
- **Gap:** Facebook and TikTok VN posts were unreadable without a login or JS, so the VN set is YouTube titles only. Have the founder paste 20 first lines and captions into `§CM-LIKED`.

## 5. CTAs: principles and examples

### Principles

- **C1. One ask, and fewer asks than gives.** Hormozi: "Give until they ask." "Give in public, ask in private." The P.S. is "one of my favorite places to make asks". "Only ask them to do one thing per call to action." He cites ~3.5:1 give-to-ask on TV. [src] *The Game*, 19 Aug 2023.
- **C2. The CTA closes the loop the hook or story opened (a contextual lead magnet).** Gray:
  - the story's last line is an Invitation, and the CTA closes the loop it opened;
  - the last carousel slide is the contextual lead magnet plus one trigger word;
  - the mix is ≈60% lead magnets, 20% offers, 20% DM keywords.

  [src] founder-sources.md, Oct 2026. A real Gray close: "Enjoy this? ♻️ Repost it to your network and follow Matt Gray for more. Get the complete framework here." [src] LinkedIn snippet, accessed 10 Oct 2026.
- **C3. Comment keyword plus DM, at the right tier.** Nik Setting: comment-keyword posts (22k likes → 6.5k leads), "follow to learn more is dead", a small CTA at ~65% of a VSL. [src] founder-sources.md. Deliver first, then ask for an email, and tie the keyword to the offer. [src] elev8or/inro guides, accessed 10 Oct 2026.
- **C4. Sends drive reach.** Mosseri: "More important than watch time or like and comment counts is send rates… sends per reach correlate more… with overall reach than anything else." [src] Social Media Today, 19 May 2024. Sends weigh more for non-followers. [src] Social Samosa, 2025. ATTRACT pieces should ask for a **send** to a named person.
- **C5. A generic engagement ask is weak and demotable.** Meta demotes posts that "explicitly request engagement (votes, shares, comments, tags, likes…)" beyond a specific call to action. [src] Meta Transparency (accessed 10 Oct 2026; first announced Dec 2017). Templates still teach "What's your top LinkedIn post trick? Share below!" [src] contentin.io, updated 18 Sep 2026.
- **C6. Conversation CTAs are choices, not opinions.** Ask "A hay B?", "nên hay không?", "tin thứ mấy?", never "bạn nghĩ sao?" (MISA, §4).
- **C7. A micro-commitment ladder by tier** (synthesis of Goh's trust stages, Nik's funnel and Hormozi's give/ask):

  | Tier | Rungs |
  |---|---|
  | ATTRACT | send / save / follow *for a named next thing* / pick A-or-B |
  | TRUST | comment keyword for *this piece's* payoff / reply with your situation / watch the long one |
  | CONVERT | DM with your case / book / apply / buy, with real details |

- **C8. Placement.** On YouTube, put the gift at ~65% and in the pinned comment (Nik; our bank #13–15). Write the email ask in the P.S. (Hormozi). Spoken last line plus the caption once (our bank).

### CTA examples (29), tagged by tier

**ATTRACT**

2. "Send this to the friend who {situation}." [repo bank #1] A send names a person, which drives reach (C4).
3. "Part 2 tomorrow: the one line I'd add to your price page." [repo] An open loop carried to the next piece. Follow becomes a reason, not a plea.
4. "Gửi cho đứa bạn đang trực page mà sợ nhắn lại khách nhé." (viết minh hoạ) Names the role plus the fear; "nhé" softens.
5. "Lưu lại, lần tới khách nói 'để chị suy nghĩ' thì mở ra đọc câu thứ hai." (viết minh hoạ) Save is tied to a future moment plus a specific item.
6. "Bạn là kiểu A (báo giá liền) hay kiểu B (hỏi trước)? Ghi A hay B thôi." (viết minh hoạ) A one-word choice (C6); it doubles as research.
7. "Ai đang lương thấp mà vẫn sợ nghỉ, comment số năm bạn đã ở công ty." (viết minh hoạ) "Ai" plus a number answer; it segments the audience.
8. "Watch this next: {title}. It answers {the question they'll have now}." [repo bank #14] The next video named as the next loop.

**TRUST**
9. "Comment PRICE CHECK and I'll send the one-page price sheet from this video." [repo] A keyword that is a buyer phrase, and the gift is *this* piece's artifact.
10. "Want slides 3–7 on one page? Comment {KEYWORD}." [repo bank #9] The gift is literally the post compressed, so it can't be generic.
11. "Comment {KEYWORD}: I'll send the exact words I just read, so you can say them {when}." [repo bank #4] Closes the loop with the script itself.
12. "Comment 'ĐỂ CHỊ SUY NGHĨ', mình gửi 3 tin nhắn lại mình dùng, mỗi tin một lý do khác." (viết minh hoạ) The keyword *is* the buyer's line from the hook, so hook and CTA rhyme.
13. "Comment SEEN 2 NGÀY, mình gửi tin thứ ba mình gửi chị khách hôm qua (đã che tên)." (viết minh hoạ) A specific, filmable gift tied to the hook's moment.
14. "If you want me to look at yours, message me {KEYWORD}. I'll reply myself this week." [repo bank #24] A DM with a personal promise and a time box.
15. "Nhắn mình bảng giá hiện tại của bạn, mình chỉ ra một dòng nên đổi. Tuần này mình đọc hết." (viết minh hoạ) A micro-audit as the gift, with a real capacity statement.
16. "Hit reply and tell me the one {thing} you're stuck on. I read every reply." [repo bank #33] Email reply is a deliverability *and* research signal.
17. "Ở phút 7 mình làm mẫu tin nhắn này; file mẫu để đầu phần mô tả." (viết minh hoạ, YouTube ~65%) Mid-roll, contextual (C3, C8).
18. "I put the {N} questions on one page. Comment {KEYWORD} and I'll message it to you (by hand, so give me a day)." [repo bank #29] Honest about manual delivery, which builds trust.

**CONVERT**
19. "If {situation} is costing you {cost in their words}, the call link is on my profile: 20 minutes, and I'll tell you straight if I can help." [repo bank #12] The stakes are restated in the buyer's words; an honest exit.
20. "{offer}: {what}, {price}, starts {date}. For {who}; not for you if {not a fit}. Message me {KEYWORD}." [repo bank #25] Real details plus disqualification.
21. "P.S. When you're ready, 3 ways I can help: 1 {gift} · 2 {workshop}, {date} · 3 work with me: {offer}, {price}." [repo bank #35; Hormozi's P.S.] An ask placed where asks belong (C1).
22. "Applications for {offer} are open until {date}: 3 questions, {time} to answer: {link}." [repo bank #37] The effort of applying is stated up front.
23. "Thứ Năm mình sửa trực tiếp tin nhắn của 5 bạn trong nhóm. Muốn là một trong 5, comment TIN CỦA MÌNH." (viết minh hoạ) A real cap with its reason (live time). The keyword is a buyer phrase.
24. "Nếu tiệm bạn đang vắng mà bạn vẫn bận cả ngày, nhắn mình chữ VẮNG MÀ BẬN, mình hỏi 3 câu rồi nói thẳng có giúp được không." (viết minh hoạ) The hook's contrast becomes the DM keyword, with an honest qualifier.
25. "Lớp bắt đầu {ngày}, {giá}. Ai đã comment 'ĐỂ CHỊ SUY NGHĨ' tuần trước, mình nhắn riêng trước nhé." (viết minh hoạ) Remembers the earlier micro-commitment (C7).

**Generic → specific pairs (the core fix)**
26. "Comment KEYWORD mình gửi tài liệu" → "Comment GIÁ SAU, mình gửi 3 câu hỏi trước khi báo giá, đúng 3 câu trong video." (viết minh hoạ)
27. "Follow để xem thêm" → "Mai mình đăng tin nhắn thứ ba, cái tin làm chị khách trả lời 'may quá em nhắc'." (viết minh hoạ)
28. "Bạn nghĩ sao? Comment bên dưới" → "Bạn báo giá ở tin thứ mấy? Ghi số thôi." (viết minh hoạ)
29. "Link in bio" → "The checklist is in the first comment." [repo bank #23] Says what is there and where.

## 6. Anti-patterns

**Hooks**
- **H1–H3** = F1–F3 above. Fix: add the cost, withhold the rule, say the actual sentence or object.
- **H4. Visible template.** "N sai lầm…", "97% người…", "Đừng … trước khi xem video này", "Sự thật không ai nói", each with nothing particular in it. Fix: keep the shape and add a twist from the coach's world. [P8, P10]
- **H5. Three hooks, three ideas** (frame, text and line diverge), or one idea said three times. Fix: one idea, three channels, each adding one detail; run the sound-off test. [P6]
- **H6. No turn word** in a hook meant to stop: no "nhưng / mà / hóa ra / càng… càng / but / until". [P3]
- **H7. Customer-service openers** in chat ("Dạ anh chị,"). Fix: name + reason + the scene in the first 8 words. [repo]
- **H8. Bait that the piece can't pay.** "Không ai dạy bạn", "bí mật", a money flex as the whole hook. Already blocked; keep it so. [P5]
- **H9. Benefit buried behind a feature** (the "21 minerals" version). [P3]
- **H10. Labels as titles** ("Tips xây dựng thương hiệu cá nhân"). [YT-VN contrast set]

**CTAs**
- **X1. A season-wide default ask on every piece.** This is the structural cause in our kit: `cta.default` plus "one keyword per Season".
- **X2. A gift named after the coach's offer, not after this piece's payoff** ("tài liệu", "guide", "free training").
- **X3. Opinion asks** ("bạn nghĩ sao?"), which are invisible and, per Meta's guideline, demotable when bare.
- **X4. "Follow for more"**, which is dead (Nik).
- **X5. Two asks**, or an ask on every piece with no give-only pieces. Hormozi: give ≫ ask.
- **X6. A keyword unrelated to the hook** (PRICE CHECK under a story about follow-ups).
- **X7. VN CTA with no particle** (reads as an order) or "Dạ" service-speak (reads as a shop).

## 7. Proposed hook rubric: the "Dừng lướt" (stop) score

Score each hook 0–2 on seven dimensions, after the existing six checks pass (they stay as *correctness gates*).

| # | Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | **Stakes**: what the buyer loses or gains, now | none | implied | said in the buyer's terms (seen, a lost booking, 8 years, a price) | Kallaway P2; Robertson P11 |
| S2 | **Turn**: the expectation broken | none | soft ("thật ra") | a clear "but/mà/hóa ra/càng…càng" or a paradox | Kallaway P3; Kane "Safety is dangerous" |
| S3 | **Picture**: can you film it or see it | abstract | an object or number | a moment with an object and exact words | P4; repo "Mạnh" lines |
| S4 | **Gap**: can the viewer name the question they now have? Is the answer withheld? | answer given / no question | vague ("điều này") | one precise question, unanswered | Loewenstein P1 |
| S5 | **Speed**: subject clear in the first ~1 s / first 6 words | after 2+ lines | by line 2 | in the first words; ≤{{hook_max}} | TikTok P7; Kallaway "speed" |
| S6 | **Alignment**: text + frame + line add up; passes sound-off | contradict or repeat | loosely related | one idea, each adds a detail; sound-off still clear | Kallaway triple hook P6 |
| S7 | **Freshness**: not a stock frame, not in recent_hooks | stock frame, nothing particular | stock frame plus a particular twist | an own shape, or a borrowed format applied to an unexpected axis | Galloway P8; P10 |

**Bands:** **Mạnh** ≥10 *and* S1 ≥1 *and* S2 ≥1 *and* S3 = 2. **Được** 6–9, or ≥10 while failing a gate. **Yếu** ≤5.

**Two quick tests before scoring:**
1. **"Rồi sao?" / "So what?" test.** Say "rồi sao?" after the hook. If the answer (the stake) isn't already inside the hook, S1 = 0.
2. **Thumb test (sound off).** Show a stranger only the on-screen words and the first frame for 2 seconds. Can they say who it's for, what's at stake, and what's surprising? Fewer than 2 of 3 means rewrite.

**Lab change (minimal):**
- Keep "≥12 drafts in ≥6 shapes". At the pick, print only a hook scoring **Mạnh**.
- None reaches Mạnh: repair the top two on their lowest dimension (usually S1/S2). The score is never shown to the coach.
- "Another hook" returns three hooks that each lead with a different *dimension* (stakes-led, turn-led, picture-led), not just a different mechanism.

**Calibration:** re-grade the 118 hooks in `qa/runs/demo-sales-coach-v134/HOOKS.md` first; most "Được" lines should land at 6–9 with S1/S2 at 0–1. If not, re-weight before adopting.

## 8. Proposed CTA rule set

1. **The hook writes the CTA.** At the hook pick, the lab also writes the *payoff noun*: the concrete thing the last line delivers (3 tin nhắn, 1 câu, bảng 5 dòng). The CTA must name that noun, or the next loop. (Gray, C2.)
2. **The piece's gift by default; the Season gift only when it fits.**
   - The default gift is the piece's own payoff on one page: the script lines, the checklist of the steps shown, the before/after message. The lab writes it with the piece, so "the gift exists before posting" stays true.
   - The Season keyword and gift are used only on CONVERT pieces or when the topic matches.
   - This replaces "one keyword and one gift per Season" as the *default*. Keep ≤3 keywords live, per the existing rule.
3. **Keyword = the hook's buyer phrase.** When the hook quotes the buyer ("để chị suy nghĩ", "seen 2 ngày"), the comment word *is* that phrase (≥2 words, caps, the existing spelling rules), so hook and CTA rhyme.
4. **Rung by tier, rotated** (C7, Nik/Hormozi):
   - ATTRACT: send / save-for-a-moment / part-2 / A-or-B. A comment keyword appears on ≤1 in 3 ATTRACT pieces.
   - TRUST: the piece's gift via keyword, a reply with your situation, or the long video.
   - CONVERT: DM with your case / book / apply / offer, with real details.
   - No identical rung *and* wording two pieces running.
   - A month's mix stays roughly 6 gift / 2 DM / 2 offer (the existing bank), but at least 1 in 4 pieces ends with **no ask beyond a send/save** (give ≫ ask).
5. **Conversation CTAs are choices.** "A hay B?", "nên hay không?", "tin thứ mấy?", "ai đang…?"; never "bạn nghĩ sao?". (MISA C6; Meta C5.)
6. **Make the micro-commitment visible later.** CONVERT pieces may reference an earlier keyword ("ai đã comment … tuần trước"), only when that keyword really ran. (C7)
7. **Engagement-bait guard.** A comment ask must deliver a named thing or answer a choice question. A coach's own "chấm" stays word for word with the existing dated note (§CM-CTA-KIT 6).
8. **VN voice.**
   - Use the CTA in the coach's address pair (from Voice).
   - End with a softening particle (nhé/nha/ạ) unless the coach's voice never uses one.
   - One sentence, spoken.
   - "Dạ anh chị" only in a 1:1 chat reply, never as a post's CTA.
9. **Placement.** Short: the last spoken line plus caption once. YouTube: the gift at ~65% plus the pinned comment plus first in the description. Email: the ask in the P.S. Carousel: the last slide. (C3, C8; existing bank.)
10. **Measure per rung.** The Friday review logs keyword comments ÷ reach and sends ÷ reach by rung, and keeps the ask lines that drew hands (the existing ASK LIST).

**Code changes this implies:**
- In `strings/{en,vn}.toml`, `cta.default` becomes a *frame*, not a sentence: "Comment {KEYWORD}, mình gửi {payoff_noun} trong video này nhé."
- `modules/*/convert.md` kit-keyword item 1: "one keyword per Season" becomes the CONVERT default only.
- `hook-library` grow-pick gains the S1–S7 score.
