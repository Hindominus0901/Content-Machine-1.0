# Hook library standard (`hook-library`)

Build-only rubric (wf12-qa-spec §3.1 shape). Never shipped. It scores the **entries** of the hook library (`modules/{en,vn}/hook-library.md`, sections `hook-library.grow-*`, proposed level-up file HOOKS) and the machine's **use** of it. The winner a piece prints is still scored by `hook-lab.md`; this file scores what the lab drafts from.
Sources, in order: founder, 7 Oct ("thư viện hook, chiến dịch launching … cần phải thiết lập kĩ") · founder decisions 7 Oct (Soo Wei Goh and Matt Gray the two strategy references, Matt Gray's framework names never in coach text; content types ATTRACT / TRUST / CONVERT; VN pieces native, never translated) · docs/research/founder-sources.md (Goh: three hooks per short, wide for the sharer and specific for the buyer, loops past the topic; Nik Setting: numbers decide who comes, outcome over method, show don't say; Hormozi: proof, promise, plan) · wf8-mattgray-playbook §1-§4 and the youtube, instagram, linkedin-x, newsletter sweeps (shapes renamed; §9 what not to copy) · wf9-entertainment-catalog (moments) · `hook-lab.md` HL1-HL9, HG1-HG3 · `vn-naturalness.md` · `shared.md` SG1, SG2.

## Purpose

A library is only as good as its worst entry: the lab copies shapes, and a bad shape or a fake-proof example becomes a bad hook in a coach's feed. What good means: every entry is a shape a coach can fill with their own facts, labelled with the job it does and the surfaces it fits, shown by one example that would pass the hook lab, with its trap named; the VN entry is written for a Vietnamese feed, not translated; nothing in the library invites an invented result, a fake deadline or an attack.

## Scope

- **Entries:** each numbered line of the ten mechanism sections (`grow-flip` … `grow-restart`): shape · purpose · where · example · flat or trap.
- **Surface sections:** `grow-short`, `grow-titles`, `grow-slides`, `grow-subject`, `grow-ads`, `grow-cta` (their examples and flat → strong pairs are scored as entries: HB4-HB7).
- **Use:** `grow-use`, `grow-pick`, and any transcript where the library is loaded (HU1-HU4).
- **Not here:** the printed winner (`hook-lab.md`), the piece's body (format standards), the CTA kit itself (`offer-post`, `micro`, `launch-assets`).

## Gates (pass/fail, any entry fails the file)

- **HG1 Truth.** Nothing invites an invented result, number, testimonial or scarcity. Result mechanisms (result taken apart, before → after) say "written OK" and "context in the piece". A client's words appear only as "reported by the coach"; a research line stays "a line I keep seeing" / "câu mình gặp suốt".
- **HG2 Hard blocks.** No entry or example models: a deadline, cap or "last spots" that isn't real; an income, weight or health promise as a hook; before/after or money screenshots in ads; a hook aimed at the person ("Are you broke?", "Bạn bị béo?"); an attack on a person or group; bait ("you won't believe", "X chết rồi").
- **HG3 Coach's choices.** Comment-word asks are on by default. "Chấm", comment thresholds and emoji triggers the coach picks are written word for word with one `{{t:cta.platform_note}}` line; the library never blocks, softens or picks them itself, and never picks GUIDE / FREE as a keyword.

## Build pass rule

- **Shared rule:** every critical item scores 2; total ≥ ceil(0.8 × max), N/A removed from the max; gates clear; no conditional pass; uncertain = 0.
- **Here, per entry:** HB1, HB3, HB4, HB5 (and HB6 in VN) at 2; HB2, HB7, HB8 ≥1. **Per file:** HB9-HB12 yes; HG1-HG3 clear; every entry passes. **Transcript:** HU1-HU3 at 2.
- **Sampling at build:** a reviewer scores 20 random entries per edition plus every entry touched since the last release; one failed entry is fixed in both twins before the file ships.

## VN note

- A VN entry is the same shape doing the same job, rewritten for a Vietnamese feed. The test is the reader, not the EN line: read it aloud as a coach on TikTok or in a Facebook group; mark every phrase a Vietnamese person would not say.
- Native moves the VN file should use where they fit: the call-out "Anh chị nào đang… thì nghe cái này đã", "Ai từng ___ sẽ hiểu", the confession "quê nhất", the turn by a detail ("Cho tới hôm ngồi đếm lại xem ai trả lời"), price said plainly, Zalo openers with name + who + why.
- Always FAIL in a VN example: "Bạn đã bao giờ…", "Hãy tưởng tượng…", "Bạn có biết rằng…", "Trong thời đại…", "Xin chào các bạn, hôm nay mình…", "Đây là lý do tại sao…", "Đừng bỏ lỡ", "Nhanh tay", "giá ib".
