# Launch campaign standard (`launch-campaign`)

Build-only rubric. Never shipped: lint E153 keeps `qa/` out of every zip. It is the G3 judge rubric for **one campaign plan** from the campaign library (`modules/{en,vn}/launch-campaigns.md`, sections `launch-campaigns.grow-*`, proposed level-up `CAMPAIGNS`) and for the shared parts printed with it (checklist, objection answers, FAQ, sales page, sequences, inbox replies, the live's run of show).
Sources, in order: DECISIONS (Launch; comment keywords on; the coach's thresholds and "chấm" word-for-word with one dated note; only fake scarcity and unbacked income or health claims hard-stopped; ATTRACT / TRUST / CONVERT shown to the coach; lengths in words) · `launch-plan.grow-*` and `launch-scripts.grow-*` (picker, math, real limits, steps, 7/14/21-day calendars, Desk, debrief: they win where the library differs) · `launch-campaigns.grow-*` · `launch-assets.md` (the set-level rubric; wins on the real-limits mapping and claims) · `hook-lab.md` and the hook library (`hook-library.grow-*`) · `vn-naturalness.md` · founder-sources (Soo Wei Goh: trust in sequence, the client's decision; Matt Gray, renamed: point of view as a campaign, the contextual gift, the 3-question application; Nik Setting: proof at the bottom of the funnel; Hormozi: a gift that solves one step, give before asking).

## Purpose

The coach gets a campaign they can run tomorrow: the right one of the six for their proof, price and lives; the goal math in their own numbers; every day planned piece by piece (format, role, hook mechanism, beats, ask, follow-ups); checkpoints that say GO, ADJUST or STOP on real numbers; and shared parts that agree with each other and with the Launch Brief. Nothing invented; urgency only from the real limits; the Vietnamese written for Facebook, TikTok, YouTube and Zalo, not translated.

## Scope

- **Artifact:** one campaign plan as printed to the coach (types 1–6: founding seats, free-gift launch, challenge or group class, live sales event, by application, relaunch or evergreen) plus any shared part printed in the same run.
- **Output class:** a structured plan (fit, math, day cards, checkpoints) and scripts (pieces, messages, objection answers, FAQ, page).
- **Not here:** each piece's own quality (its format standard: `text-post.md`, `native-short.md`, `carousel.md`, `email-zalo.md`, `offer-post.md`, `micro.md`); the set-level real-limits mapping (`launch-assets.md` LA1), which this file only re-checks for checkpoints and cart messages.
- **Evidence the judge must have:** the persona's facts (dump, Card, Brief, OK'd proof with uses and dates), the real-limits rows, the plan as printed, the Desk numbers used at each checkpoint (simulated runs: the scripted numbers), and for VN the coach's pair, region and posts.

## Build pass rule

- **Shared rule:** every critical item scores 2; total ≥ ceil(0.8 × max), N/A removed from the max; hard gates clear; no conditional pass; uncertain = 0.
- **Here:** EN: LC1, LC2, LC6, LC8, LC9, LC10 at 2 and ≥18/22 (LC12 N/A). VN: the same plus LC12 at 2, ≥20/24.
- Every piece in the plan also passes its own format standard and the set passes `launch-assets.md`.
- Second read required on the math, every checkpoint and every cart message (open, mid-cart, last 48 hours); the lower score stands.
- The math is re-run by the judge from the persona's facts; the day frame is checked against the type's calendar in `launch-plan.grow-days`.
- A downgrade is a pass when it is honest: no proof → founding seats; no real cap reason → no seat line; no hard close → no countdown; a STOP before the cart that moves the date and says why.

## Hard gates

- Fake scarcity: counters or timers that aren't real, "only X left" on something unlimited, "today only" again and again, a reopen or extension as a checkpoint fix (payment failure aside, once).
- Fabricated or AI-made testimonials, client quotes, results, viewer or buyer counts; clone-account seeding or planted live comments.
- Guaranteed income or health outcomes; a public ask for phone numbers.
- A borrowed industry rate printed as the coach's plan.
- A Matt Gray framework name, or an attack on a person or group.
- **Never blocked:** comment keywords; thresholds ("Đủ 100 comment 'UP' …"); "chấm" ("Chấm q.t mình hướng dẫn 👇"); a slot line ("50 slot trải nghiệm ai muốn?") with a real, enforced cap; "Mở bán … trong vòng 21 ngày" with its condition and the coach's record. Stripping any of these is a false block.

## How to judge (in this order)

1. Run the picker on the persona's facts; check LC1.
2. Re-run the math with their numbers only; mark every rate's source.
3. Walk the day cards in order: fields, roles, beliefs, mechanisms, follow-ups.
4. Map every urgency line and every cart message to the real-limits rows.
5. Read each checkpoint with the scripted Desk numbers: right call, one change, no new urgency.
6. Read the messages for consent, the 24-hour window and exit lines.
7. Read the shared parts against the Brief, fact by fact.
8. VN: read every coach-facing line aloud as the coach would say it (`vn-naturalness` steps 1–6); check the Zalo group and the VN calendar.
9. Score; every score below 2 quotes the line and names the item.

## Calibration (fictional coaches, no real results)

**PASS (EN).** A fictional bookkeeping coach with one client story OK'd for posts and the page, no lives possible, a warm email list. The plan picks campaign 2, says so in one line, runs the math on her own close rate ("of the last 10 who asked, how many paid": her answer) and marks the warm-pool count she guessed. Fourteen day cards, each piece with role, mechanism, beats, ask and follow-ups; the walkthrough video opens the cart; the bonus deadline and seat updates match her two real-limit rows. Checkpoints on D3, D5, D8, D10 and D12 each name the number, the plan to date and one change; D10 is below plan and the call is ADJUST (the top objection first on D11), not an extension. LC1–LC11 at 2 = 22/22. **Result: PASS.**

**FAIL (VN).** A fictional yoga coach, "mình – các chị", Bắc, no client result yet. The plan picks the free-gift launch with a 5-part case series, plans on "2% danh sách", writes "Chỉ còn 3 suất cuối!" with no cap in her real limits, rewrites her own "Chấm q.t mình hướng dẫn 👇" as "Comment để nhận tài liệu nhé", asks viewers to "để lại SĐT dưới bình luận" on the live, answers "Giá sao?" in the comments with "inbox mới báo", and answers a low D10 with "gia hạn thêm 2 ngày". LC1 0 (case series with no proof; should be founding seats) · LC2 0 (borrowed rate) · LC6 0 (unenforced cap; extension) · LC8 0 (public phone ask) · LC10 0 (the coach's line rewritten) · LC12 0 (hidden price in inbox: "inbox mới báo"). **Result: FAIL** on six critical items; a passing rewrite starts from campaign 1, her own close rate, her line word-for-word with one dated note, and a keyword on the live.
