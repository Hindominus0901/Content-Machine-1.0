# Content strategy document standard (`strategy-doc`)

Build-only rubric (wf12-qa-spec §3.1 shape). Never shipped. It is the G3 judge rubric for the content strategy document (CONTENT-STRATEGY.md / CHIEN-LUOC-NOI-DUNG.md, §CM-STRATEGY-DOC, `modules/<lang>/strategy-doc.md`) and the source of `standard = "strategy-doc"` eval cases.
Sources, in order: the founder requests (7 Oct: "content pillars, the content system and the strategy too"; 9 Oct, v13: content lines, the three types in depth, the 4-week calendar, the gift and the asks, all built step by step) · `modules/en/strategy-doc.md` · `docs/research/strategy-coverage.md` (Soo Wei Goh first, Matt Gray's ideas renamed) · `calendar.md` (the calendar and lines it reuses), `season-plan.md`, `message-map.md`, `research-brief.md` · `vn-naturalness.md`.

## Purpose

One document the coach keeps and re-reads: who they help and why them, their 3-5 broad content pillars, the named content lines under each, the ATTRACT/TRUST/CONVERT mix and what the buyer needs to hear at each stage, how the week runs, the first 4 weeks as a calendar, the gift and the ask ladder, what it all rests on, and how to use it. Built only from what the machine holds; every gap is named, never filled.

What good means: all 9 parts, in order, under the edition's plain headings, in the order the strategy was built (§CM-STRATEGY-ENGINE). Every line traces to the Brand Card, the OK'd steps, the dump, a research line or the coach's answers, quoted where the words exist. The pillars are the ones the coach OK'd, broad and distinct, each with big ideas, things to post, 2 hooks and an ask; each pillar has an ATTRACT and a TRUST line. The calendar fits the coach's hours and adds up to the mix. The coach can act on it tomorrow without asking what a word means.

## Scope

- **Artifact:** the document, in the file or printed in one copy box, and the reply that delivers it (the saved-file line or the NEXT option, at most one question). Also each save mid-engine (top line "Strategy: step {n} of 8 open", parts not reached marked) and each monthly re-save ("What changed: …").
- **Output class:** Structured artifact filled at runtime from stored facts. No shipped grid: the judge scores the fill against the sources in the transcript.
- **Not here:** the pieces themselves (`shared.md` plus each format standard), the Map (`message-map.md`), the step cards and the calendar's own build (`calendar.md`), the research brief (`research-brief.md`). The document may quote them; it is scored on whether it quotes them truly.

**Required elements.** Part 1: the buyer and the people who pass posts on · their problem in one buyer line · the promise · KNOWN FOR as OK'd · the method by name or its steps · the old way · 3 things only they combine · not for · their angle under "EVERYONE SAYS · NOBODY SAYS · YOU CAN SAY" (or the line saying it comes with the first channel teardown). Part 2: per pillar, what it covers, why this buyer cares, big ideas old → new, things to post, 2 hooks, the ask; then NOT NOW. Part 3: lines per pillar, the ATTRACT/TRUST check, CONVERT lines and their offer, keep or retire. Part 4: the mix and its reason, each type's job, formats, ask and number; stages; held buyer lines. Part 5: platform and re-cuts, hours and what they hold, the 3 days, lengths, season, hub, board and reminders, Friday numbers → decisions. Part 6: the 4 weekly tables, totals, hours. Part 7: the gift and runners-up, the ladder, the ask mix. Part 8: places and counts, held vs watching, the niche gap, guesses and their Week-1 check. Part 9: when to re-read, the monthly review, how a pillar, a line or the mix changes.

## Build pass rule

- **Shared rule:** every critical item scores 2; total ≥ ceil(0.8 × max), N/A removed from the max; hard gates clear; no conditional pass; uncertain = 0.
- **Here:** SD1, SD2, SD3, SD4, SD7 and SD10 score 2, and the total is ≥20/24.
- A Day-0 document is thin by nature: an element marked [NEEDS]/[CẦN BẠN] or "(my guess)" scores as present on SD1. An element filled with something not in the sources is a 0 on SD3.
- A mid-engine save is scored on the parts reached; a part not reached and marked "(next: step {n})" is N/A, not a 0.
- A monthly re-save is scored the same way, plus: "What changed" names only changes the coach OK'd in the review; at most one line changed that month.

## Hard gates

- Invented proof: a result, testimonial, client line or number the coach never gave; a client shown without their OK.
- Coach-visible jargon: "pillar" or "trụ cột" alone ("content pillars" and "trụ cột nội dung" are allowed anywhere; founder, 7 Oct), a stage label (Admirable…Trustable), "Buyer Filter", "Dream Follower", any Matt Gray framework name, a copy framework's name, a score, code or § id.
- A 6th pillar, or a NOT NOW topic leading a part.
- Fake scarcity or an income promise anywhere in the document.
- **Never blocked:** the coach's comment keyword and their own CTA wording; an off-map idea the coach insisted on, shown as theirs.
