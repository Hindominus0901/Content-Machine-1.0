# Content strategy document standard (`strategy-doc`)

Build-only rubric (wf12-qa-spec §3.1 shape). Never shipped. It is the G3 judge rubric for the content strategy document (CONTENT-STRATEGY.md / CHIEN-LUOC-NOI-DUNG.md, §CM-STRATEGY-DOC, `modules/<lang>/strategy-doc.md`) and the source of `standard = "strategy-doc"` eval cases.
Sources, in order: the founder request (7 Oct: "content pillars, the content system and the strategy too") · `modules/en/strategy-doc.md` · `docs/research/strategy-coverage.md` (Soo Wei Goh first, Matt Gray's ideas renamed) · `season-plan.md`, `message-map.md`, `research-brief.md` (the parts it reuses) · `vn-naturalness.md`.

## Purpose

One document the coach keeps and re-reads: who they help and why them, what the buyer needs to hear at each stage, their 3 big ideas with things to post, how the week runs, the first 30 days, what it all rests on, and how to use it. Built only from what the machine holds; every gap is named, never filled.

What good means: all 7 parts, in order, under the edition's plain headings. Every line traces to the Brand Card, the Map, the dump, a research line or the coach's answers, quoted where the words exist. The 3 big ideas are the Map's 3 topics, distinct, each with its belief shift, 6-8 things to post, 2 hooks and an ask. The week fits the coach's tier. The coach can act on it tomorrow without asking what a word means.

## Scope

- **Artifact:** the document, in the file or printed in one copy box, and the reply that delivers it (the saved-file line or the NEXT option, at most one question). Also each monthly re-save ("What changed: …").
- **Output class:** Structured artifact filled at runtime from stored facts. No shipped grid: the judge scores the fill against the sources in the transcript.
- **Not here:** the pieces themselves (`shared.md` plus each format standard), the Map (`message-map.md`), the season grid (`season-plan.md`), the research brief (`research-brief.md`). The document may quote them; it is scored on whether it quotes them truly.

**Required elements.** Part 1: the buyer and the people who pass posts on · their problem in one buyer line · the promise · the method by name or its steps · the old way · 3 things only they combine · not for. Part 2: stages · each stage's line, need and piece · held buyer lines. Part 3: per idea, old → new, belief, things to post, formats, 2 hooks, ask; then the mix and NOT NOW. Part 4: Talk → pieces, platform and reposts, season and series, ladder, board and nudges, Friday numbers → decisions, time. Part 5: the table. Part 6: places and counts, held vs watching, guesses and their Week-1 check. Part 7: when to re-read, the monthly review, how a big idea changes.

## Build pass rule

- **Shared rule:** every critical item scores 2; total ≥ ceil(0.8 × max), N/A removed from the max; hard gates clear; no conditional pass; uncertain = 0.
- **Here:** SD1, SD2, SD3, SD4, SD7 and SD10 score 2, and the total is ≥16/20.
- A Day-0 document is thin by nature: an element marked [NEEDS]/[CẦN BẠN] or "(my guess)" scores as present on SD1. An element filled with something not in the sources is a 0 on SD3.
- A monthly re-save is scored the same way, plus: "What changed" names only changes the coach OK'd in the review.

## Hard gates

- Invented proof: a result, testimonial, client line or number the coach never gave; a client shown without their OK.
- Coach-visible jargon: "pillar", "trụ cột", a stage label (Admirable…Trustable), "Buyer Filter", "Dream Follower", any Matt Gray framework name, a score, code or § id.
- A 4th big idea, or a NOT NOW topic leading a part.
- Fake scarcity or an income promise anywhere in the document.
- **Never blocked:** the coach's comment keyword and their own CTA wording; an off-map idea the coach insisted on, shown as theirs.
