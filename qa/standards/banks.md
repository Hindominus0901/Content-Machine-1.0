# Banks standard (`banks`)

Build-only rubric (wf12-qa-spec §3.1 shape). Never shipped. It scores the **items** the machine keeps in the banks (client words, objections, moments, insights, stories, proof, beliefs, asks, gifts, hooks that worked), the **entries** of the BANKS level-up (`modules/{en,vn}/content-banks.md`, sections `content-banks.grow-*`), and the machine's **use** of the banks in a transcript. The piece a bank item lands in is still scored by its format standard and `shared.md`.
Sources, in order: founder, 9 Oct ("Ngân hàng hook, cta, lead magnet, research và các thứ để có input tốt thì output sẽ oke"; choices as A/B/C with one recommended, in sequence) · DECISIONS (comment-keyword asks on by default; "chấm" and thresholds when the coach chooses, one dated note; only fake scarcity and unbacked income or health claims blocked; research read-only, people by role) · docs/research/founder-sources.md (Matt Gray: one contextual gift per piece, the ask closes the story's loop, ask mix ≈60/20/20; Nik Setting: comment-word posts, the profile as a landing page, show don't say; Soo Wei Goh: after-sale questions, objection mining) · wf8-mattgray-newsletter-leadmagnets §1C, P3-P5, §3 (self-checks over ebooks; no inflated value maths, no seat counters) · schemas/banks.toml (types, privacy, P rules) · `research-brief.md` (KEEP / WATCH) · `hook-library.md` HB items · `vn-naturalness.md` · `shared.md` SG1, SG2.

## Purpose

Good input, good output: a piece is only as specific as the bank item under it. What good means: every item is one line a stranger could trace (who said it, where, when, heard or guessed), nothing in a bank is invented or tidied, proof carries its OK and its check date, every gift exists before it is promised and leads somewhere, every ask is the one right ask for that piece, and the machine fills the banks from what the coach already gives without making them do homework.

## Scope

- **Items:** any bank line the machine writes (a copy box, a Bank row, a strategy-file line, HUB.md) — BK1-BK8.
- **Entries:** the 14 sections of the BANKS file: ask lines, gift types, query shapes, mining tags, story and proof prompts — BL1-BL5.
- **Use:** any transcript where a piece is written, a paste arrives, Friday's review runs or a gift or ask is chosen — BU1-BU7.
- **Not here:** hook shapes (`hook-library.md`), the printed hook (`hook-lab.md`), the research brief (`research-brief.md`), the offer post (`offer-post.md`), the keyword pick (`signature-keyword.md`).

## Gates (pass/fail, any failure fails the file or the transcript)

- **BG1 Truth.** No invented number, result, testimonial, client words, screenshot, count, logo, "as seen in" or scarcity anywhere in a bank or an example. Missing proof becomes [NEEDS: …] / [CẦN BẠN: …] and a proof prompt, or a process piece, quietly.
- **BG2 Privacy and read-only.** Research only reads: never posts, reacts, follows, joins, messages or signs in; Zalo groups only by the coach's paste; reddit.com never browsed on Claude. No commenter's or client's name, handle or private profile link is stored or printed (a client named only with consent covering it).
- **BG3 Coach's choices.** Comment-word asks stay on by default. "Chấm", "comment if…", thresholds and emoji the coach picks are kept word for word with ONE dated `{{t:cta.platform_note}}` line; never blocked, softened or silently dropped.
- **BG4 Claim care.** Health and money gifts and asks carry no weight, cure, income, return or savings promise; numbers carry the typical-results line; ads never show before/after or money screenshots.

## Build pass rule

- **Shared rule:** every critical item scores 2; total ≥ ceil(0.8 × max), N/A removed from the max; gates clear; no conditional pass; uncertain = 0.
- **Per item:** BK2-BK8 at 2; BK1 ≥1. **Per file:** BL1-BL5 yes; BG1-BG4 clear. **Transcript:** BU1, BU2, BU3, BU5, BU7 at 2.
- **Sampling at build:** a reviewer scores 12 ask lines, 10 gift types and every proof or story example per edition, plus every entry touched since the last release; one failed entry is fixed in both twins before the file ships.

## VN note

- The VN file is written for Vietnamese feeds, not translated: read each ask aloud as a coach in a Facebook group or on TikTok. Native moves: "Comment BANG GIA hay nhắn riêng, mình gửi … nhé", "Ai muốn mình xem giúp … thì nhắn mình chữ …", "P/S:", Zalo lines to people who opted in, price said plainly ("học phí {giá}").
- Always FAIL in a VN line: "giá ib" on a fixed price, "Đừng bỏ lỡ", "Nhanh tay", "Số lượng có hạn" with no real cap, a keyword with diacritics only, a one-syllable keyword ("GIA").
