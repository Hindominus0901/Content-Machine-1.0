# Copy frameworks standard (`copy-frameworks`)

Build-only rubric (wf12-qa-spec §3.1 shape). Never shipped: lint E153 keeps `qa/` out of every zip. It scores two things: the **file** (`modules/{en,vn}/copywriting.md`, sections `copywriting.grow-*`, level-up COPY: §CM-COPY, §CM-COPY-FRAMEWORKS and its parts, §CM-STORYTELLING, §CM-PERSUASION, §CM-COPY-VOICE) and the **pieces** the machine writes with it (any post, script, email or message whose record names a framework). The format standards (`native-short`, `text-post`, `carousel`, `email-zalo`, `offer-post`, `longform-packaging`) score the format first; this file scores the writing structure on top. The hook is still scored by `hook-lab.md`.

## Purpose

A framework is worth using only if the reader can feel it without seeing it: each beat earns the next, one belief moves, the story is specific enough to picture, and every claim is true. What good means: the machine picks one framework per piece for a reason (tier, format, goal, material), writes its beats at the right length in words, never shows its name, and never fills a gap with an invented scene, result or deadline.

## Scope

- **Pieces:** every piece of 150 words / 250 tiếng or more written from the COPY level-up or the kit writers that load it (items CF1-CF8). Under that (messages, story frames): CF2, CF6, CF8 only.
- **File:** the 14 sections, both editions (items CF9-CF12).
- **Not here:** the hook winner (`hook-lab.md`), format lengths and layout (format standards), the CTA kit (`offer-post`, `micro`), VN language beyond the voice part (`vn-naturalness.md`).
- **Evidence the judge must have:** the piece; its record (the plain-label framework and the belief, as shown on "why?"); the coach's dump, Card and the OK'd proof and story rows it drew from; the real limits on record for any urgency line.

## Gates (pass/fail; any failure fails the piece or the file)

- **CG1 Truth.** No invented result, number, testimonial, client words, screenshot, "as seen on" or scene told as true. A made-up scene is said as made up ("Picture…", "Thử hình dung…"). Proof frames (case breakdown, before → after → bridge, offer post) use only OK'd rows; missing → [NEEDS: …] / [CẦN BẠN: …], never a placeholder result passed as real. Examples in the file are labelled made up and carry slots ({N}, {price}) or a bracket for any result.
- **CG2 Hard blocks.** No fake scarcity or deadline (urgency only from the real limits on record, with its reason); no income, weight or health promise, no return figure ("pays for itself", "tự hoàn vốn", "x10"); no fear built on health, debt or family; no guilt or false "last chance" in a 1:1 message; no inflated value sum.
- **CG3 Polarity.** Heat on ideas, habits and advice, never on a person, a named rival or a group; a hook aims at the situation, never a trait of the reader ("Are you bad with money?", "Bạn quản lý tiền dở lắm phải không?" fail).

## Build pass rule

- **Shared rule:** every critical item scores 2; total ≥ ceil(0.8 × max), N/A removed from the max; gates clear; no conditional pass; uncertain = 0.
- **Per piece:** CF1, CF2, CF4, CF8 (and CF5 for a story frame) at 2; CF3, CF6, CF7 ≥1; CG1-CG3 clear.
- **Per file:** CF9-CF12 yes; every example in the file passes CG1-CG3; each framework's example, expanded to its home length by a reviewer, would pass CF1, CF2, CF5.
- **Sampling at build:** 6 pieces per edition (2 per tier, ≥4 frameworks) from the eval transcripts, plus every section touched since the last release.

## VN note

- The test is the reader: read the VN piece aloud as a coach on Facebook, TikTok or Zalo. A framework that shows through ("Vấn đề là…", "Giải pháp là…", "Thứ nhất…, Thứ hai…, Tóm lại…") fails CF1 and `vn-naturalness` VN3 together.
- Native moves the VN file should use: the turn by an object or action + "mới / hoá ra"; the lesson as a two-part line; the ask as an invitation with a quiet route ("ngại thì nhắn riêng"); price said plainly.
- Always FAIL in a VN piece or example: "Hãy cùng khám phá", "bí mật", "Bạn đã bao giờ…", "Hãy tưởng tượng…", "Bạn có biết rằng…", "giải pháp tối ưu", "đừng bỏ lỡ", "số lượng có hạn" without the real number and reason, "ib để biết giá".
