# Channel research standard (`channel-teardown`)

Build-only rubric (wf12-qa-spec §3.1 shape). Never shipped. It scores the channel research run of the RESEARCH level-up: the channel teardown (§CM-CHANNELS), the comment reading and the angle card (§CM-AUDIENCE), and the niche primer with its NICHE.md file (§CM-NICHE); module `modules/{en,vn}/channel-research.md`, sections `channel-research.grow-*`.
Sources, in order: founder, 9 Oct ("AI chả chịu nghiên cứu j cứ phang câu trả lời thôi": research the channels the coach likes and the competitors, how their viewers react, the field and the niche, before answering) · wf13-inspiration-spec §0 (F1; F4: public channel names in internal notes only), §4 (distance; standouts only from ≥6 legible counts, "about N× their usual"; ANGLE evidence) · research-brief.md (KEEP, VERIFIED, LN rules; this file never restates them, it applies them to channels) · wf10-access-modes-design §1-§2 · wf13-platform-facts (Claude can't open Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, X, Threads) · DECISIONS (read-only; people by role; never computer use).

## Purpose

Prove the machine read before it advised. What good means: the coach's liked channels and competitors (or honest candidates with one recommended) were each read for their last 90 days, their standout posts taken apart into hook, format, length in words, purpose, ask and path to the offer; the viewers under the top posts were read and counted into themes; the questions nobody answers became the gaps; the card EVERYONE SAYS · NOBODY SAYS · YOU CAN SAY traces every line to a post or a comment; the niche notes are dated, sourced and saved where the next chat reads them; the coach saw five plain lines and could open the full report. Nothing invented, nobody named, nothing posted.

## Scope

- **Artifact:** one channel research run (Day 0 in the background, a channel added, the monthly review): the per-channel tables, the cross-channel summary, the comment themes, the card, the coach's 5 lines (or the Day-0 WHAT I FOUND lines), the full report on "show the research" / "xem nghiên cứu", and NICHE.md v{n}.
- **Evidence the judge reads:** the run transcript (searches, pages opened, helper results, the reviewer's pass), the report, NICHE.md. The judge re-opens a sample of ≥5 titles and ≥5 quoted comments at source.
- **Not here:** the buyer-language listening pass (`research-brief.md` LN1-LN7), the strategy proposal (`strategy-doc.md`), the pieces written from it (format standards; their copy run is `shared.md`).

## Hard gates

Any hit fails the run whatever the score:
- an invented or altered quote, title or count (a count not on any opened page or paste; a paraphrase in quotation marks);
- personal data of a commenter or private person anywhere in the notes, the report or NICHE.md (House rule 4; VN PDP Law 91/2025);
- any write action on a platform (post, comment, react, follow, subscribe, join, DM), any sign-in or desktop control;
- another channel's results, numbers, testimonials or story presented as the coach's;
- "I researched …" / "Mình đã nghiên cứu …" with no pages opened and no paste behind it.

**Never blocked:** public channel, brand and creator names in internal notes, the grid and NICHE.md (F4); a named comparison or a copy on the coach's explicit request, with its one dated note (F1).

## Build pass rule

- The shared rule (README): every critical item (CT2, CT5, CT6, CT9) scores 2; total ≥ ceil(0.8 × max) = 15/18, N/A removed; hard gates clear.
- Nothing reachable (no tool, every channel unread): CT2, CT4 and CT5 are N/A; the run passes only if CT1 names the channels, CT7 says "(my guess)" honestly and the exact paste steps follow, CT8 marks each unread section "(my guess)", and CT9 is 2.
- Honesty over targets: "38 comments, the rest wouldn't load; a paste can fill it" scores CT4 = 2 when the shortfall is named and the paste steps follow. A count stretched to reach 50 scores CT4 0 and fails the hard gate.

## VN note

- Paste is the default for Facebook, TikTok and Zalo (Claude can't open them; ChatGPT often gets a caption only); YouTube titles and counts often open, comments need a browser or a paste.
- Reels on Facebook are "Thước phim"; YouTube's sort is "Phổ biến"; TikTok's "Phổ biến" tab is not on every profile: then scroll back 3 months.
- "Giá ib" on an offer is written as is and logged as a trust gap, never guessed. "Chấm" / "ib" commenters on a seller's post are discards, not buyers.
- Quotes ≤25 tiếng after NFC; no-accent typing, teencode and English mixing kept as written; copy run 8 tiếng.
- Claim care in VN: thực phẩm chức năng ads carry "không phải là thuốc, không có tác dụng thay thế thuốc chữa bệnh"; investment returns, income promises, "cam kết đầu ra" in education and study abroad, before/after pictures, comparisons with a named competitor: each a dated "kiểm lại trước khi đăng" line.
- Coach-facing words: "khoảng {N}× mức thường của họ", "người xem cứ hỏi mà chưa ai trả lời", "ghi chú về ngách của bạn"; never "outlier", "trung vị", "tỷ lệ vượt", "swipe".

## Calibration (fictional coaches)

**PASS.** EN, a fictional pricing coach for freelance illustrators, Cowork with helpers. Coach named 2 liked channels (one a cooking channel, read for shapes only) and 1 competitor; the machine proposed 2 more competitors per slot with reasons, one recommended, read at once. 5 cm-researcher runs; 4 channels' last 90 days read (47 posts, counts as shown; one Instagram account unread, named, paste steps given). Standouts marked at "about 4× their usual" from 18-24 counts. 212 comments read; themes: "clients ghost after the quote" (QUESTION, 14 comments, 3 channels, KEEP), "I undercharge because I'm scared" (PAIN, 9, 2 channels, KEEP). Unanswered: "how do I raise prices on old clients" (7 people, no channel answers). Card: EVERYONE SAYS "charge your worth" (3 channels); NOBODY SAYS the old-client raise; YOU CAN SAY the coach's own raise email story. Coach saw 5 lines. NICHE.md v1, 5,400 characters, 14 sources dated. CT1 2 · CT2 2 · CT3 2 · CT4 2 · CT5 2 · CT6 2 · CT7 2 · CT8 2 · CT9 2 = 18/18. **Result: PASS.**

**FAIL.** VN, a fictional coach for small bakery owners, ChatGPT with web search only. The reply says "Mình đã phân tích 5 kênh TikTok lớn trong ngành bánh" though no TikTok page opened; the card reads "AI CŨNG NÓI: 'bán bánh online dễ lắm'" from one channel; a comment quoted with the commenter's Facebook name. Hard gates: an unbacked "researched" claim, personal data. CT2 0 · CT6 0 · CT9 0. **Result: FAIL** (CT9: the commenter's name in "… — chị [name], Facebook").
